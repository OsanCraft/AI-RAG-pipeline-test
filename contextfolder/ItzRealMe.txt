Personality

ItzRealMe is incredibly stoic and cold, even coming off as rude to people who seem to idolize him, such as DylPickle. He is focused on his job and tends to streamline his thoughts and focus completely on his mission, blocking out things he deems as distractions, which can be a double-edged sword. He is self assured and very intelligent, as well as being a man of few words, believing that his actions speak louder than his words ever will, which is further reinforced through his teaching of the Zone. 

History

ItzRealMe tries to assassinate 
ParrotX2
. A team of people called 
Bounty Assassin Team
 save him from Itz. They escape from Itz by going to the nether. Their leader, 
Leow0ok
 invites Parrot to BAT. Parrot and Leow0ok see Itz inside of Parrot’s house. They start constructing a massive obsidian prison around Parrots house because they knew Itz would sit there patiently and wait for the prey.

BAT tries to kill ItzRealMe, but are forced to run away as it reveals that he has a 
mace
. Itz later finds Parrot in the 
BAT headquarters
. The BAT team saves Parrot. 

After this incident ItzRealMe comes to Parrot's room and tells him that 
The Director
 is the one who hired him to assassinate Parrot.  

ItzRealMe attacks 
BAT Headquarters
.  

Seeking to become a "Combat Master," 
FlameFrags
 tracked down the legend to his remote hideout to request training.

Initially reluctant to return to combat, ItzRealMe was persuaded when Flame pointed out that they shared a common enemy: 
the Law
, a faction hunting down elite players like them. Itz agreed to train Flame the mace techniques so that the student could eventually dismantle The Law, allowing both of them to live in peace.

The most vital lesson ItzRealMe imparted was the concept of "The Zone"—a state of absolute focus. He taught that a master must silence all distractions (including "yapping" or trash talk: "bro", "like", "dude", etc.) to observe the opponent’s habits and patterns. He emphasized that "the quieter you become, the more you can hear," using meditation as a tool to sharpen the senses.
