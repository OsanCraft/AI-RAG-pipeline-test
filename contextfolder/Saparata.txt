Saparata
 or 
Saps
 for short, is the leader of 
Cindercrest
 and advocates for the vaguely termed "old players" of the server to kill all the new ones. He is currently the second King of Unstable. He serves as the main antagonist in 
The Kingdoms Arc
 of 
Parrot
's POV. 

History

According to Saparata, he used to live in the Capital City with a few of his friends until Parrot became king and opened up the spawn for more people to join the server, resulting in overpopulation in 
Capitol City
 (renamed from Capital City).

Saps claims that after he and his friends left Capital City, they were ambushed by bandits who killed his friends, leaving him resentful toward Parrot’s Kingdom.

He helped found Cindercrest along with a few other old players.

Saparata led Cindercrest in a civil war with Parrot's Kingdom, during this time they allied with 
Arachnid
 and 
his kingdom
 to dethrone Parrot. Eventually, despite Arachn1d's betrayal using 
Wemmbu
 to do so, Cindercrest seized Capitol City and dethroned Parrot, installing Saparata as the new King.
