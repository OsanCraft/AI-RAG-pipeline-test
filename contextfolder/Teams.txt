Cindercrest

Cindercrest is a group of players led by 
Saparata
. It focuses on killing all new players as the old players are 'starving' thanks to them. It has succeeded in its goal, making Saparata the second King of the Unstable SMP.

Fantst's Team

Fantst's Team were a group of players owned by 
Fantst.

The first appeared in How 
How I DESTROYED a Hardcore Minecraft SMP.
 They were responsible for destroying 
Ice Mountain Civilization
. They later tried to flood the spawn with Ender Dragons. They used 
Wemmbu
 to weaken 
MinuteTech
, so they could enter the End. They sent 
GamerCat021
 and two other members to kill them, which resulted in everyone dying except for GamerCat. They fought them again at the End City. They fought them for a third time in the main End island, which resulted in multiple casualties, including GamerCat. Everyone eventually retreats except for 
Baablu
 and 
SuperstionalRealism
, the latter of which is killed by Wemmbu. Baablu is spared.

Season 2
, from E2S2 all the way to the Law Finale.
