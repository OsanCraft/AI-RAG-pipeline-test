The Law's Elite Force
 is a division of 
The Law
 consisting of higher-up elites armed with netherite gear. The Law's Elite Force was created by 
LettuceK
 as part of his Law and had 5 commanders as its members, 
Deputy_Ace
, 
SargeLaw
, 
SargeRaxby
, 
the_Oroboros
 and 
TheG0DofWar
. As they are very skilled in combat, The Law's Elite Force's tasks were to reinforce forces against high-profile targets especially players such as 
Wemmbu
 and 
FlameFrags
.

The Law's Elite Force was in the 2v1000 battle against 
FlameFrags
 and 
Wemmbu
, none of them survived. Then they appeared again when fighting 
Jaden's Pirates
 and 
Wemmbu
, the latest appearance of them was in the episode: 
I Got Trained by A Minecraft Spear Master
, they were meant to kill 
ManePear
 and 
FlameFrags
, with no doubt, they lost and no one survived except 
SargeLaw
. As Netherite is rare on the Unstable SMP, the only person we know for sure in The Law's Elite Guard are probably the commanders themselves.

In the 1v1000 it is shown that many of The Law's Elite Force had maces, which points to the possibility of them being former Mafia players. These players also probably believe in The Law and are unaware of LettuceK's manipulation that allowed him to get total control. Members like SargeLaw think that they are hunting down a evil person and in FlameFrags's latest video he does not kill FlameFrags despite him attempting escape multiple times. This shows that at least most members actually believing in The Law's Ideals. 

Trivia

SargeLaw is the only alive LAW elite member left.
