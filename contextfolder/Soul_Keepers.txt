The 
Soul Keepers
 are a group of players who reside in the 
Underworld
. They are notable for being able to place and break blocks in the Underworld which other players cannot, and also portals to any coordinates in the 
Far Lands
. They are mysterious and not much is known about them, however they might have access to unknown exploits. 

They also capture players who happens to be trapped in the Underworld and imprisons them, while patching up holes of the Nether bedrock preventing escape.

Morasha

Wemmbu
. They capture 
Boosfer

All of the remaining members except for KiaranTheHold0 attack and capture Wemmbu. Wemmbu then escapes with the help of Boosfer and kills Auvmor. They fight below the nether roof.
