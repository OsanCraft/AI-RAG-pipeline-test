The 
Ice Mountain Civilization
 is a small civilization in 
Unstable Universe
. It was created by 
Loppezz
, 
Arcn
 and 
LettuceK
 while they were part of the 
Skyblock Civilization
, and was later expanded to the 
Ice Mountain Civilization
.

The 
Ice Mountation Civilization
, or the 
Cold Civilization,
 is a small empire built inside the jagged ice Mountation peaks that were built and terraformed by 
SpokeIsHere
 as part of 
ParrotX2
 and 
Wifies
 Scavenger Hunt. It was destroyed by Fantst.  

History

SpokeIsHere pays someone to build an ice mountain for a scavenger hunt designed for Parrot and Wifies, likely Squiddo.

After the dissolution of 
Skyblock Civilization
, 
loppezz
, 
Arcn
, and 
LettuceK
 flee to an isolated ice mountain, where they create a small nation. Wemmbu stumbles into the Snow Civilization, where he is promptly attacked and kicked out by loppezz, Arcn, and LettuceK, who are still upset at 
Wemmbu
 for leaving them with 
The Invisible Mafia
 in the Skyblock Civilization. Shortly after, Wemmbu returns with 
eggchan
, who helps them come to a peaceful resolution and the pair become temporary citizens of the Civilization. Unbeknownst to loppezz and the others, Wemmbu had an alternate agenda to use the Snow Civilization to lure a diamond trim Mafia member to Wemmbu so he can complete his training for Mane. This upset Eggchan, who felt guilty for lying, and he convinces Wemmbu to protect the Snow Civilization from the Mafia members.   

The civilization is taken over and later destroyed by 
Fantst
 despite Wemmbu and loppezz trying to stop him.
