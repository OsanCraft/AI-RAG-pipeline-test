Ender Pearl Cannon
 is a mechanism to teleport player or entities to a specific location on the server. It is widely used on the 
Unstable SMP
.

Mechanism

Ender Pearl Cannon use TNT duper to provide momentum for aligned ender pearl and offsets velocity to determine target location. The pearl loads the chunks during travelling.
