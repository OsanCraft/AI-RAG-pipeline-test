Kierrrr
, also known as 
Kier
, is a supporting character featured in 
Unstable Universe
. He and his friend 
Its_Dev_
 work for 
Boosfer
 as the 
Treasure Hunters
.

History

Boosfer sends Kierrrr and 
Its_Dev
 to steal specific items from 
ParrotX2
 and 
Wifies
. Kierrrr and Its_Dev_ try to intercept Parrot and Wifies during a quest, but they escape using invisibility.

Kierrrr and Its_Dev_ find Parrot and Wifies again. Parrot gives them a book, but edits the book to get rid of some important clues. They convince Kierrrr and Its_Dev_ that the hot air balloons around the area are in the shape of an arrow. Kierrrr and Its_Dev_ leave.

Parrot and Wifies go to Boosfer's, who is in charge of Kierrrr and Its_Dev_, base. parrot and wifies try to convince him not to steal these items, but he doesn’t care. They leave.

Kierrrr and Its_Dev_ steal all of the items that Parrot and Wifies obtained from the quest.

Boosfer pays Wemmbu to destroy "the valley", a section of the quest. Wemmbu finds Parrot and Wifies and agrees to team with them to steal items from Boosfer, Kierrrr, and Its_Dev_. They destroy the top layer of their headquarters with an 
Orbital Strike Cannon
. Wemmbu finds their secret vault of items and together, they steal thousands of netherite and diamond blocks from them.

Parrot and Wifies go to the end as part of their quest. They find Wifies prized dragon head. Kierrrr and Its_Dev_ try to kill them, but they escape to the outer end islands. Parrot and Wifies find an ender chest and use the items in there to try to kill Kierrrr and Its_Dev_. They manage to escape.

Its_Dev_ and Kierrrr vote against Boosfer in 
The End Civilization
.
