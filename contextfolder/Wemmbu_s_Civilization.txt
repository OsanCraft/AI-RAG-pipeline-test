Wemmbu’s Civilization
, or 
The Grand Empire
, was a Minecraft civilization established by 
Wemmbu
 within 
Unstable Universe
. The civilization was marked by large-scale constructions, a well-structured society, and strategic planning, though it did not dominate the server. 

History

PrinceZam
 tells Wemmbu to do him a favor. He wants Wemmbu to make a civilization to guard a vault. PrinceZam tells him not to go inside. Wemmbu gets his friend 
Eggchan
 to help him build his empire.

Wemmbu sends Eggchan to distract FlameFrags. Wemmbu uses TNT minecarts to try to kill FlameFrags. It pops his totem and they mine down to try and escape. Eggchan accidentally breaks all of Wemmbu’s armor.

C4CorvetteOwner

Horace_Altman

ClownPierce

ClownPierce comes back and hears Wemmbu talking with Parrot. He starts killing villagers in an attempt and get information, but Wemmbu tells him he has no information. Parrot escapes.

Wemmbu and eggchan try to figure out how they could kill FlameFrags. While planning, FlameFrags comes over and tries to kill them. PrinceZam destroys all of his civilization and tells him to pretend like none of this ever happened. He says that his original plan was to chunk ban Wemmbu, but he changes his mind and says that Wemmbu is too weak to even be a threat anymore.

Wemmbu goes to his orbital cannon to try and destroy the 
Zam Empire

They decide to try and break into the empire and kill PrinceZam. They are unable to kill PrinceZam.

They escape the cell but they had barred the exit with iron bars and wemmbu needed his pickaxe. He finds it in the cake chest and mines the bars. He and Egg escape with their lives.

He flies around with the guards chasing him but he had 4 stacks of rockets and most of the guards give up.
