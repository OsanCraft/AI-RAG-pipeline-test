Plot

FlameFrags
 was exploring the server until his wolf teleports to him and began growling, which only happens when a wolf gets attacked. FlameFrags decides to check on his base and he finds out his base was griefed by a toxic team. The team of toxic players had blown his entire base up and killed all his wolves and the wolf that teleported to him was the only survivor. 

FlameFrags heads out to set a temporary camp until he gets attacked by a band of players from the toxic team. They use pots to steal FlameFrags's weapon but FlameFrags proves that he can win a fight against these players with his fists. The toxic players loot FlameFrags's camp and destroy it and he has an argument with the remaining players. They reveal that their leader is 
Razor2gud
. FlameFrags's wolf kills one of the remaining players and one of them drops a lodestone compass. 

FlameFrags goes to where the lodestone compass leads to and finds Razor2gud. FlameFrags tries to confront Razor2gud but he taunts him and runs away. FlameFrags tries to have a fight with Razor2gud but he says that if he wants to fight him, he has to downgrade his gear. After switching to iron armour and weapons, Razor2gudbrings his army to try to kill FlameFrags. The fight goes well, they start using strength potions but FlameFrags is still able to kill all of them. FlameFrags finds a map in one of the houses and realizes that the base he raided wasn't the only base the players own. If he wanted to find all of their bases and destroy them as well as getting revenge on Razor, he had to find the bases. 

FlameFrags sets up another temporary camp inside a cave where he gives his dog new armour and a name, Ashen. FlameFrags uses his map to find a bunker since he knew that they could have TNT after what happened to his base. On his way to the bunker, FlameFrags finds another group of members from the same team. He goes into the bunker and is met with more people trying to defend it. FlameFrags defeats a wave of players there. He begins looting the base until another wave of players jump him.  He uses the fireworks and the crossboy he looted to kill them all. FlameFrags then blows up the entire bunker.

FlameFrags heads to another town and he kills the players living there in case one of them drops a lodestone compass. While he was looting a potion stash, he finds a bunch of strength potions and protects his dog until he gets confronted by Razor2gud and his team. FlameFrags crafts a bunch of TNT minecarts and he also enchants his iron leggings with blast protection to take less damage as well as gathering a few turtle master potions in case he's about to die. FlameFrags kills the army with the minecarts. FlameFrags has a fight with Razor2gud but he runs away again after his armour breaks. FlameFrags destroys the entire town until he finds another lodestone compass. 

FlameFrags arrives to other towns owned by the city and kills everyone there as well as blow up the houses. FlameFrags arrives at Razor2gud's base and he's not playing around anymore. As soon as he confronts them they continue to complai about FlameFrags having netherite armour while they have diamond armour. FlameFrags refuses at first but he downgrades his armour before having a fair fight in the colosseum. FlameFrags begins fighting Razor2gud and it goes well until Razor2gud activates a trap of TNT which was under the colosseum the entire time. As the team leave the colosseum, the TNT explodes and would've killed FlameFrags but he survived using turtle master. FlameFrags begins killing the remaining members of the team and blows up some houses in front of Razor2gud. Razor2gud begs for FlameFrags not to kill him as there are worse people out there but FlameFrags tells him that he will kill them all before killing Razor2gud.
