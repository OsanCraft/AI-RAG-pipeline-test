PrinceTetra
 is the current leader of the 
Merchant City Vanguard
, and possibly of the whole of 
Merchant City
 after 
DanchoGold
 was overthrown.

History

PrinceTetra is seen protecting the gates of his home. He lets 
ParrotX2
 enter Merchant City with his bodyguards 
TheobaldTheBird
 and 
ReinaDrop
. 

He is asked to leave after Parrot captured DanchoGold, the leader of Merchant City. He thinks about this for a moment, but after a vanguard member points out that if Dancho is killed they won't have to work for him, PrinceTetra and the rest of the vanguard force him to flee on horse.

PrinceTetra joins Parrot's army to stop 
Cindercrest
.

In 
Unstable SMP: Civil War
, PrinceTetra considers blowing Parrot up after 
Fort Feather
 is conquered, but is convinced not to by Parrot's speech.

He is seen at the leaders meeting in both Civil War and 
Civil War: Finale
.

Personality

He is known to be sceptical, especially of Parrot. He often doubts his choices and was originally against joining the army until he overthrew DanchoGold.
