History

Invisible Guy

Wemmbu

Law

He gave Eggchan a shulker full of maxed gear with trims and xp for Wemmbu to use.

Trivia

Despite being called Old Man Linn, Linn is actually 22 years old and is just called old by children who see him as ancient, as most of the UU players canonically (and fanonically) are all but children.
