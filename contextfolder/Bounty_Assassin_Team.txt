Practices

Members have separate rooms, and they can go to meeting room to convene a meeting.

Some members run a mini-team inside BAT.

Spies work in a group of 3, and must neutralize targets before reporting back to BAT.

Spies have access to books that contain investigation informations and are required to sorting them.

Spies should always be invisible, and Leo and Jumper would ask them to write their name on a sign to verify their identity.

When applicants is more than positions, they are given tasks to accomplish, and whoever accomplish first gets the positions.

History

ItzRealMe
 tries to assassinate 
ParrotX2
. A team of people save him from ItzRealMe. They escape to the nether. They invite Parrot to Bounty Assassin Team. In the meeting room there was a blackboard with a list of players captioned The Director List, indicating they have already started to investigate 
the Director
, likely overhearing 
Ashswagg
's talk in the 
Far Lands
. Parrot and 
Leow0ok
 see ItzRealMe inside of Parrot’s house. They start constructing a prison around ItzRealMe.

Parrot is given his own room in the 
BAT Headquarters
. BAT tries to kill ItzRealMe, but are forced to run away as he has a mace. ItzRealMe later finds Parrot in the BAT Headquarters. BAT saves Parrot. ItzRealMe tells Parrot that 
The Director
 is the one who hired him to assassinate him. 

BAT tries to kill 
FlameFrags
 but fail and run away. 

MrCube6
 tries to assassinate Parrot by trapping him under the void. 
Derapchu
 finds Parrot and saves him. Parrot sees an invisible player sitting in one of the chairs.

Derapchu is trapped by MrCube6 on a pressure plate. Parrot and the team have to figure out which redstone lines to activate to save him and the entire base from exploding within a certain amount of time. MrCube6 also says that he sent a TNT bomber plane towards their base. They are able to save Derapchu.

Parrot sets up a trap and catches MrCube6. They trap him in a prison. MrCube6 manages to escape. MrCube6 steals all of the gear from the headquarters. He gives these items to 
4CVIT
. Parrot and Derapchu take the shulker boxes of items back. A team of bandits try to steal from Parrot and Derapchu, but fail.

BAT finds MrCube6 and detains him. They interrogate MrCube6. They give MrCube6 a dandelion. MrCube6 escapes with a stasis chamber, but not before he gives Parrot a book. The book states something about a dandelion, implying that The Director somehow knew that MrCube6 got a dandelion.

Parrot finds a chest in his room with a sign saying to run. Inside the chest is two stacks of ender pearls and an invisibility potion. The BAT headquarters go into lockdown mode, so Parrot uses an invisibility potion and hides. The BAT tries to find Parrot to presumably kill him. Parrot flies away to make his own base.

BAT finds Parrot and claim that he’s The Director. Parrot runs to the nether. They find Parrot on the nether roof. Parrot manages to escape.

JumperWho
 finds Parrot in an abandoned city. JumperWho tells him that she was the one who put the chest and the sign in his room. Parrot becomes a JumperWho spy to figure out how he had been framed for being The Director.

JumperWho gives Parrot the pieces of evidence that BAT thought showed he was The Director. Leow0ok does a "mole check" to see if any of JumperWho’s spies were moles by having them hold a green shield. Parrot takes a green banner off of the wall and combines it with his shield. Parrot is the only spy who holds a green shield, showing that he is a mole. Parrot manages to escape.

Parrot finds a book asking Parrot to meet up with him. Parrot spies on BAT’s meeting. JumperWho betrays him. Parrot manages to escape during the chaos. JumperWho tells Parrot that he was actually just pretending to betray Parrot because BAT didn’t trust her. Parrot discovers that someone was spying on them. JumperWho uses a goat horn to call back her spy. They kill the spy.

BAT watches Parrot and The Director have a conversation, which convinces BAT that Parrot is not The Director. The Director teleports away. Leow0ok discovers that JumperWho killed a spy, and kick her off the team.

A player named 
TruOriginal
 tries to kill Nufuli, Parrot, and Leow0ok using hacks. They are eventually able to escape. The BAT headquarters is destroyed by TruOriginal who uses a hack to use other people's usernames and skins. TruOriginal tries to kill them. Leow0ok stays behind so BAT can escape. Leow0ok eventually escapes and finds BAT.

TruOriginal tries to kill them but eventually runs away. BAT make a new base that Parrot and Nufuli guard. TruOriginal tries to kill them again. He changes time to try and kill them. BAT lead TruOriginal into a trap to try and kill him, but he teleports Parrot into the trap, almost killing him. He is luckily able to escape. They pretend that they have a second trap and scare TruOriginal away.

TruOriginal finds Parrot and 
Nufuli
. Unbeknownst to him, Parrot and Nufuli were above a secret underground base, where Derapchu and Leow0ok were AFK sleeping in real life. TruOriginal deletes all of Parrot's items. TruOriginal teleports Parrot thousands of blocks into the sky. Nufuli saves Parrot from dying.

TruOriginal kidnaps Derapchu and Leow0ok. He pretends to be Leow0ok using a hack and tries to kill Parrot and Nufuli. Using a trap, they are able to kill TruOriginal.

Nufuli leaves the team, and as he is leaving, the Director kills him. They see the Director and try to kill him. The Director uses a pearl cannon to escape. BAT use the pearl cannon to try and go after the Director. They find themselves in an obsidian room, where the Director tries to kill them using an army of zombies with netherite armor and weapons. They are able to escape.

BAT went over to 
4CVIT
's redstone shop to see if The Director is there. They paid 4C a shulker of netherite armors they collected from aforementioned zombies, and 4C told them that a player matching all the described characteristics always leave through one direction. They set an ambush in that direction and eventually found a player wearing the same armor as The Director. They forced him to drink milk, revealing he was MrCube. The real Director appeared, saved and criticized MrCube. The Director gave Parrot an ultimatum to find his identity and set off a TNT trap, but BAT managed to fly up in time.

JumperWho rejoins BAT after saving Parrot. She leads them to a secret base that she created. The Director gives BAT a message, telling Parrot to meet up with him in one week. SpokeIsHere finds the secret base BAT live in and secretly pranks them and steals from them, and pretends he's the Director. Spoke is eventually caught and is forced to escape.

BAT spies on the Director and Parrot having their conversation. JumperWho's spies spy on a list of suspects for people who could potentially be the Director. They see all of them online while the Director and Parrot have their conversation. Parrot sees the Director hold a chest plate with a red armor trim, causing him to believe the Director is Spoke, as Spoke wears that chest plate. Parrot convinces Leow0ok and Derapchu to pretend to be spies so that Parrot can threaten to kill the supposed spies unless JumperWho confesses. This plan fails as Leow0ok and Derapchu stop pretending to be spies after JumperWho doesn't confess.

While JumperWho and Derapchu are out of the base, Leow0ok tries to kill Parrot. He eventually decides to spare Parrot's life. Parrot tells Leow0ok to leave and he obliges.

JumperWho and Derapchu find Parrot at Wifies' base, and Parrot tells them about Leow0ok trying to kill him. The Director finds them, and so they try to kill them. The Director traps JumperWho and Derapchu in an obsidian room over the void.

Parrot and Leow0ok make a plan to save JumperWho and Derapchu using a happy ghast. Leow0ok saves them using a happy ghast while Parrot distracts the Director.

Season 2
