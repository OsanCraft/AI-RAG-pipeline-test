History

District 13 is founded by some former members of the Farlands Civilisation, including Parrot, Wifies and more. After the main headquarters were created, Horace decided to leave and join the Mafia.  

Some district 13 members go looking for new people to add to the team, 
Parrot

Mapicc

By the time of the Unstable SMP election, the former resistance base had been turned into a town by a small group of players. Parrot, Theo, Horace, and Fymada would arrive at the base seeking to use it as a staging ground for a rebellion against LettuceK and The Law, where they would ally with it's new inhabitants. Horace would restore the base to it's former glory, and with the arrival of reinforcements in the form of Spoke, Mapicc, Wemmbu, FlameFrags, PrinceZam, JumperWho, JadenMAN, MinuteTech, and other recruits; the District 13 resistance group was officially reformed and set out to defeat The Law in a final confrontation at the Northern Council. At the end of the Battle of the Northern Council, District 13 would decide the fate of LettuceK allowing him to live however imprisoning him for life.

The District 13 base was presumably abandoned following the Battle of the Northern Council, as it appeared that none of the players who had turned the base into a town had survived the battle.
