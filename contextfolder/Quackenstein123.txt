History

They find a secret stash of loot, including the diamond tier Mafia player's armor and a mace, proving that Quackenstein123 is the Mafia player. Quackenstein123 comes into the vault. Spoke and Mapicc threaten to tell BeckyTronVIII about this. Quackenstein123 gives them a book which allegedly contains the coordinates of where every stasis chamber is stored.
