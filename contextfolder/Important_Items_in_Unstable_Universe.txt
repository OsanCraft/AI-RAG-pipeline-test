Parrot's Spyglass

Main article: 
Parrot's Spyglass

 
 
 
 
 

Parrot's Spyglass
 is a recurring item in Unstable. It was originally given to 
Wifies
 by 
Parrot
 in 
I Uncovered Minecraft's Greatest Secret
, mentioning that it has "sentimental value" to him. Eventually, 
Wifies
 gave it back to Parrot before chunkbanning himself. It appeared in most of Parrot's videos up until the finale of 
The Director Arc
. In 
Escaping Paragon: The Perfect Minecraft Prison
, Parrot was forced to drop the spyglass by Wifies onto the platform below, which he picked up before it despawned. Later on, Wifies forced Parrot to stand with him on pressure plates which would detonate hundreds of TNTs and blow them up if one of them stepped off. However, Parrot kept the spyglass and dropped it on the pressure plate, allowing him to walk off of the trap. Wifies stepped off his pressure plate to run towards the spyglass so he can pick it up one last time, setting off the TNT and killing him as well as destroying the spyglass in the process. Parrot still has a spyglass, albeit being a different one.

deanthebean9's Goat Horn

Main article: 
Deanthebean9's Goat Horn

 
 
 
 
 

Dean’s Goat Horn was a goat horn (Yearn) given to 
deanthebean9
 in 
The Search for Minecraft's Far Lands
 by 
Wifies
 after 
Parrot
 told him to do so. The horn was meant to be used if 
Dean
 was ever in danger, since Parrot assumed that Dean was not smart enough to use messages. Later, Dean admits that he was leaking their coordinates to 
The Mafia
, a faction that have been hunting them down, hence how they kept finding them every time, which led to Dean leaving the team after blowing the horn. Parrot later finds him in 
Unstable SMP: The First War
 in the jungle where they had their last argument, but refused to let him rejoin because of Dean's earlier betrayal. Wanting to help, Dean secretly rejoins the Mafia to help Parrot by sabotaging his mafia teammates during the war. Parrot, noticing this, tries to kill him, thinking he's a free kill, not knowing it was Dean because of invisibility. They both knock each other off a really high bomber plane, causing both of them to pop their totems when they land, which clears Dean's invisibility. Dean blew the horn one last time before the Mafia pulled his stasis, teleporting him into the void — the punishment for losing invisibility — and ultimately dies.

George Junior (Fish bucket)

Main article: 
George Junior (fish bucket)

 
 
 
 
 
 
George Junior
 is a salmon fish in a bucket that 
Spoke
 always keeps in his hotbar. Spoke has a full shulker of these and usually replaces him every time he dies. He is the oldest important item in the entire series dating all the way back to 
Episode 1
.

It also served as Spoke's secret identity (Fish bucket) while he was infiltrating 
The Invisible Mafia
.

The shulker box full of George Juniors was presumably destroyed when 
JamatoP
 burned all Spoke's inventory and ender chest in 
Finding 1000 Players Who Went Missing
.

Alt (Netherite Chestplate)

Main article: 
Alt

 
 
 
 
 
Spoke
 wearing Alt in the thumbnail for 
I Almost Got BANNED from the Unstable SMP...
 
 

Alt
 is an exploited Netherite Chestplate that 
Spoke
 always wears. It was given to Spoke by 
JamatoP
 when they were testing out the exploits. It has every possible enchant that a chestplate can have, except for Thorns. It has a Snout armor trim with redstone material. Spoke also used to have other illegal exploited armor pieces but they were all broken (except for Alt) while fighting 
Wemmbu
 in 
How to Destroy the Unstable SMP
 and 
The Invisible Mafia
 in 
The End of the Minecraft Mafia
. It accompanied Spoke for more than 2 Seasons. It was later broken by 
Mapicc
 in 
Finding 1000 Players Who Went Missing
 after 
JamatoP
 told him that Spoke was lying to him and that 
Leo
 didn't kill him but Spoke did.

This, alongside George Junior, is the oldest important item in the entire series.

Enchants:
 Protection IV, Blast Protection IV, Fire Protection IV, Projectile Protection IV, Mending, Unbreaking III.
