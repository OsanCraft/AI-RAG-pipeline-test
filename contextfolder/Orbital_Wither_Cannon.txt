The 
orbital wither cannon,
 or the 
blue wither skull nuke cannon
, is a redstone orbital cannon designed by WMC. It launches a payload of blue wither skulls, which are more powerful than standard TNT blasts and can destroy water, lava, and obsidian, making it more powerful then the 
standard orbital cannon.

Function

The orbital wither cannon dupes TNT and stores it in lazy chunks, as well as storing blue wither skulls. The TNT then blasts the wither skulls to the target location, which obliterates the area. 

History

JamatoP reveals his orbital wither cannon and uses it to wipe out SpokeIsHere's army.

JamatoP uses the cannon to destroy Purgatory.

SpokeIsHere presumably gains control of JamatoP's orbital wither cannon(s).
