History

Helios is a horse first seen in 
Unstable SMP: The Great Farm War
 where 
Lomedy
 promised to give Flame a super fast horse if Flame helped him win a farming war against 
Spongs
 and 
Willsion
. Flame agrees to help Lomedy. Spongs griefs 
Lomedy's Farm
 multiple times but together Flame and Lomedy rebuild. Lomedy rides Helios to 
Merchant City
 to trade. Flame and Lomedy also loot abandoned bases and find useful resources like crops, netherite hoes and music discs. Flame and Lomedy decide to counter-raid Spongs' Farm and Flame steals Spongs' prized sniffer egg. Flame manages to kill some of Spongs' teammates using only a hoe and leather armor. Lomedy is furious that Flame cannot follow the farmer code and threntens to not give Helios if Flame breaks the farmer code. Spongs raids the farm again and this nearly convinces Lomedy to give up farming until Flame helps Lomedy rebuild his farm. Together, Lomedy and Flame eventually win the war by destroying Spongs' Farm and making them quit. Lomedy awards Flame the horse as his prize.

Trivia

Helios was usually seen without any horse armor until the events of 
1000 VS 1000 Player Minecraft Civil War
 and 
I Got Hunted By a Minecraft Combat Master
 where Helios was first seen with netherite horse armor.

Helios also is the first pet to be featured on a thumbnail.

Helios is also the first pet to be featured in another protagonist's video before 
Sky
 was featured in 
Wemmbu
's video.

Helios has been featured in more videos than 
Ashen
 and 
George Junior
 making Helios the most featured pet in Unstable Universe

Helios means 'sun' in Greek, and was the Grecian sun god before Apollo took over.
