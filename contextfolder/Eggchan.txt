Personality

Eggchan is a composed and lighthearted individual. He always allies himself with Wemmbu and is one of the few people he trusts. When he's first introduced, Egg was originally a pacifist, claiming that wars can only produce more wars, but in recent episodes, he forgoes this sentiment in favor of the belief that 'sometimes death is the only answer.'     

Egg is more than willing to risk himself to save others that he trusts as seen in recent videos. He is usually depicted as the brains between Wemmbu and himself, although he is seen as a more unskilled and unserious player than his ally. He enjoys simple and calm tasks, such as fishing, saying that after he escaped Proton, he was going to go somewhere to fish for the rest of his time of the server. He opposes lying and advocated protecting the 
Snow Civilization
 from the Mafia, rather than using its citizens to advance Wemmbu’s training.
