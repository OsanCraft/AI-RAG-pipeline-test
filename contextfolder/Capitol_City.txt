Capitol City

Fymada
 located near spawn. It was previously known as 
Capital City
 before Fymada was appointed as Mayor. Located a few hundred blocks from 
Spawn
, this metropolis is a safe space for all players including new ones who survived spawn. It is notably a major economic center of the server, home to many shops, jobs, and houses. Hundreds of players live here, included 
ParrotX2
 and 
TheobaldTheBird

Structure

Capitol City is home to many shops and structures. The city takes a "Desert Empire" theme, being made out of a lot of sandstone and other yellow blocks. But this is not all, as there are some more mental structuring as well. The City has many rules, some are not that great or fair. For example, there is a 90% tax on all items sold. The currency is diamonds, so for every 100 diamonds a player makes, they will only get 10 after taxes. Most of these goods are sold on "Market Street". 
ParrotX2
 used to run a potion shop there as well. There are many different buildings and places in Capitol City as well, each having their importance. 

Main Building

The Building

The Main Building is one of Capitol City's most important attractions. Built in the middle of the complex, this tower is huge, reaching over a hundred blocks in height. 
Reddoons
 has meetings and does tax calculations on the top floor. To explore the Main Building, there are only two known ways of reaching higher floors. You can either climb the ladder to the top—which will take a while or use the secret mini elevators that are reserved for guards and important people, as seen in 
Orbital Strike Cannon vs Unstable SMP
. Either way, players must still get a reservation before climbing to the top, as 
Reddoons
 is almost always busy. 

Train Station

The Station

Capitol City's Train Station is an important part of the civilization. It consists of 50 tracks each leading to separate stations all across the spawn area of 
Unstable
. Players may buy a ticket from the front desk for the price of 4 diamonds after taxes. 

Grand Hotel

The Hotel

The Grand Hotel is not just for a temporary stay, it's also a business outpost of some kind. Although not much is known about it, it is still an important part of the city, being the second largest building in the complex. It can house up to 800 players, although there have still been reports that stay is way too expensive—even from 
Theo
 himself, who was in major debt to the hotel. He had to give up his elytra, which he later received back after "paying back" his debts. 

Post Office/Mail Building

Mailing Complex

The Mail Building/Post Office is home to the city's mailing industry, located near Merchant Street. Players pick up and drop off mail, and from there, the workers will carefully sorting out and place the deliveries where they are supposed to go.

The Lionels

The Left Lionel

The Lionels are some of the most exotic attractions in the entire city. They resemble two huge lions, each on a short pillar. They have a powerful sense in them that suggests to the city's strong power.

History

ParrotX2
 and 
Theo
 went to Capital City, where Parrot found a job in a potion shop, and a secret job with Theo of taking players out of spawn safely to Capital City.  They made a lot of diamonds doing this, but they do get in trouble from 
Reddoons

Hexatope

Parrot said he would only do it if Reddoons clears the debt of every player in the city, which surprisingly, he immediately agreed. Parrot became the city hero after rescuing Hexatope.

The Kingdoms Arc

Parrot, after becoming king, fires SirPig_ from as the mayor, giving 
Fymada
 the role instead. She plans on expanding the city and lowers taxes from 90% to 10% and receives mostly positive support. The city's name was changed to Capitol City.

Parrot becomes king, but his policies are generally unfriendly towards OG's and old players in the server. The reason for this is over population and lack of resources. The 
Capitol City City Police
 almost take over the running off the city. New players who try to enter are killed at the gate and the new players already at Capitol City are forced to live in crowded buildings with a 2x2 for a room.

ShoeBilly
 teams up with the 
police
 to find Parrot. He kills some innocent players when he was fighting with Theo, and even causing a massacre after entering a cramped building. The Police Force is also engaged in the massacre possibly killing up to 100 players or even more. The Police Force warned that that was just the start and threatened on doing a purge of new players in the city. Luckily for all the new players, the plot failed. However for the players who originally lived in Capitol City as well as OG's throughout the server, their conditions are not getting better.

Capitol City was mentioned a multiple times during the Civil War, and appears much later in the war. After a speech by 
Saparata
, Parrot, FlameFrags, and more allies appear including his netherite armed 
Royal Army
 stand ready to defend Capitol City, attempting to arrest Saparata. After ordering Fymada to evacuate the city, Wemmbu declares it a futile effort and executes Arachn1d's orders blowing up the entire evacuation party, and the war goes on which ends with over more than a thousand presumed deaths. The aftermath was shown with half of Capitol City being left in shambles in the end of
