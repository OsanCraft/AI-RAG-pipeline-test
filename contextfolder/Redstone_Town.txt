Redstone Town
 is a major location in 
Unstable Universe
. Unlike the chaotic outer world of the server, Redstone Town stands as the main trading outpost for redstone. It's a safe place where redstone experts and even people who need/have redstone can buy and sell powerful components used for some of the most complex machinery on the server, including key devices like 
Orbital Strike Cannons
 or arrow cannons.

In the town, there are some key landmarks as well, the main ones (that have been shown) being the "
Basic Redstone Shop
", run by 
4CVIT
 and the "
TNT Shop
".  With workshops, vaults, and powerful items behind its walls, it’s more than just a place to buy gear—it’s a critical supply line for survival and power. For the BAT team, their stop at Redstone Town isn’t just business—it’s the key to fighting back.
