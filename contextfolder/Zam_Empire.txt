The 
PrinceZam Empire
, often referred to as the 
Zam Empire
, was a powerful faction led by 
PrinceZam
 in 
Unstable Universe
.

History

Wemmbu 
agrees to help the Zam Empire kill 
SpokeIsHere
. Spoke has his supporters attack the Zam empire. Nobody dies in Spoke’s team except for 
TeamKalal
 as they all used stasis chambers to escape.

Parrot and Wifies go to the Zam Empire multiple times as part of a scavenger hunt set up by Spoke.

The Zam Empire finds Spoke and tries to kill him, but he chunk bans himself before being killed in the same chunk ban that Wifies is in.

Parrot breaks out everybody who PrinceZam had imprisoned, including Wifies and Spoke. PrinceZam hires 
ClownPierce
 to assassinate Parrot but he fails.

PrinceZam asks Wemmbu to do him a favor. He wants Wemmbu to make 
a colony
 to guard a vault he has, and tells him to never go inside. This turns out to just be a plan to kill Wemmbu. He sends his guard 
FlameFrags
 to kill Wemmbu and pretends that FlameFrags betrayed him. Wemmbu checks inside the vault and finds nothing. PrinceZam tells Wemmbu that he is too weak to be a threat to the empire anymore.

Wemmbu goes to his 
Orbital Strike Cannon
 to try and destroy PrinceZam’s empire, but sees the orbital cannon has been blown up. Wemmbu and 
eggchan
 go back to spawn and see that the Zam Empire built a giant PrinceZam statue at spawn.

Wemmbu and eggchan discover that PrinceZam has started taking over the spawn. Once they are far enough from the Zam Empire, 
ManePear
 says that he’s not a big fan of being a guard of PrinceZam, and that if they need help, they can help him.

Wemmbu and eggchan blow up the PrinceZam statue at spawn.

Wemmbu and his civilization try to use an orbital cannon to destroy the Zam Empire, but see that they’ve put a sheet of obsidian above the entire empire meaning that they can’t use the orbital cannon. They decide to try and break into the empire and kill PrinceZam. They are unable to kill them. they are able to escape.

Wemmbu convinces ManePear to betray the Zam Empire.

ManePear distracts 
yungwillx
, a guard of the Zam Empire, by talking to him while Wemmbu sets up a trap at his base. Wemmbu comes into the base invisible and pretends to be ManePear. He sets off the trap, but it fails to kill him. Yungwillx gets PrinceZam, 
Merffinn
, and 
Peentar
 on to help him survive. All of yungwillx’s armor breaks, and they take him hostage. They leave a message at Peentar’s house with instructions for him. They take Peentar hostage. While they are taking Sorhin hostage, the Zam empire breaks out yungwillx and Peentar. 

The entire Zam empire destroys 
Wemmbu's Civilization
. They trap Wemmbu in an obsidian box and they watch ManePear and FlameFrags fight. ManePear’s armor breaks, so he stops fighting. They both run away, but then realize that they took eggchan, Wemmbu's friend.

PrinceZam takes down the obsidian sheet protecting the Zam Empire. Wemmbu goes over to PrinceZam, with no armor, and they imprison Wemmbu. 

Wemmbu enters the Zam Empire on the pretense of reuniting with eggchan. The guards take him to Zam, and they take him to eggchan's cell. Zam forces Wemmbu to go through an inventory check before entering eggchan's cell. Wemmbu drops all of his items, but saves his ender chests, fishing rod, elytra, and . Wemmbu triggers the orbital cannon, causing a lag spike long enough to allow Wemmbu to place down an ender chest and regearshimself and eggchan. Wemmbu flies away with eggchan, pursued by Zam and his guards. Eggchan escapes, while Zam and his guards chase Wemmbu around the Zam Empire, attempting to kill him mid-flight.

The orbital strike begins coming in, gradually destroying the Zam Empire. Zam's guards abandon him, leaving Zam to fight Wemmbu alone. After the fight, ClownPierce takes Zam, Wemmbu, eggchan, and Pangi to a prison and the empire ends.
