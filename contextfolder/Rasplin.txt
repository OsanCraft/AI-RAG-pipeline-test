Rasplin
 was a minor antagonist in 
Unstable Universe
. He was recently seen in 
ParrotX2
's video, 
Finding the Tomb of the Minecraft Mafia
, where he served as a gambler in a floating house far out in the End. 

History

Rasplin was seen as a member in 
Roshambogames Civilization
 which was attacked by the 
PrinceZam Empire
.

He challenged Parrot to a game of eight-fold, and if Parrot won he would give him the key he needed to open the door to the Tomb of the Mafia. He places increasingly large bets and wins every game, taking a shulker box full of diamonds and Parrot's armor. 

Shortly after their last game, he was killed by 
Marlowww
 in an attempt to retrieve the key.

Trivia

Rasplin likely rigged the game of eight-fold, evidenced by the fact he won every game he played with Parrot. Assuming this is true, he used the rather ingenious strategy of getting his dealer 
HerobaneNair
 to give Parrot a high-value card, making him bet confidently, then following the strong card with a weak one.

He owns a Roblox rant channel with over 800k subscribers.

He has returned to Minecraft recently after a long period of retirement.
