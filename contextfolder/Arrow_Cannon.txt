An 
Arrow Cannon
 is a powerful ranged weapon that fires in a fixed trajectory. It is also sometimes (inaccurately) called a 
railgun
. It fires by using TNT to launch arrows at an extremely high velocity, capable of one-shot killing even fully geared netherite players with double-handed totems. This works due to the Minecraft mechanic of arrows dealing more damage the faster they travel, and because the cannon's arrows register as several rapid hits instead of one.

A variant of the arrow cannon is the 
Orbital Arrow Cannon
, which fires at the operator's crosshair direction instead of a fixed direction. It can either be ground-to-air or air-to-ground.
