Personality

Swight is characterized as a cold and calculating mercenary. Unlike other figures in the Unstable Universe who are driven by power or faction loyalty, Swight is motivated almost entirely by professionalism and profit. He views the world through the lens of contracts and compensation, famously showing no interest in the identity or "justice" of his targets. 

History

Swight was originally contracted by 
the Law
 with a clear mission: to track down and eliminate 
FlameFrags
. To find a student worthy of his time, Swight hosted a tournament to see who was the "best player" for him to teach, eventually selecting a mysterious "invisible player."

Throughout their sessions, Swight put the player through rigorous combat trials, unaware that his student was actually his target, FlameFrags, using a secret identity. Swight’s training focused on elite PvP mechanics, reflecting his real-world status as a Tier 1 player in multiple disciplines.

The tension peaked when the invisible player was revealed to be FlameFrags. Instead of completing the assassination for the Law, Swight showed his true mercenary nature. He agreed to spare Flame and "betray" the original contract in exchange for high-tier gear vault. This solidified Swight as a character who values personal gain and skill over professional loyalty to any organisation
