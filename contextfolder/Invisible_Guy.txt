History

I Joined  100,000 Player Minecraft Server

Wemmbu
 was tasked to collect a mace by 
FieldsOfElysium
. For knowing the location of the mace, he needed to fight a 4V4 Battle in an arena. When one of the warriors on Wemmbu's side escaped in terror when they saw 
TheGOdOfWar
, the Assassin offered himself as the replacement, and thanks to their help, they won.

Later, when 
Wemmbu
 is at the mace's location and is about to get the mace, the Assassin threw an enderpearl and quickly took it. Wemmbu and the Assassin fought, with Wemmbu ultimately winning and forcing the Assassin to give the mace back.

Minecraft Pro Vs 1000 Player Civilization

At the end of the video, 2 players managed to take 
Eggchan
´s book. When they were about to read it, the Assassin appeared and 
killed them both with a TNT minecart
. The Assassin takes the book.

I Got Hunted by the World's Deadliest Assassin

Wemmbu
 and 
Eggchan
 were at their base, when suddenly the Assassin shows up, saying he has come to claim Eggchan's life, threatens Wemmbu, and uses a 
Stasis Chamber
 to teleport away. Wemmbu ignores the threat, and is immediately hit by an arrow railgun. Suddenly, the Assassin appears above him, using a 
mace
 to pop another of Wemmbu's Totems. After Wemmbu retaliates, the Player teleports away again. Confused, they head back to their base, when the Assassin appears in the ground below Wemmbu and explodes him. In the ensuing encounter, the Assassin almost bans Wemmbu before teleporting away.

Later, after defeating 
Dizzyu
's mercenaries, the Assassin shows up, attempting to kill Wemmbu using a 
Crossbow Cart
. The ensuing battle ends with Wemmbu and Eggchan splitting up and escaping.

Wemmbu tries to buy more PvP materials at a local village, however, when he arrives, he finds the inhabitants killed and the Assassin waiting for him. After tricking Wemmbu into wasting the last of his 
Orbitals
, he traps him in a Redstone Flying Machine designed to contain Wemmbu. The Assassin tells Wemmbu that he was paid to capture him alive, presumably by 
Arachnid's Kingdom
, and wants Eggchan dead for personal reasons. Wemmbu is broken out and saved by 
ParrotX2
.

After escaping the Assassin's Trap and briefly fighting him, Wemmbu and Parrot split up, Wemmbu intending to ban the Assassin. Convincing him to burn his 
Stasis Rods
, Wemmbu starts fighting the Assassin, who uses a Trident, Mace and Elytra to fight him. Wemmbu finally manages to ban the Assassin, Cytharam. His Armor, his Trident, his Elytra, and his Mace do not drop. We only see following items drop from the kill:

Wind Charges

Breeze Rods

Invisibility Potions

Cobblestone

Golden Apples

Enchanted Golden Apples

Bottles o' Enchanting

Ender Chests

Water Buckets

Shulker Box

Sweet Berry Bushes

Wemmbu loots Cytharam's Items. Suddenly, he hears the Assassin's Voice behind him. The screen cuts to black as Wemmbu turns around, revealing the Assassin somehow still standing behind him.
