ralamus
 was a player featured in 
Unstable Universe
. He was a member of the 
Stoneface Bandits
 and was killed by 
ReinaDrop
.

Personality

Little is known of Ralamus's personality due to his lack of speaking lines and quick death, but others in his group treat him as a powerful warrior and hype him up, telling us he is well-liked by them. He also probably is somewhat good at PVP due to him being selected to fight Reina.

History

Some time in the past he is stated to have single handedly killed the entire Snowlicker clan which is treated as a very impressive feat.

He was summoned to 1v1 
ReinaDrop
. She dropped a dripstone on him, killing him.

Trivia

He was played by HappyJaffa.

Ralamus actually had two other people playing him, but was killed during testing. HappyJaffa was the third rendition of Ralamus.
