LuigiToan
, also known as 
Luigi
, was a minor character in 
Unstable Universe
.

History

LuigiToan builds a house and creates a journal. LuigiToan befriends 
CoinMonke
.

PrinceZam
 recruits LuigiToan into the 
Zam Empire
.

A fight breaks out in the Zam Empire. LuigiToan is moved to a different house.

LuigiToan helps the Zam Empire chunk ban 
SpokeIsHere
.

LuigiToan is seen guarding a prison containing SpokeIsHere and 
Wifies
.

LuigiToan is seen during the Zam Empire's last invasion of 
Wemmbu's Civilization
.

LuigiToan leaves the Zam Empire.

CoinMonke forces LuigiToan to mine at 
Capital City
.

Reddoons
 forces people in Capital City to play a game which decides if they live or do not live. Reddoons rigs it so LuigiToan dies. CoinMonke refuses to kill him, so Reddoons kills LuigiToan himself.
