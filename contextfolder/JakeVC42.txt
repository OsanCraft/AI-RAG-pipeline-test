Personality

Jake was known for being more serious and focused than the rest of the Law Team. He was a relatively skilled player, getting kills before FlameFrags even with significantly weaker gear. He followed orders and stayed on task, often keeping formation and reacting to threats while others, like 
Spongs
, 
Lomedy
, and 
Gunter
, were more chaotic. Many viewers saw him as one of the more reliable members of the Law Team, with some saying he was one of the only ones who listened to FlameFrags's commands. His behavior during the mission led to him being seen as a dependable member of the squad.

Trivia

Jake is one of very few characters to gain significant screen time without being able to talk, often using only his expressions and movement to communicate.
