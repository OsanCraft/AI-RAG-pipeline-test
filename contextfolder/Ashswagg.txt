Personality

Ashswagg is known to be power-hungry, controlling, egotistical, manipulative, and evil, seeking to bring down civilizations with the end goal of taking over the server with his 
Mafia
. He thrives on entertainment and mostly uses this as a point of contingent to mock other's attempts to take him down. He tends to feign importance and uses more 'abstract' ideas, using indirect and flowery language, biblical motifs/metaphors, and basing a lot of his actions around philosophical ideas and metaphors.  

Ash very commonly uses and shows manipulation tactics: going into detail on using fear as a method of control in 
I Survived Minecraft's Most Corrupt City
 or pretending to be poor and helpless around 
Spoke
 to earn his trust and gain access to an important book, until ultimately betraying 
Spoke's
 mission and threatening to kill 
Mapicc
.  

Despite this, he has a more unserious side, as shown in his interactions with 
Squiddo
 along with his dramatic lamenting while talking to lesser ranked 
Mafia
 members. His interactions with 
Fish Bucket
 and his friends also show that he's rather lonely and insecure.

He also seen to somewhat fear remorse and actively shields himself from this and other's emotions. This is exhibited when Parrot confronts Ash about killing Wifies in 
Unstable SMP: Doomsday
 and Ash simply claims it wasn't him. This is also shown throughout his interactions with Squiddo. He is shown to believe that every action a player does is for power and appears to be aware that he went too far in his goal for it. He also claims to see many of his own qualities in Spoke and believes Spoke will someday make a 
mafia bigger and worse than his own
.
