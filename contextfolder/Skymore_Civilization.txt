The 
Skymore Civilization
 was a failed, now destroyed civilization created somewhat far out from spawn by 
Parrot
, the king of the Unstable SMP. It was probably meant to solve the overpopulation crisis at Spawn, the Northern Council, and Capital City. 

History

The civilization was meant as a safe haven for the new players who have been getting attacked by older ones. ParrotX2 brings 1000 players there, promising to return in a week with supplies while they set up the civilization. However, when he returns, the civilization is abandoned. there is no clear cause as to why it is abandoned, but there is a sign from a player named Zoe to her friend, Reina. Later, we find out that this is a player named 
zoe444
 who escaped from the 
Cindercrest
 nation, leaving clues for her friend 
ReinaDrop
 to find. 999 of the original players have been killed by ShoeBilly and Cindercrest including Zoe, with the sole survivor being ReinaDrop. When the Cindercrest nation attacked Skymore, 990 players were killed with 10 being taken hostage. Soon, 8 more died, leaving only Zoe, who escaped after being scared by 
Saparata
 but likely died of starvation, and Reina, who is now traveling with ParrotX2 and 
TheobaldTheBird
 to defeat the Cindercrest nation.

Later we find that 
ShoeBilly
, a renowned assassin, slowly killed all 1000 players when they went on expeditions for food. The only survivors of Skymore were Zoe and Reina who were forced to grow wheat for Cindercrest.
