JamatoP
, also known as 
Jamato
, is a supporting character—turned antagonist, featured in the 
Unstable Universe
. He was 
Spoke
's first ally on the server and now serves as his arch-nemesis as the leader of the 
Null Hunters
. He serves as the main antagonist of 
The NULL Arc
 and its continuation 
The Purgatory Arc
.

Personality

JamatoP is at first seen to value morals and boundaries a lot, refusing to mess with Minecraft's code and leaving when Spoke plays with it. He cared a lot about his friends, trying to keep Spoke safe during the 
Mafia
 by disappearing. He displayed some amount of kindness, being one of the only people on the server willing to hang out with Spoke; but his willingness to bend his own morals to do things just as bad or worse than his enemies made this kindness less prevalent.   

After a period of hiding, his personality shifted to be more self-righteous and condescending overall. It is shown that he has grown a large amount of disdain for Spoke, blackmailing his former friend into silence about NULL twice. He is incredibly determined and smart, being about to defeat the former leaders of NULL and going to great lengths to get what he wants, further backed up by his self-righteousness. He seems to harbor a large amount of denial that he could ever be in the wrong or go too far, only accepting this when Spoke finally defeats him. He claims that he only wanted his old friend back but is unable to see that part of Spoke's situation is his own fault. He refuses to directly kill Spoke, as killing Spoke would mean that he was right about Jamato being just as much of a monster as he is; something Jamato simply can't live with.
