Personality

Kenadian is highly intelligent, adaptable, and cautious. He like to always have a plan and have extensive knowledge of Minecraft mechanics and prison escaping.   

Fearful of the risk of his friends dying, he was initially determined to keep all of them safe and inside of the prison rather than let them take on a heavy risk of escaping 
Proton
. His pursuit for their team's safety leads him to nearly sabotage 
ParrotX2's
 breakout plan. Kenadian is very selfless when it comes to their friends and trusts them greatly, sacrificing himself to let 
Parrot
 escape, because he knew they would find his Stasis Chamber and break him out.
