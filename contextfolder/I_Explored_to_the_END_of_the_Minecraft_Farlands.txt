I Explored to the END of the Minecraft Farlands
 is the 58th episode featured in 
Unstable Universe
. 

Plot

Wemmbu
 and 
Eggchan
 continue exploring the farlands to get more powerful since Wemmbu is tired of losing fights. They explore a cherry blossom biome then a few mountain biomes. Their goal is to find The Forge and get powerful gear. They continue exploring until they find a trial chamber. Wemmbu didn't like the sight of a trial chamber as he is one of the only mace wielders remaining since 
The Mafia
 destroyed all the trial chambers on the regular Unstable SMP. He loots the trial chamber and destroys all the ominous vaults until he gets attacked by a player in full netherite named 
Firestar2008
. He almost kills 
Firestar2008
 but he uses a stasis chamber.

Eggchan suggests that they should find him as he may know something about The Forge. Wemmbu tells Eggchan that he cannot follow him because he doesn't know where he went; Eggchan sends Wemmbu screenshots from the fight where 
Firestar2008
 messes up and shows his lodestone compass. They follow the direction they believe the lodestone compass lead to. As they explore they find more weird biomes such as more glitched terrains, and a biome with chunks of every biome in the game. They attempt to go to the nether but to their surprise, it doesn't work in the farlands. 

They continue exploring and finding more glitched terrain with repeating patterns. They get lucky as they find a repeating dungeon with infinite enchanted golden apples and experience until the chests gets cut off. They continue exploring and they reach the bottom which is water that leads to the void. They ride a boat until they see the end of the terrain. They see an ocean monument covered by glass. They explore it and find more echoes. This time it's an echo of 
Lakerek
 with all his friends fighting an elder guardian. They leave and check their stasis chambers. They continue exploring until they find a fractal-like pattern of grass blocks. They fly through the grass blocks and Wemmbu notices the blocks getting farther apart from each other. Wemmbu tries to restock on wind charges from Eggchan but they get attacked by another player named 
Nitenly
. 

Nitenly
 tries to kill Wemmbu since he also has a mace. Wemmbu successfully hits 
Nitenly
 to the void but he doesn't die which could mean that one of the stasises that they had previously encountered belonged to Nitenly. Eggchan suggests that he's attacking because they don't want them entering their home since he killed Lakerek. They are about to reach the absolute end of the farlands as there are much less grass blocks. They find a stronghold and loot it to find eye trims (which no longer exist on the regular Unstable SMP since The Mafia used them all) they make a cobblestone generator in the portal room to get more blocks to bridge with. Wemmbu finds another trial chamber farm and he also finds a house which he then blows up by making TNT with the gunpowder from the dungeons he looted earlier. 

As they reach the end, they find a platform with a tree. Wemmbu decides to attack 
Nitenly
 again as he was the one guarding the platform. Wemmbu breaks some of 
Nitenly
's armour and he escapes using his stasis. They continue bridging as the sign warned them to turn back and they find a city in the void.

How I SURVIVED the Unstable SMP
