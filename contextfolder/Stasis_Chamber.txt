Stasis Chamber
, or simply 
stasis
, is an ender pearl that stays and oscillates at a safe place and can be triggered (pulled) by a variety of mechanisms, manually or remotely. It is widely used across the 
Unstable SMP
 and many other servers.

Mechanism

The ender pearl is thrown into an upward bubble column created by soul sand and water, or thrown at a strict angle towards some honey blocks on an oscillating piston. Basically, any mechanic that can make a pearl stay in stasis works for a stasis chamber. The former one can be turned into a trap by adding wind charged chicken launcher above the pearl and piston to remove soul sand under the pearl, with bedrock below the soul sand removed by glitch. The pearl will load the chunk so it is always triggerable (after 1.21.2). Fishing rod is connected to wirelessly pulling stasis by the same glitch used in setting fishing rod trigger for 
Orbital Strike Cannon
 and 
Orbital Dog Cannon
