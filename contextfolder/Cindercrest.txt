Cindercrest
 is the ruling faction of Unstable consisting of elite warriors led by 
Saparata
. Cindercrest is known for their revanchist ideals favoring the old players while killing off new players or forcing them to work for Cindercrest. They are one of the only factions to have all of the most powerful fighters on the server working with them. Cindercrest is seen as one of the richest factions to be ever on the server, being able to equip thousands of players with full netherite gear. While they were more advanced, they were also lesser in numbers compared to the 
Royal Army
. They are the main antagonists in 
The Kingdoms Arc
 of 
ParrotX2
's POV. 

Background

The leader of Cindercrest is general 
Saparata
, who commands the entire Grand Cindercrest Army. His goals are to take over 
Parrot's Kingdom
 and position himself as the King. Due to its alliance with 
Arachn1d's Kingdom
, it is likely he acknowledges 
Arachn1d
 as a king as well and a valid pretender to the throne. 
Saparata
 has achieved his goals and has become Unstable SMP's second King.

Cindercrest's creation was driven due to the new players joining the server have been taking up huge amounts of resources, leading to supposed food shortage for "old players". Therefore they've made it their primary goal to eliminate the server's new players, destroy 
ParrotX2
's kingdom and kill everyone not involved with them. According to 
Saparata
, after he and his friends almost starved to death and his friends were killed by bandits 
Saparata
 decided to create Cindercrest to enact revenge on the "new players" of the server blaming them for the food and resource shortage. Despite that Cindercrest attacks also old players that are not loyal to him, demonstrating their hypocrisy. Also in fight in Capitol City, Saparata manipulates the new players into his side, which most of them agreed and defected to Cindercrest side. However, he ends up lying and kills them all anyways.
