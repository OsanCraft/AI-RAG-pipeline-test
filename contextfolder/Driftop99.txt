Driftop99
 was a minor antagonist and the leader of the 
Sculk Kingdom
.   

History

He sent an army to 
Wemmbu
's Base. They ended up kidnapping 
Eggchan
. They met up with 
Arachn1d
. 

He faces Wemmbu in front of the castle. His very unintimidating voice is equally as threatening as Wemmbu in Prot II armor. They decide to 1v1. Wemmbu is unable to beat him because of lack of fireworks, which destroys his principal fighting strategy. 

As a last-ditch effort, Wemmbu pots Turtle Master and tries orbitaling Drift. This allows Wemmbu to land a Mace Combo and finally kill him.
