Personality

ShoeBilly_ has been shown to be sadistic and spiteful to the server's new players, placing him firmly on the side of the old ones. Billy craves power and has no qualms about killing people to increase his own personal strength and influence. The danger Billy poses is further tempered by his confidence and PVP ability.

He is a self-described selfish person, and his philosophy is one where the strong rule. He is at one point referred to as "the butcher" by a corrupt member of the Capital City Police Force, suggesting a long history of previous bloody crimes. When he first meets Parrot, he is already a mercenary and assassin of renown. His ascent to this status was no doubt fueled by his ruthless greed. He believes that friends hold people back and are worthless and he says so to Theo and others.   

History

Shoebilly joins 
Capital City police
 and attacks 
Parrot
 and 
TheoBaldTheBird

He later destroys a building with hundreds of new players inside. He plans a massacre of the server's new players in Captial City, but the plot is foiled and he retreats to whence he came. 

CinderCrest

Skymore.
 The civilization is easily overrun by him and Cindercrest's forces. Devoid of compassion or mercy, Shoebilly enacts yet another bloodbath, this time killing almost 1000 new players. The massacre is of the same scale as the one Wemmbu and Flamefrags enacted in their 
2v1000

Law

Parrot finally confronts him after reading Zoe's story and asks him if he feels any remorse. Shoebilly says he feels none at all and tells Parrot that only the strong are meant to survive, which reminds Parrot of old memories with 
Ashswagg
. However, Shoe is forced into a confrontation with Theo. After a very hard-fought battle, Shoebilly is forced to retreat. When Theobald returns to Parrot later, his netherite armor is missing, presumably broken by Shoe.
