FlameFrags Base
 is a megabase built by 
LAW
 soldiers. Flame most likely decided to lead a peaceful life after 
LettuceK
's imprisonment. This is the base currently used by 
FlameFrags
, 
Spongs
 and 
NotSkrilley
.

History

The base was built by LAW soldiers for FlameFrags but LAW soldiers came to take down the base after it was determined more resources were used on the base than planned. Flame had to pay the LAW back by working as a LAW soldier for 100 days after the commander refused his extra netherite armor.

After the events of the 
Mist Civilization
, NotSkrilly joins Flame and resides at the base.

Wemmbu
 partially destroyed the base in search of 
Eggchan
. It was rebuilt by the LAW. Spongs commissioned a custom dwelling under the base during the rebuilding and currently also resides in the base despite Flame's displeasure.
