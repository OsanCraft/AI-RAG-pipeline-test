Battles of Capital City

Battle for Capital City I

The Battle for Capital City I was a conflict between two forces: the Capital City Guard Force lead by 
Salvationism
 and a group of bandits lead by 
Boosfer
. The Guard Force leads a group of soldiers to the wall of the attack and builds impromptu reinforcements before the bandits arrive by water. The bandits attempt to scale the wall and defeat the guards, but are repulsed with the aid of Wemmbu. 
Result:
 Capital City victory.

Battle for Capital City II

The Battle for Capital City II was a conflict between anarchist SpokeIsHere and a group of LAW soldiers lead by Deputy_Ace. SpokeIsHere brings the LAW an ultimatium to present his (believed) friend Mapicc within twenty-four hours. When Spoke doesn't see Mapicc, he attacks Capital City. Before the gates, Deputy_Ace attempts to stop him with a force of diamond LAW soldiers. SpokeIsHere kills most of the soldiers with TNT. Deputy_Ace flees to LAW headquarters and Spoke gains control of Capital City. 
Result:
 SpokeIsHere victory.

Aftermath

Spoke plants TNT over the entire city and builds four TNT Cannons on each side of the main building and prepares to nuke Capital City. ParrotX2 arrives from Northern Council and persuades Spoke to relent. Spoke goes searching for Mapicc.

Campaign for Capital City

The Campaign for Capital City was a series of skirmishes between King ParrotX2 with TheobaldTheBird and a group of soldiers from the Merchant City Vanguard lead by ArthurTheKnight2; and the rogue guards in Capital City. ParrotX2 infiltrates Capital City with TheobaldTheBird and ArthurTheKnight2 and splits up with them. ShoeBilly_ ambushes TheobaldTheBird and kills ArthurTheKnight2 resulting in a fight between ShoeBilly and TheobaldTheBird. Meanwhile, ParrotX2 fights a group of policemen on a chain tightrope between buildings and escapes. 

Eventually, ParrotX2 is cornered in a holding area for new players by three policemen. TheobaldTheBird ambushes them after fighting with ShoeBilly_, resulting in them defecting and leaking the location of the majority of policemen. TheobaldTheBird, ParrotX2, and Fymada capture the policemen and regain control of Capital City.

Battle for Capital City III

Saparata forces LettuceK to attempt to persuade old players to join his cause. ParrotX2 and his commanders confront saparata and inform him that his gear stash has been destroyed. Parrot instructs Fymada to evacuate all citizens, but Wemmbu launches an orbital strike cannon nuke shot, killing hundreds of innocent players. Cindercrest and Cave Kingdom armies invade Capital City through the ruins of the main building as ShoeBilly and Wemmbu attack Parrot and his commanders. Parrot instructs ReinaDrop to open the gates, allowing the Royal Army to enter the city and attack the Cindercrest and Cave Kingdom armies. Siege of the End Civilization

The End Civilization was a civilization located millions of blocks from Spawn in the End dimension, built by a team of builders led by 
Horace_Altman
 and founded by ParrotX2, Wifies, and deanthebean9. It was protected by chunkbans and later a dome of glass and water.

The Mafia cannot cross the chunkbans, causing them to construct and send A1C bomber planes (MythicalPingu) to destroy it. Following this, the glass dome is built.  The Mafia then gets a mole in, who turns off the chunk bans, allowing the Mafia to take the civilization. Parrot flees and returns to destroy it to insure that the Mafia cannot control it. 
Result:
 Total Mafia victory.

Fall of the Square Civilization

The Square Civilization was a civilization led by Nico and Cash. It had little to no defenders (or armor) and only had defences of one block wide tall granite walls, which were easily destroyed in the Mafia's wake. The assault on Square Civilization was headed by ashwagg himself and had dozens of soldiers in the attacking force, allowing them to take the city in minutes. 
Result:
 Absolute Mafia victory.

The Battle For Mafia Stasis Chambers was one of the most important battles of the server's history, being the battle that ended 
The Invisible Mafia
. Directed by 
ParrotX2
, the "battle" was an operation performed by mainly two players, them being 
SpokeIsHere
 and 
Mapicc
.

Planning

The idea of the battle was that the two players would go to the mafia's "stasis chamber headquarters", which was used to keep the mafia's members forever loyal, and destroy them, ending 
Ashswagg
's power arc. It took many weeks of planing to get the coordinates for the HQ, but in the end, with spoke's help, they managed to pull it off by using a book that was found in 
Quackenstein123
's base. It had hints to the HQ's location which was later deciphered in another act. After destroying all the chambers, the mafia's members were free from the curse, and they could become visible again. This day is the reason for the 
BMC/AMC Timeline
, as the Battle of Stasis is the mid marking point for the server's time according to this fandom's users.

Battle of the Zam Empire

 
 
 
 
 
 

Summary

The 
Zam Empire
 was, at the time, the biggest civilization of all time, rivaling even 
The Invisible Mafia
 in power and control of the server. The empire was based on a social-monarchy type system. 
PrinceZam
 was the emperor of this complex. He owned most (if not all) of the land and power. In the mist of all of this, a new conflict started.

The Conflict

It all started when PrinceZam made enemies with 
Wemmbu
.  Zam betrayed him and broke their alliance as Wemmbu was "so unbelievably weak" for the civilization's development. Wemmbu and his small team were enraged by this, but PrinceZam had no fear. He did not believe a player he found "useless" could do anything against his team of some of the best players on the server.  Wemmbu constructed an orbital strike cannon.

Battles at Spawn

Skirmish at Mafia Spawn Prison

The skirmish at the Mafia Spawn Prison was an attack between Wemmbu and eggchan against Mafia soldiers to create a distraction as ParrotX2, SpokeIsHere, and Mapicc infiltrate the prison below. Wemmbu kills several soldiers and forces others to retreat, but is forced to retreat after the arrival of Mafia reinforcements. Wemmbu flees inside the Prison as eggchan escapes. 
Result:
 successful distraction, Wemmbu-eggchan victory. 

Aftermath:
 SpokeIsHere finds the pearl cannon to the Mafia Stasis Chambers and destroys the Mafia.

Duel at Spawn

Following saparata's successful destruction of Capital City, he moves on by himself to capture Spawn, which has been left unguarded. saparata is confronted by King ParrotX2, resulting in a duel. saparata defeats ParrotX2 and captures Spawn for Cindercrest. 
Result:
 decisive Cindercrest victory.
