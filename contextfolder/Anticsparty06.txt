Anticsparty06
 was a player who first appeared in 
Unstable Universe
 
Season 3
. 

History

He was a peaceful player, building his base with his friends, until 
Null Hunters
 showed up. They raided his base and kidnapped him and his friends. They were brought to the Road of NULL, where Antics used the ender pearl cannon to teleport to the 
Far Lands
.

He built a new ender pearl cannon to take him to spawn. He wrote a book called 
road of null
 before getting captured by them.

Trivia

The name anticsparty06 is most likely a reference to one of Spoke's alternate Minecraft accounts, being anticsparty53, which dates back to 2020 or earlier.
