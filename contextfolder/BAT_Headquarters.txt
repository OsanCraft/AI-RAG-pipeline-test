Features

Player-launching system

This system helped players exit the base, launching them a thousand blocks in the sky, so they could use elytra more effectively.

Control room and alarm signal

The operator in the control room could send alarm signals to all rooms or to a specific room.

Emergency button

Players could send an emergency signal through a button in their rooms.

Communication system

Each room was equipped with a dispenser which could send written books to the other members of BAT. The sender would input the room's number as the book's name.

Privacy system

This system is a special lava-curtain between the two layers of the window.

Storage room

Although mostly blown up this room still contains massive amounts of wealth and treasure
