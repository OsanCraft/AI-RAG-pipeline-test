The Totem Of Null
 is a Totem of Undying enchanted with Curse of Vanishing that controls all of 
NULL
, currently owned by 
Spoke
. It is the only known enchanted totem on the server. The Totem is said to have the power to drop all of the stasises of NULL into the void if popped. It is also the reason why the strongest/Leader in NULL is the owner of the totem, due the risk of losing the totem, putting NULL in jeopardy.  

Origin

The Totem Of Null
 is said to be given to the strongest player in 
NULL
 due to the risk of it being stolen. The totem belonged to an unknown leader of Null prior to 
JamatoP
's leadership. JamatoP had beaten the previous leader of NULL and took control of the totem, and by proxy all of NULL aswell. 

After Spoke beat JamatoP in a challenge for the Totem of Null, he took control of the totem and became the current leader of NULL.
