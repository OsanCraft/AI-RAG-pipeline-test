Dingus
 is the name of 
TheobaldTheBird
's donkey, which was gifted to him.

Theo originally had thought Dingus was a horse, until he tried to put armor onto it and failed. However, when he saw Dingus's speed, Theo's disappointment faded. 

Theo also truly cares about him as when he was taken by the 
Capitol City Police Force
 and when the merchant city guard force got him back Theo said, "I missed you buddy".

Dingus and Parrot's horse got kidnapped by 
Cindercrest
 during the events of 
Searching for a Civilization that Doesn't Exist
.

In 
Unstable SMP: Civil War
, 
ShoeBilly
 is seen riding Dingus.

Trivia

According to Theo, Dingus doesn't like ShoeBilly. Parrot also agrees and said he looks like an idiot on Dingus.
