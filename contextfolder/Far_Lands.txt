The 
Far Lands
 is a terrain that "replaces the world border" and generates 12 million blocks from 
Spawn
. The more you traverse into the Far Lands, the more glitches occur, and more terrain is corrupted and deleted. The glitches are said to change a lot. The end of the Far Lands is the Minecraft void. There are many directions in the Far Lands; however, the one seen the most is the direction towards the 
Far Lands Civilization
 and SerpentBound's kingdom. 
PlanetLord
 says that he thinks that the player who first discovered The Far Lands was the one to make it such a mysterious places and generated all of these Biomes, glitches and exploits.

Inhabitance

In the Far Lands, you will most likely find 3 types of individual player entities. First, there are the 
Farland Zombies
, people trapped in with no clear way out. Some zombies are either early adventurers or players captured by 
NULL
 that tried to escape. 
First Generation Players
, who are more experienced and richer "zombies". 
Echoes
. Not real players, just predictions of them that repeat over and over. Almost nothing is known about them, and finally 
NULL
 who patrol The Farlands and capture escapees and other Farland zombies.

The 
Null Hunters
 is known to recently starting to take over Far Lands. This created more Farland Zombies who had to survive in the resource-scarce region or refugees like in PlanetLord's base. NULL, however is not invincible. Players can use biomes to their advantage, shown in the Dark Forest where an entire squadron was disrupted and eventually destroyed in The Dark Forest. NULL hunters in The Farlands are also less experienced, and thus do not have Totems of Undying that can teleport them, since they usually fight escapees and other Farland zombies armed with very weak armor. 

Glitches and Mechanics

The Far Lands has many glitches and mechanics that make it a truly mysterious place on the Unstable SMP. These are some of the seen glitches and mechanics of the Far Lands.

This includes infinite repeating loops of past historical events. The main echoes that the Far Lands give home to include the 
Zam Empire
 creation echo and the First Generation Players vs Elder Guardian. Echoes, or memories, are repetitions of past events. They occur in the Far Lands as well as in 
the Underworld
.

Perhaps the biggest elephant in the room is the repeating patterns of the Far Lands. These patterns take existing structures, whether man-made or not, and make them so that they infinitely repeat. A few of the infinite repeating patterns include the infinite dungeon and spawner that 
Wemmbu
 and 
Eggchan
 looted, gaining over 64 enchanted golden apples. Another massive repeating pattern is the infinite array of 
Orbital Strike Cannons
. These are the very orbitals that Wemmbu uses to this day. It is unknown what creates a repeating pattern because it's not as though they only repeat naturally generated structures, but they also include the orbital cannons, which aren't natural. This implies that there are duplication glitches in the Far Lands, which would explain how the 
OG players
 got to be so rich in the first place, and are likely related to 
the Forge
. 
Sharpness
 also only went to the Far Lands to try and find a duplication glitch.

Another glitch is that of connection between 
the Underworld
 and the Far Lands, because when you use a nether portal anywhere but the Far Lands, it takes you to the nether, but when you use an existing nether portal in the Far Lands (you cannot build one), it takes you to the Underworld. This is interesting because it implies that there is a significant history that links the Underworld and the Far Lands together. 
Null Hunters
 can light portals in the Far Lands but not normal players. It is unclear whether these portals lead to the Nether or the Underworld.

Far Lands features biomes that has pathways that led to different worlds. Examples include a cave found in the Flowery Plains that leads to the Red Plains, although under Null presence, and somewhere in the Dark Forest hiding a staircase which was found to go infinitely down. The staircase is also connected to different worlds shown in 
Finding 1000 Players Who Went Missing
 as they leave the same staircase, but it is theorized to connect to the Underworld. The Fabric itself has numerous paths leading to locations currently unknown.

Mapicc has mentioned that he stays away from biomes due to rumors of players coming back whose "souls are corrupted into darkness". This means that some Far Lands biomes are not only corruptions in world generation, but also a cognitohazard.

Some biomes are capable of manipulating game mechanics. The Dark Forest is a good example, taken to the extreme where lighting and visibility is very limited while also names and skins being hidden even without crouching. 

PlanetLord has said that the first person who discovered the Farlands generated all of these glitches.
