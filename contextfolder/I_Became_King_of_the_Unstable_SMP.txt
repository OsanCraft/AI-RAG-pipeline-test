Plot

Parrot
 starts off the episode by explaining how many players have tried ruling the Unstable SMP, but all have failed except one; 
LettuceK
. Lettuce started like any other player, all the way at the bottom and joined the corrupt 
Capital City
 and forced to mine for the rest of his life with no chance of escape. 

This was until Parrot came along and joined the mining civilization and managed to free everybody there. Ever since that day, Lettuce tried to fulfill his promise and created the group known as 
The Law
, hunting players that were corrupt, but was still undermined, and needed to have more power and become the king of the server. This lead to Lettuce sending a thousand players to their death against 
FlameFrags
 and 
Wemmbu
, he used the leverage of the aftermath to run an election and rig it for himself. 

Parrot, who saved Lettuce in the past, saw right through his lies and rivaled him against the competition. Throughout the entire election, Lettuce had blackmailed, framed, and attempted an assassination on Parrot, and after realizing Lettuce would've lost the election due to the second debate, he declared himself the first king of Unstable. Parrot ends off his dialogue by saying Lettuce had become what he swore once to destroy.

Lettuce, his right-hand man 
Deputy_Ace
, and eleven other lawmen take Parrot to prison on their happy ghasts. Parrot dislikes the idea of having to sit around and watch Lettuce become the king although he hadn't won the election and calls out Lettuce for power grabbing. Lettuce says he's scared of what would happen if he didn't win and says it's nothing personal. Parrot reminds Lettuce that they used to be friends back in the mining city, Lettuce says he hadn't forgotten and he wanted to be like Parrot when he freed him and hated that Parrot was his opponent. 

They make it to Law HQ and Lettuce brings Parrot down to the prison. Parrot says he's making a mistake, Lettuce says he's doing what needs to be done. Parrot asks what if he doesn't comply, and Lettuce says he would kill him, but doesn't want to and to not make it difficult with a saddened voice. 

Parrot goes ahead and follows, an inventory check is conducted and he gets cleared. Lettuce starts leading him to his cell and gives him a set of curse of binding copper armor as per protocol due to Wemmbu's previous escape attempts. After getting put into the solitary confinement cell, Lettuce tells Parrot if he ever wants to see 
Theo
 again, he'd have to admit that he was wrong about everything and to endorse him. Parrot asks if he's stupid for thinking Parrot would do any of that after his best friend got kidnapped and got sent to prison. Lettuce says no and knows eventually he'd do so.

Lettuce and Ace leaves, a new commander named BenjaminTheI keeps watch of Parrot. Soon later, Ace returns and brings 
Th3Blurpenstein
 into solitary and tells Benjamin to look out for them. Parrot and Blurpenstein talk to each other and Blurpenstein reveals that Wemmbu had escaped this exact prison, Parrot asks how Wemmbu did so, Blurpenstein explains that the lights went out and a riot begun. Benjamin blocks off the iron bars and door so the two can't see each other anymore. Parrot thinks to himself no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't do anything as Lettuce became the king of the server. 

All of a sudden, the lights go out and a secret door opens up behind Parrot and a shulker box full of gear is placed right at the door. Parrot blows up the doors that requires keycards and runs out of the Law HQ. Parrot realizes that thousands of lawmen roam around the server and the potential of stumbling across one was very high. A lawman spots him and sees that Parrot was the escapee and says he's gonna snitch on Parrot. Parrot hits him back to knock him away and run the opposite direction, Parrot grabs a piece of cactus and intends to use it to break his armor. 

He isn't able to take on the fight with his copper armor and the lawman did eight hearts of damage per hit. Parrot gets multiple hits on the lawman and eventually gets all of his armor broken, he equips all of his diamond armor and locks in and demands where Theo is. The lawman tells Parrot to stop and tells him that Theo is at Smuggler's Pass. Parrot asks why he should believe him and the lawman describes the terrain of Smuggler's Pass and says he saw Theo there himself. As Parrot's heading off, the lawman says there's no chance he could free Theo as he'd have to be a smart player to do so and that he would report Parrot to Lettuce. Parrot charges up his crossbow and uses his flint and steel and TNT minecart to blow the lawman up instantly.

Parrot then walks all the way to the Smuggler's Pass and splashes invis. He goes down a tunnel inside of the mountain and sees a massive castle built inside of it. He sneaks his way into the castle without being seen and sees Theo standing in the middle surrounded by lava at the very bottom. Parrot splashes fire resistance and climbs up a lava flow. He then uses a punch bow to launch himself onto a manmade stalactite and bridges over to the platform, clutches with water, and splashes both of them with invis and tells Theo to hide. The two lawmen keeping an eye out bridged over to the platform and they use the time to cross over and escape. 

They catch up on what happened over the past few days, Theo says Parrot could not endorse him and they need a plan, Theo says Lettuce made him set another stasis chamber and gets teleported as soon as he says that. Lettuce comes seconds after and says he wants to prove to Parrot that his friend's life is always in his hands and reminds Parrot if he wants to free Theo, he needs his endorsement and that he can't get Parrot in the way. Parrot says his whole plan was to keep his friend hostage until he gets endorsed and asks Lettuce what kind of king he is for doing all of this. Lettuce says the only one that's fit to rule and one that's willing to do whatever it takes. 

Lettuce says he's going to hold an announcement at the 
Northern Council
 in two hours and expects Parrot to endorse him there, and if he doesn't, a lot of people would get hurt, and if he tries to fight back, the server would be too divided. Parrot says it won't happen after he got sent to prison, the election got held off, and his best friend got kidnapped. Parrot angrily demands Lettuce to give Theo back, Lettuce says it wouldn't happen, Parrot pots up and tries to attack Lettuce and he calls out his commanders to protect him. With no other choice, Parrot goes with Lettuce to the Northern Council.

Parrot and Lettuce ride a happy ghast all the way to the Northern Council, Lettuce reminds Parrot that him and Theo will die if he doesn't renounce his statements and that he's willing to offer them positions in his kingdom. The announcement starts, Lettuce first mentions that the election has been put on hold, but the remaining candidates had something to share. 
Boosfer
 goes first and says he was never interested in the election and asks for all his supporters to send his votes over to Lettuce. 
Purpled
 goes next and says the same thing as Boosfer and to have all his supporters to move their votes to Lettuce.  

It gets to Parrot's turn, he says the last time he was up on stage he called Lettuce a hypocrite and now wants to tell the server that it's still the case and will not be endorsing him after he kidnapped Theo and tells the crowd to not make Lettuce their king. Lettuce tells Parrot to follow him into the castle and asks him who he thinks he is for saying that, Parrot says he wouldn't endorse him no matter what and that Lettuce had the audacity to bring Parrot up on stage. 

Lettuce thought Parrot would come to his senses and gave him so many chances, but this was Parrot's last and makes Parrot follow him. Lettuce, Ace, and a dozen lawmen bring Parrot back to the mining city where Theo is being held hostage by two Law elite guards. Lettuce says they were there and forced to mine and never have the chance to escape and thinks Parrot regrets ever helping Lettuce out back then, Parrot says he doesn't regret it at all and that he saw a kind person and not the person that's standing in front of him now. Lettuce says he can't do it himself and makes Ace and the two elite guards the ones to kill them, him and the rest of the lawmen go on the other side of the island and blows the horn signaling Ace to kill Parrot and Theo. Parrot says this it it and says it was fun playing on the server with Theo. 

The camera goes off of Parrot and looks up, two hits are heard and the wither sound effect plays twice, hinting that two players died. It pans back down and Parrot is standing there. Parrot is shocked that Ace killed off the two guards and he says they have to go now. Lettuce comes back and maces Parrot and asks what Ace is doing, Parrot says there's a mine somewhere and goes down into it and covers it up. With the little time they have, Ace reveals that Lettuce had ignored everything he said for too long and that he was the one that freed Parrot out of the prison. Ace gives them his pickaxe and says he was going to hold them off and tells Parrot that he'd be a great king. 

Lettuce confronts Ace, Ace says what he's doing is wrong and what Parrot and Theo stand for is better than what Lettuce is doing. The last words Ace says before he gets killed off is "I'd rather die standing than live kneeling." They dig out of mining city and Parrot builds a tombstone for Ace. Parrot says they finally need to defeat Lettuce then says they need to return back to 
District 13
 to prepare. 

At the entrance of the District 13, Parrot calls out 
Horace
 and 
Fymada
 for following them. All four of them walk in and see a group of players who used this base as their town, Parrot goes on to explain to them about the election and Lettuce and if nobody was going to step up, Lettuce would be the permanent king of Unstable.  

The people see that Parrot was the one that stood up against Lettuce and agrees to help them out. They start setting up their plan, Horace would stay back with the others to rebuild the base as Parrot, Theo, and Fymada went out to recruit more players. Parrot and Theo tasked themselves to find 
Flame
 and 
Wemmbu
 each while Fymada recruited any players that she knew that were interested. Parrot recruited a few random players who were interested, but knew Wemmbu was practically impossible to find at this rate, so after a full day of looking, he returns back to District 13. Parrot sees the new revamped base and sees that Theo hadn't come back, so he teaches the new players how to PvP. 

After two days full of practice, Theo comes back and Horace reveals that he knew a Law stash full of loot and so Parrot and Theo go out to get it. They park their happy ghast at an island nearby and swims over to the Law stash where lawmen still guard it. They sneak into it and grab a few armor sets and Parrot writes on a sign to keep looking. Parrot sees a room being guarded by two lawmen and sees that it's a room that has chests full of potions. Parrot mines through the stone, but Theo coughs unmuted and alerts the lawmen that they're there. 

Theo says he only brought four minecarts to fight and that there's more in his ender chest, but is unable to get them since he gave his pickaxe to Parrot. The two go back to the island with their happy ghast and fly up, the lawmen begin to build up to them, Parrot tries to bow them off and kill them with fall damage, but the water saves them. Parrot uses his spyglass to look down and sees an invisible player with a trident and thinks it's 
Spoke
. Spoke summons multiple withers and blows up tons of lawmen, Parrot and Theo jump down to help him. Parrot asks what Spoke was doing there, Spoke says there's been too much that's happened since the last time they talked.  

They deal with the last few remaining lawmen and Spoke says that 
BAT
 had a revolution and now 
JumperWho
 and her spies are sided with Spoke and that he'd been searching for Parrot to combine forces. They loot more of the chests and Spoke explains that they fought a huge army at their prison and expected them to come to the Northern Council, but instead they were heading over to where they are now. The next wave of lawmen show up and get onto the island, at the same time, Wemmbu comes flying towards the castle and sets off an orbital strike cannon onto the castle. There are too many lawmen for all of them to deal at once, and Flame comes right after. It's revealed that Spoke had already recruited Flame and Wemmbu apart of their army. 

Parrot, Theo, Spoke, and Flame all ride back to District 13, but Spoke says the happy ghast is too slow, so he makes Theo fly back alone and the other three go back to the castle where Spoke has the rest of his army to go get them. At the castle,

,

 are all there and welcome them in. They then all go back to District 13 and gears Parrot's army up. Parrot and Spoke step up and have their speech, Parrot starts off by saying they have all been affected by Lettuce and his army someway and somehow. Lettuce had hunted, blackmailed, and tried to kill all of them, all because true justice is served by doing whatever it took. 

Parrot says they're going to storm into the Northern Council and show Lettuce wrong. By the time they start heading off, Lettuce knew that they had all combined forces and gathered up all of Law to stop them. They step onto the Northern Council, Parrot steps up and has a talk with Lettuce first before heading head first. Parrot says Lettuce probably wished he banned him off, Lettuce asks what he thinks he's doing and that all of the server's problems are standing right behind Parrot and everyone that he's sacrificed would had been all for nothing and says Parrot is wrong for thinking Lettuce would let the world take everything from him again.

Lettuce gives Parrot one last chance to turn back around, Parrot says it's too late to surrender and everybody behind him has a reason to fight back. The two wish each other good luck and backs up to their team, Wemmbu immediately sets off three orbitals and kills hundreds of lawmen in those shots. Parrot thinks Lettuce is stupid for thinking that this army was able to take down his army after wiping out a majority of the lawmen in a short amount of time. They all realize that neither Lettuce or the commanders were at the fight and go up to where Lettuce and his commanders were. Parrot says Lettuce let all of his people to die and ran like a coward, Lettuce reminds them again he isn't stupid and makes them turn around and hundreds of Lettuce's netherite lawmen come out of hiding and a second fight begins.

Parrot knows he's not good enough to kill the players off individually, and goes off to be a distraction for the better PvPers of the army to get the kills. The perspective switches over to Theo's, who blows up multiple lawmen in one minecart each time. Back over to Parrot, he towers up and places his shulker boxes full of gear, Wemmbu flies over to regear as well, and Lettuce comes flying in and maces them off. Parrot starts to run out of resources and needs a way back onto the tower to regear, but it's practically impossible with how many lawmen are on him at once. Parrot goes onto the side of the Northern Council and builds up on the back of the election board to get to his shulkers. It switches back over to Theo again, who's shown to be destroying the lawmen once more.

After an hour of fighting, it was only Lettuce and a few remaining lawmen left, all of Parrot's army target Lettuce until it's down to his chestplate being the only armor piece remaining, he then flies out of the fight. They count who's there and see if anyone died, Wemmbu and Minute come back and says Lettuce managed to escape. They're all finally able to rest from all of the fighting and Parrot walks around the Northern Council just looking at the ground. Lettuce is then shown walking back through the front of the Northern Council. Parrot asks Lettuce what he's doing there and Lettuce says if they really want to kill him, to do it and that he doesn't want to live on a server that doesn't want him, and that his goal was to enforce the will of the server, but if the server doesn't want justice, he surrenders. Parrot makes Lettuce follow him, but some people like Flame wants to kill him right there.

They all decide a vote to see who wants Lettuce dead and it ends in a tie. Flame asks why Parrot wants to keep Lettuce alive, Parrot says if they kill him, they're no better than him. Parrot says he will resume the election that Lettuce started and if Lettuce somehow manages to win, then that's the server's decision. Lettuce says he knew he wasn't going to win ever since Parrot's execution failed and says the server was too far gone now and he tried to warn Parrot. 

Parrot tells Lettuce that he's going to prison for a long time. Parrot looks at the warzone that they had caused, Wemmbu and 
eggchan

The next scene shows Parrot with 5,828 votes and Lettuce has 3,973 votes. Parrot now wears a crown on his skin, him and Theo are inside of the castle ready to walk out, Theo asks what Parrot will do as king, Parrot says he doesn't know but he has some things planned. Parrot heads up and Theo tells him good luck and that he's proud of him, but not to let it get to his head since he's still not the biggest bird. Parrot steps up in front of the crowd as the episode ends.
