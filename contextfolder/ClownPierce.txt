ClownPierce
, also known as 
Clown
, is one of the two main antagonists (alongside 
Ashswagg
) of 
Unstable Universe
 
Season 1
. He is one of the secondary antagonists of Unstable Universe Season 2 alongside 
The Law
. He was allied with the Invisible Mafia.

Personality

ClownPierce has a reputation of being both ruthless and dangerous. He takes extreme measures to achieve his goals, such as relentlessly hunting down 
ParrotX2
 with the help of his team during the events of 
I Got Hunted by Minecraft's Deadliest Players
. He enjoys entertainment and often torments his targets with grand entrances or complex games, social experiments, and scavenger hunts. The fear and terror he inflicts onto the server have caused him to gain some notable enemies, with some people even going as far as creating entire alliances based on killing Clown. 

He is also known to have one weak spot, that being 
Branzy
. He is also close friends with FerreMC, they care about each other’s life, but they don’t usually have to worry about each other because both Ferre and Clown are very skilled players.

Season 2

We got mentioned about Clown first that it is known that he executed 
Verda
 and citizens of "mining town" because they resisted to tell where the 
Verdaria
 was.

It is rumored that ClownPierce claimed the entire Nether dimension as 
his territory
. He constructed a massive, highly-secured vault to store his wealth and began hunting any player who entered the dimension without permission. His goal was to create a monopoly over Netherite and other essential Nether resources.

ClownPierce’s growing monopoly eventually forced rivals into a desperate alliance. FlameFrags and Parrot (With 
TheobaldTheBird
) launched a high-stakes infiltration mission with dual objectives: FlameFrags wanted to make an assassination of ClownPierce in order to get information about Wemmbu, while Parrot aimed to breach and loot his secure vault. But, they had some kind of accident, and Flame betrays Parrot and Theo and tells Clown where their location is in exchange for Wemmbu's location.

They then went to location of the birds 
(Parrot and Theo)
 and while Parrot gets to escape, Theo is being captured. Parrot jumped down attempting to negotiate, but in result got captured also.

Clown apologizes and tells Flame that he knows nothing about Wemmbu location and proceeds to execute Parrot, but Flame accepts it and tells Clown that they'd have to get through him first, betraying him in result. Clown runs away to catch Parrot so his vault isn't ransacked leaving his army behind.

Some time later, Clown is met by Parrot, who came to request an order of 40,000 TNT to use to defend his kingdom from 
Cindercrest
. Clown, still holding a grudge against Parrot for raiding his vault, charges three times the price (60,000 diamonds up from 20,000). Parrot initially declines the offer, however Clown tells him that Cindercrest have already departed towards his kingdom, revealing that Cindercrest purchased goods from Clown prior to their conversation, and says they would stand no chance without potions, technology, or gear. Parrot reluctantly agrees to the deal and pays Clown the diamonds. Clown tells Parrot before he leaves that he is welcome to come back to him whenever he needs to buy more things, however warns him that if he comes back for any reason other than that, he would kill him.
