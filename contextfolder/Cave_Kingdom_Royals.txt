The Royals of the Cave Kingdom are the highest ranking members of the 
Kingdom of the Caves
 (excluding 
Arachn1d
). They have the access to netherite armour and any other valuable materials they might have.

History

Whilst they first appear in the death chat in 
ParrotX2's
 
Unstable SMP: Civil War
, their first chronological appearance is in 
1000 Players Simulate UNDERGROUND Civilization in Minecraft
, where they are seen at the tavern. One of them, 
Trixxio
, attacks 
Wemmbu
. The fight is interrupted.

They participate in multiple battles, in one of which 
Yungyx
 accidentally blows up one of them (it is worth noting Yungyx is also a Royal).

They are seen in multiple of Arachn1d's meetings.

In Arachn1d's Purge, they are the only ones allowed to keep their armour. 

Trixxio is sent to guard the cave biome, more specifically the crafting table.
