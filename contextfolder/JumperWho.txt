Personality

Jumper is shown to be intelligent and tends to think critically like 
Parrot
. She cares a lot about her morals and will sometimes sacrifice others for them. This same motivation occasionally causes her to team with people she considers an enemy or to go behind her friend's backs multiple times. Overall, she is protective and kind, protecting anyone she believes to be innocent and being reluctant to do anything too hastily, such as how she refused to kill Spoke without proper evidence or to hunt down Parrot just because he 
might
 be the Director. She also cares deeply about her spies, even turning against her leaders who use them for their own gains.

Season 2

JumperWho joins Bounty Assassin Team.

JumperWho puts a chest and a sign saying to run in Parrot's room with two stacks of pearls, and an invisibility potion. BAT comes into Parrot's room and tries to kill him as they think he is 
The Director
.

JumperWho finds Parrot in an abandoned city. She tells Parrot that she was the one who put the chest and sign in his room. Parrot becomes a JumperWho spy to figure out how he had been framed for being The Director.

JumperWho gives Parrot the pieces of evidence that BAT thought showed he was The Director. 
Leow0ok

Parrot finds a book asking Parrot to meet up with him. Parrot spies on BAT's meeting. JumperWho betrays him, telling them that Parrot is spying on them. Parrot manages to escape during the chaos. JumperWho tells Parrot that she was actually just pretending to betray Parrot because BAT didn’t trust her. Parrot discovers that someone was spying on them. JumperWho uses a goat horn to call back her spy and kill the spy. 

BAT watches Parrot and The Director have a conversation, which convinces BAT that Parrot is not The Director. The Director teleports away. Leow0ok discovers that JumperWho killed a spy and kicks her off the team.

JumperWho rejoins BAT after saving Parrot from death to help hunt down the Director. JumperWho leads them to a secret base that she and her spies created.

JumperWho sends spies to secretly spy on suspects for the Director while the Director visits Parrot. The Director holds a chest plate with a red trim on it, causing Parrot to believe the Director is Spoke, as Spoke wears that netherite chest plate. Parrot convinces Leow0ok and Derapchu to pretend to be spies so Parrot can threaten to kill the spies so that JumperWho confesses to lying about none of the suspects being the Director. Parrot fails to do so.

Spoke found her and convinced her to give him the mace in the arena. She was later captured by Jamato and sent back to the prison.

Spoke found her again in the second arena event. She managed to build an army for Spoke, but later led the members to escape when they complained that Spoke was killing them and argued with Spoke about this. This was her final moments with Spoke before she vanishes.
