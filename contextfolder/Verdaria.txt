Verdaria
 was a civilization created by 
Verda
, 
MyraMoon
, and 
Luma317
 that acted as a safehaven for them and their treasures. Ever since word got spread about the existence of it, many players spent years trying to find it, but failed. One player, ClownPierce, threatened Verda to reveal the location of it, but refused to, so Clown killed Verda, leaving Verdaria to be some kind of rumor.

In the end, 
Parrot
 and 
Theo
 found the great treasure of Verdaria, they decided to gave it to poor people on the server.
