Ouroboroid
 is a character in 
Unstable Universe
. 

History

Ouroboroid had access to slime mobs, and hands them over to the trader RockyUnleashed39 while invisible.

Wemmbu
 attempts to converse with him and later kill him, but Ouroboroid has access to enchanted golden apples, wind charges, and a riptide trident. He is able to escape.

Wemmbu obtains a screenshot of Ouroboroid who had revealed a compass for a split second, and 
eggchan
 calculates where the compass leads to. 

Wemmbu finds Ouro's base and they get in a fight. Despite having many totems of undying, Ouroboroid is overpowered, and Wemmbu is able to negotiate with him and tell him about 
LettuceK
's plans, and explain that Lettuce plans to destroy the Pirate Civilization. 

Ouroboroid takes Wemmbu to the storage. LettuceK was able to invisibly sneak in and steal a few shulker boxes with music discs in them. Ouro immediately threw a wind charge to the self-destruct button, but it was too late.

Wemmbu attempts to distract LettuceK while Ouroboroid gets gear. LettuceK kills Ouroboroid. 

Trivia

Ouroboroid may be related to The_Oroboros, the soldier of the Law due to their similar names, and armor trims.

LettuceK tells Wemmbu and Ouroboroid "I got the better of the two of you", which may have a double meaning in this regard.
