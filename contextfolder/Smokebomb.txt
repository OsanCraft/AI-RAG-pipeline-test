Smokebomb_ is one of the finalists in 
Antaggg
's tournament. 

History

He participates in the tournament using his mace-cart strategy. He beats ArrowSpammerXX in his first fight and an unknown player in his second one. He is seen with Thunderbeast and ItzSharpen's group. He moves on to the semi-finals, where he easily beats 
Falconu
. 

In the finals, he teams up with Sharpen to go up against 
FlameFrags
 and Falconu. He starts off beating Flame, but when he pulls out his own carts, he starts getting continuously popped. Since Flame has blast prot, the damage dealt against Smokebomb is much higher and his helmet breaks. He surrenders the battle.
