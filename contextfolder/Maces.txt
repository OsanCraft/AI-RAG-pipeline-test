The 
mace
 is a weapon that deals more damage the farther the user falls before contact, making it extremely overpowered in fights. In the 
Unstable Universe
, maces are rare due to the 
Invisible Mafia
 destroying every ominous spawner reachable, except for those in the 
Far Lands
. Maces play a very powerful role in the Unstable SMP, a player owning one showcases immense power, often in the form of riches, influence, or combat prowess. All Diamond Trim members of the 
Invisible Mafia
 with the exception of 
FlameFrags
 owned a mace, but these maces have largely been destroyed and few remain. Most 
LAW
 Elites also owned maces.

Crucible

As an unnamed mace, Crucible originally belonged to the 
diamond level Mafia member
, 
Iqcniq
. He was killed by the combined efforts of Wemmbu, Eggchan, ParrotX2, and Wifies with Crucible being picked up by Wemmbu. It was briefly stolen by Jaden after 
Eggchan's Civilization
 collapsed but he was forced to give it back at the end of 
I Explored an Abandoned Minecraft Civilization
 by Wemmbu. 

It was stolen again by 
Sharpness
 in 
I Explored Beyond the Minecraft Farlands
 which led to Wemmbu chasing him into the 
Far Lands
. FlameFrags tricks Sharpness into giving him the mace, lying that he knew a duplication glitch. Flame keeps the mace and reveals he never knew any dupes before leaving. He later returns the mace to Wemmbu, stating he had no use for it. 

It was given to 
Eggchan
 by 
Wemmbu
 in 
I Got Hunted by the World's Deadliest Assassin
 after the fight of Wemmbu and the Invisible Player, so Eggchan would have a weapon to protect himself with. Eggchan was seen holding out the mace often during the rest of the episode. It is likely that the Invisible Player had obtained it from him after they took Eggchan hostage, but it is unknown what really happened. 

The mace has not been seen since. (presumably held by the Invisible Player.) 

Incinerator

This mace was given to Lomedy by FlameFrags, after Flame defeated the ex-diamond-trim mafia member, 
VEN0M
. It was given back to Flame during the 
The Great Farm War
 so that he could defend Lomedy's farm. It was used by Flame throughout his Training Arc and was named 
Incinerator
 during this time. 

It was used by Flame to defeat thousands of LAW men. In 
2500 Players VS Minecraft Combat Master
, Flame chooses to give Lomedy Incinerator back as a part of his apology to the farmer. While Flame is leaving, Lomedy uses Incinerator to mace Flame and pops his totem, saying it was his payback and forgives Flame. 

Lomedy gives Flame Incinerator in 
I Became a Minecraft Combat Master
 so that he can defend their new base against 
Arachn1d
 and his men. It is given back to Lomedy after the fight. 

Executioner

Executioner originally belonged to the LAW soldier, 
Venthatguy
, who fought 
Wemmbu
 and 
FlameFrags
 with it in 
2 Minecraft Pros VS 1000 Players
. After Flame and Wemmbu kill him, Wemmbu picks up the mace and uses it against Flame after the latter initiates a duel. 

Wemmbu drops the mace down a stab shot hole as he has laid down the burden to compete with Flame after yielding to Flame and reflecting over their past fights. Unknowingly, 
LettuceK
 shows up and reveals he's been watching them fight, then he swoops down to pick up the mace Wemmbu dropped. LettuceK named the mace 
Executioner
 some time between this and the final battle. Its current status is unknown, for LettuceK was without it when he surrendered. 

Jaden_MAN's Mace

This unnamed mace originally belonged to Lomedy, but was stolen from him by Jaden after they were attacked while trying to build Flame a new base. Wemmbu stole it from Jaden during the 
Invisible Knight
 arc, but it was given back to Jaden in the same episode. 

Flame makes it his goal to retrieve this mace in 
2500 Players VS Minecraft Combat Master
 because he believed it'd repair his and Lomedy's relationship. Flame is unable to retrieve it due to Wemmbu protecting Jaden.  

FieldsOfElysium's Mace

This mace originally belonged to an unnamed player who protected the 
Kingdom of Wheat
. After retiring, the mace was left in a tomb with only one person knowing the way to it, SirLordWessley. Seeking protection, 
FieldsOfElysium
 asks 
Wemmbu
 to retrieve this mace. The mace is stolen by a invisible player who Wemmbu fights and successfully corners them into dropping it. Wemmbu decides to return the mace to FieldsOfElysium instead of keeping it for himself. It is unknown what happened to the civilization as it was destroyed and FieldsOfElysium was nowhere to be found.

Destroyed Maces

Wemmbu owned a mace which he crafted. After it was given to Mapicc, he burned it. The mace had no enchants.

TruOriginal
's mace was destroyed by an orbital strike cannon by BAT.

FlameFrags burned a mace that belonged to LAW soldier Weesean19x before re-matching Wemmbu.

317 maces were in double chests in the Far Lands. The maces were burned by Wemmbu despite Eggchan's attempts to snag one for himself. All of them were unenchanted.
