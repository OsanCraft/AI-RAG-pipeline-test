Fymada
 is a supporting character in 
Unstable Universe
. She was also formerly the Mayor of 
Capitol City
.

History

Fymada was a citizen of 
Capital City
. She was first seen having to pay off taxes to 
Reddoons
 and didn't have enough diamonds to pay. After Reddoons left her, 
Parrot
 and 
Theo
 secretly gave her enough diamonds to pay her taxes. After Reddoons cleared all taxes, she, as well as the rest of Capital City thanked Parrot and Theo.

Fymada ran for ruler of 
Unstable SMP
. She won the debate against 
SirPig_
 and said that she will lower the taxes in Capital City which got her many votes.

In round 2 of voting, Fymada was eliminated from the election after she befriended 
Parrot
 and 
Horace
 because 
Arachn1d
 stole some of her votes.

She helped fight the 
The Law
 alongside 
District 13
 she gets a few kills and votes to spare Lettuce.

In Parrot's first 100 days as King, she was declared Mayor of Capitol City (originally Capital City). She also saves Parrot and Theo from the withers at spawn a long with Horace Altman.

During the events of 
I Exposed a Corrupt Minecraft Civilization
, Fymada felt incredibly disappointed in what she believed was her ability to rule Capitol City with the police killing every new player who tried to join Capitol City. Eventually, Parrot convinced Fymada to help him prove his innocence. She did this by getting a bunch of players to overhear the Capitol City police confess to their crimes.

She appeared to be a commander of 
Royal Army
, leading 
Capitol City Police Force
.

She was possibly ousted after 
Saparata
 won the Civil War, as 
Cindercrest
 were shown to kill players in Capitol City.
