The 
Warriors Council
 is a group of players in charge of leading the 
Warriors District
 in the 
Far Lands
. It is made of 5 elite warriors, and in order to become a council member, a player must defeat an existing council member or pop the totem of the Leader. Players on the council are distinguished by their shields, which are white with a black skull on it, and by their silence armor trims.

History

One of the mission leaders die to a diamond tier Mafia 
player
.

Parrot and 
TheobaldTheBird
 have a competition for who becomes the next mission leader. TheobaldTheBird wins using TNT minecarts.

Parrot agrees to fight 
DumbIsDumb
 to become a council member. By flooding the arena with water and using tridents, Parrot is able to win.

Purpled
 tries to have the council members kill Parrot, but he escapes. Parrot becomes the new leader because Purpled is outvoted by the council members. Parrot unites the warriors and the farmers, and tells everybody to throw their diamond trimmed armor into a cactus and replace it with normal netherite armor.

Ashswagg takes the warriors hostage. The farmers find the prison and break everyone out. The Mafia destroys the Far Lands Civilization and the Warriors Council ends indefinitely.
