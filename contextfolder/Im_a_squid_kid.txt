im_a_squid_kid
, also known as 
Squid
, is a minor character featured in 
Unstable Universe
. He was a member of the 
Sleepy Nation
.

Trivia

im_a_squid_kid
 is most famous for fighting 
Technoblade
 in the 
Great Potato War
 in 
Hypixel SkyBlock
, in which the two competed initially for #1 in the game's Potato collection, and later for the first player to reach 500,000,000 potatoes collected.

He is also notable for his series in which he tries to collect Dyes in 
Hypixel SkyBlock
, alongside being #1 in the game's Raw Porkchop collection at one point.

His Private Island is among the most visited in the game, where players can see his paintings.

im_a_squid_kid
 has confirmed in a Twitch stream that he died off-screen during the destruction of the Sleepy Nation.
