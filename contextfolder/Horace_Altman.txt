Personality

Horace is hardworking and relatively friendly. He is an incredibly talented builder and built 
Wemmbu's Civilization
, 
The End Civilization
, 
Capital City
, 
Skyblock Civilization
, 
District 13
 and 
Eggchan's Civilization
. He has a deadpan sense of humor and often tries to stay out of conflict due to him being a builder and not PvP oriented. 

He also takes a lot of pride in his building, and is very upset when his civilizations get destroyed. He holds a grudge against 
the Invisible Mafia
, claiming he knows them very well because they keep destroying his builds. He also holds a slight grudge against 
Parrot
, when he remarks several times about the fact Parrot promised that the End Civilization would not be destroyed, which turned out to be false.  He has been an incredibly helpful player at several points in the story and is in general very dedicated and generous. Unfortunately he joined the Invisible Mafia due to these reasons, after helping Eggchan in the Egg Civilization.
