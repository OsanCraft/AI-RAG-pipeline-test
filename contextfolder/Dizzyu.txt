dizzyu
 is a character seen in 
Unstable Universe
. 

History

dizzyu is seen as a miner in 
Capital City
.

Later on, he is the leader of a group of players hired by the 
Invisible Player
 to kill 
Wemmbu
 and 
eggchan
. He sends some bandits after them to kill them in 
Highwater City
 but fails. Egg eventually figures out their banner pattern and leads them to his headquarters. Wemmbu feigns a surrender but eventually is given gear by Egg to kill them. Dizzy is eventually forced to surrender and they interrogate him and discuss about using him as bait until the Invisible Player shows up and gives him room to run.
