History

He was one of the many players trying to enter the Tomb of the Mafia. He was waiting whilst invisible for 
Marlowww

ParrotX2
, who was also hiding nearby, thought was her addressing him. She then proceeds to try to mace Nightv0ltage9. He was able to survive for a while, but eventually fell victim to her mace.
