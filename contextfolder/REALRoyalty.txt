REALRoyalty__
 is a player on the Unstable SMP. 

They were first seen in 
ParrotX2
's video, 
100 Days as King of the Unstable SMP
 as a player at the auction. They were buying basically everything there, even buying an enchanted book for 800 diamonds. When bidding for a chicken started, they were bidding enthusiastically until Parrot bid 500. They went to 501, which was a sign that they were most probably running out of money after spending so much on the auction. When Parrot asked him to stop bidding, and that his kingship was at a risk if he didn't get the chicken, he proceeded to bid 1000, 100 over the amount Parrot and 
TheobaldTheBird
 had. Theo, in a panic, accidentally bid 100,000 diamonds instead of 1,100. REALRoyalty stated that he needed the chicken, and was dissapointed by the loss.
