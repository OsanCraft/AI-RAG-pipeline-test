Personality

Spoke has a very chaotic and manipulative personality. He often ignores the needs and feelings of others in exchange for what he wants; showcasing a very dedicated and determined side that often leads to amoral decisions. His egotistical nature is best reflected when he chooses to make excuses for, underplay, or deny his wrongdoings instead of admitting them, often lying even to his 
best friend
 about his actions. An example of this is when he killed all the witnesses, made an enormous lie and went all out to hunt down 
Leow0ok
 just to cover his accidental burning down of a single village. 

He is a very good speaker and has great social skills, being the only leader of 
an empire
 that did not promise anything to its members, as well as the one who gathered and lead the protagonists to defeat 
the LAW
. Despite this, he is continuously avoided by other players due to his reputation as an untrustworthy and dangerous individual, his enigmatic nature, as well as his willingness to take extreme measures to get things done (like ruthlessly killing players that do not join his army in 
Unstable SMP: Purgatory
). He is currently the leader of 
NULL
.

Spoke, when idle, is mischievous and enjoys pranking others, including tricking 
Parrot
 & 
Wifies
 to uncover a secret and stealing items from 
Reddoons
. He can be frivolous and flippant even when others are deadly serious; claiming to be 
the Director
, playfully saying "boom boom boom", and pulling off a TNT cart trap whilst being hunted by 
B. A. T.
 in their void base. He enjoys laughing over silly things like the moonwalking spy. He genuinely cares about his friends, in particular 
Mapicc
. When threatened with losing Mapicc (or any of his close friends), he becomes much more desperate, impulsive, and stubborn; threatening to blow up the entirety of 
Capitol City
 and refusing to believe Mapicc doesn't want to be his friend anymore even when Mapicc tries to kill him several times over. 

He can be reckless and self-endangering. For example: he often builds nether portals despite knowing the Nether is under 
ClownPeirce's
 control. Despite this, he is quite intelligent, being the only person in 
Purgatory
 smart enough to outsmart Jamato, and is shown to have an extensive mental library of traps and escape methods. He was able to hold up a title of being "The Player Impossible to Catch" for a very long time prior losing the name, his 
chestplate
, all his items, Mapicc, and 
JumperWho
 to JamatoP because of his obsession with killing his 
old friend
, revealing Jamato's identity, and keeping his own secrets safe.  

It is unclear whether he will redeem himself or go down a dark path, especially after he became leader of NULL, as in doing so he seems to have gotten more egotistical and callous—being willing to kill his own best friend who he had fought so hard to keep just so he could take control of NULL and violently knocking the 
NULL commander
 away from the Totem of NULL to be sure the commander didn't get it. Either way, he is currently presented as an anti-hero.
