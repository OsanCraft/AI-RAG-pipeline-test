Personality

Reina is very smart (and has great depth perception), as shown by her winning a 1v1 with Ralamus by dropping a piece of dripstone on him; and the clues left by Zoe, which used complex ciphers she was intelligent enough to easily understand. She is rather insecure and is quiet; 
Theobald
 has expressed that she rarely says more than three or so words at once. Sometimes she acts a bit cocky which annoys her allies (like Theo), but overall, she cares about her friends. She has a vengeful side, and desires to kill every last member of 
Cindercrest
.  

History

Reina joined Unstable and nearly starves to death before 
Zoe444
 kindly gives her food. They join 
Skymore
 together. 

Reina was one of the 10 survivors of the 1000 player civilization that was massacred by 
Cindercrest
. She and Zoe were taken to grow crops for Cindercrest. They eventually escaped, but were split up. Reina was found in Skymore by 
ParrotX2
 and TheoBaldTheBird, who gave her the news that Zoe had died. She then decided to join Parrot and Theo's quest to battle Cindercrest.

Reina won over the strongest bandit by using a trident given by Parrot to pierce dripstone and skewer her opponent. She helped threaten the leader of Merchant City, 
DanchoGold
. She and Theo went to spy on 
Cindercrest
. She acted determined, confident and cautious, just like a clone of Parrot.

Reina controlled the 
orbital arrow cannons
 to defend against 
Cindercrest
, which killed the majority of 
Cindercrest
 members over two battles. 

She was captured by 
Saparata
 and 
ShoeBilly_
 while distributing food. Parrot convinced her to not just let herself die by reminding her of her time on the server. 
Saparata
 likely banished her after he won the Civil War and became the second King of 
Unstable SMP
.
