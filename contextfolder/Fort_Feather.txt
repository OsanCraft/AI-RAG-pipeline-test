Fort Feather
 was a military base created by 
ParrotX2
 to defend his kingdom from 
Cindercrest
. The initial 
army
 of players was gathered here, in order to train for the civil war against Cindercrest. The fort was the site of the first battle of 
The Unstable SMP Civil War
, and was self-destructed during this battle by Parrot using TNT in an attempt to kill Cindercrest soldiers, after being conquered by Cindercrest's 
blimps
.

King Parrot made escape tunnels for his army to retreat in however he underestimated the strength of Cindercrest. Cindercrest used their blimp to get over the fortress and Cindercrest soldiers fell below. Without any defence to aerial attack the fort was overrun. King Parrot attempted to blow it up to kill Cindercrest soldiers but the resulting explosion only slowed down Cindercrest's advance and did not take down many Cindercrest players, leading to a major defeat.
