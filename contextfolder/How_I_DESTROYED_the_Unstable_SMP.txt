How I DESTROYED the Unstable SMP
 is the 43rd episode featured in 
Unstable Universe
. 

Plot

The video begins with 
Wemmbu
 looting a trial chamber until 
loppezz
 calls him. Wemmbu gets startled and maces her until he realizes it's loppezz. Loppezz explains to Wemmbu that a team took over her ice mountain and she wanted him to help her get it back. Wemmbu gets into the ice mountain and attacks members of Fantst's team. Fantst says that if he keeps trying to kill his teammate he will blow up the ice mountain. Loppezz tells Wemmbu to stop because she tells him that they're not lying. 

Wemmbu and loppezz enter the ice mountain again but invisible. 
Fantst's
 teammate, 
homunculus1031
 spots them and puts them inside a cell. Wemmbu and loppezz annoy Fantst's teammate to leaving. Wemmbu tells loppezz that he cannot solve any problems without killing anyone. Loppezz insists that they try to defuse the bomb by putting water all over the insides of the base. While they are doing so homunculus spots them so they leave.

Wemmbu sneaks in to the ice base until a player spots him so he kills them. Homunculus gets Fantst to stop being afk and informs him that Wemmbu is back in the base. Fantst and Homunculus both attack Wemmbu and Wemmbu almost kill both of them until Fantst blows up the ice mountain base. Loppezz watches the explosion and feels helpless. Wemmbu kills Homunculus and right as he's about to kill Fantst he teleports away using a statis chamber. Wemmbu goes back to loppezz and gives her a full set of netherite armour before leaving.
