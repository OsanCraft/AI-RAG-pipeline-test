Pyxii
 is a minor character on the 
Unstable SMP
. 

She is the sole survivor of the 
Invisible Mafia
 massacre while a part of the 
Warriors District
 in the 
Farlands
. Following this, Pyxii distanced herself from organized factions before later reappearing within 
The Law
. 

She later ran for King on the server, where she won against 
Fwendly
, but lost in the first round. 

Personality

Pyxii is characterized by a generally composed presence, maintaining the same tone across high conflict and low conflict situations. Her dialogue is typically concise, with occasional tuff remarks that are not expanded upon.

Trivia
