History

He accompanied Flame and 
NotSkrilley
 when they went to search for Lomedy. They got attacked, and Flow helped them fight off the 
Mist
 players. When Griffincoat6 forces Flame to train them, he goes to the place where Lomedy is hiding. He tells Flame not to train them so that Mistrul and the Mist don't kill them because they seem more of a threat.

He tells Flame that he killed Lomedy to get him enraged. He beats him with ease. After killing Mistrul and 60 other Mist players, Flame fights Flowtives and beats him, as he is in the zone. Flame let's him live so that he can live with the grief of losing his friend. Flow tries to explain to Flame that Mistrul was not the villain here. He says he was only the protector. Flame, however, does not listen.

Trivia

He is considered to be one of the best pvpers in the entire world, right now being 22nd on MCTiers, having a RHT1 and many T2 and T3. He is also known for cart PvP as RLT2.

In the comment section of the video Flow says "until we meet again, FlameFrags", hinting on him returning in a future video

Flowtives has played a few minor characters in Flame's older videos

He is the creator of FlowPVP, one of the primary replacements of MCTiers alongside MCPVP and MCLeagues
