History

Myra created Verdaria with Verda, and Luma to hide their treasure in. The trio decided to create a difficult treasure hunt to prevent the wrong people from finding it. Myra, along with her friends, then disappeared. Throughout the years, many players have tried to find their city, but all have failed until 
Parrot

Parrot and 
Theo

Jagle

ClownPierce
