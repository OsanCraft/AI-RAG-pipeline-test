The 
Nether Civilization
, also known as the 
ClownPierce Empire
 or 
ClownPierce Kingdom
, is a city located near the spawn area of the Nether dimension. It is a monarchy-based kingdom, with 
ClownPierce

FerreMC

These materials include netherite and required potion brewing ingredients like nether wart. They have made the Nether very dangerous, and anyone caught without proof of being in the civilization is killed.

Kingdom of Unstable (Overworld)

The Kingdom of Unstable (no name known) is an area of land under the control of the reigning king of Unstable, who's seat is in Northern Council. It encompasses a extremely large area and is roughly a square shape, bordered by LAW City to the south, Fort Feather to the east, Newtopia to the west, and Ironclad City to the north. The current ruler of Unstable is 
Saparata
, and the capital of Unstable is presumably Capital City.

Capital City
, led by 
Reddoons
 (and quoted by Ash to be 'basically his'), is a civilization in 
Unstable Universe
. The city is split into two parts: The Mines, and The City. The City is empty and it is dubious if The City was always a ruse for The Mines or citizens of Capital City were placed into The Mines prematurely to produce more diamonds.

Allyship

Most of Capital City's plot relevance is with it's allyship with 
The Invisible Mafia
. In 
I Infiltrated The Minecraft Mafia
, it is expanded upon that the allyship between Capital City and The Invisible Mafia is a one-way transactional relationship where Red will supply the Mafia with goods and the Mafia will remain neutral with Red.

In 
I Discovered a Minecraft Sky Civilization
, guards are shown to be stationed at Capital City, but in 
I Infiltrated The Minecraft Mafia
 they are gone, so it can be assumed that between videos, stations ended at Capital City, along with more interpersonal things like Red's position in the mafia.

The Mines

The Mines are a subsection of Capital City, being where most people in Capital City reside. It is alluded to be some kind of equivalent to a labor camp--minus it being prisoners placed in there; made to mine for valuable ores all day until nightfall. The Mines subsection was watched over 
CoinMonke
 as a warden.

The pitfall most workers found themselves in was the promise to be able to leave The Mines if they reach an absurd quota of diamonds, mostly being impossible without the help of Parrot stealing from Red's vaults. 

It gradually grew to also have a roulette table, following Red's gambling motifs, to kill one player after a week as a punishment for being the target of Red's suspicions.

The Mines are now seemingly defunct after I Survived Minecraft's Most Corrupt City.

History

ParrotX2
, 
Wifies
, and 
deanthebean9

They find a group of miners, with 
CoinMonke

PikaTM

Reddoons finds out about people getting set free and sees that his diamonds have been stolen. He triples the required quota. Using "the smugglers", Parrot and Wifies are able to get white wool. They break into one of the vaults using the wool, as there are sculk sensors detecting sound. They get in and steal the diamond blocks. Coming back to the mine, Reddoons catches them. Parrot and Wifies store all of the shulker boxes of diamonds in pots to not get caught with the items.

Reddoons figures out that they used the pots to store the diamonds. Reddoons makes it so whoever has the lowest amount of diamonds plays a game of roulette to decide if they live or not. Deanthebean9 and Parrot are tied for lowest. Parrot loses, but CoinMonke says he can’t kill him, as he doesn’t want to kill him. Parrot threatens to tell Reddoons that he didn’t do his job unless he helps them steal from Reddoons. They find a bunch of different locks in the vault, but luckily they are able to brute force through them by trying all combinations, and breaking the redstone. They realize they forgot to bring shulkers and leave. CoinMonke tells them Reddoons plans to lock down all of his vaults.

Parrot overhears Reddoons talking to CoinMonke. Reddoons mentions that

Capitol City 2.0, commonly known as Capitol City, is a city leaded by 
Fymada
, located near spawn. Centered a few thousand blocks from 
Spawn
, this metropolis is a safe space for all players. It is home to many shops, jobs, and houses. Hundreds of players live here—which formerly included 
ParrotX2
 and 
TheobaldTheBird
.

Structure

Capitol City is home to many shops and structures. The city takes a "Desert Empire" theme, being made out of a lot of sandstone and other yellow blocks. But this is not all, as there are some more mental structuring as well. The City has many rules, some are not that great or fair. For example, there is a 90% tax on all items sold. The currency is diamonds, so for every 100 diamonds you make, you will only get 10 
after
 taxes. Most of these goods are sold on "Market Street". This is also where you could have had found 
ParrotX2
, as he used to run a potion shop there as well. There are many different buildings and places in Capitol City as well, each having their importance. 

Main Building

 
 
 
 
 
Picture of the Building 
 
 

The Main Building is one of Capitol City's most important attractions. Built in the middle of the complex, this tower is huge, reaching over a hundred blocks in height. This is also where you will most likely find 
Reddoons
, the owner of the city. He does meetings and tax calculations on the top floor. For people who want to explore The Main Building, there are only two known ways of reaching higher floors. You can either climb the ladder to the top—which will take a while or use the secret mini elevators that are reserved for guards and important people, as seen in 
Wemmbu
's "
Orbital Strike Cannon vs Unstable SMP
". Either way, you still must get a reservation before climbing to the top, as 
Reddoons
 is almost always busy. 

Train Station

 
 
 
 
 
Picture of the Station 
 
 
Capitol City's Train Station is an important part of the civilization. It consists of 50 tracks each leading to separate stations all across the spawn area of 
Unstable
. It costs four diamonds per ticket now, due to inflation, which might not 
seem
 like a lot, but it is because of taxes. In total, with 90% taxes, it actually costs forty diamonds to get only four back. Anyhow, you buy a ticket from the front desk for that price and your good to go—it just might take a while to wait in line. On the bright side though, you can look around to find less popular front desks, i.e like the one that 
Lezy
 ran, which had no people in line for it. 

Grand Hotel

 
 
 
 
 
Picture of the Hotel
 
 

The Grand Hotel is not just for a temporary stay, it's also a business outpost of some kind. Although not much is known about it canon-wise, it is still an important part of the city, being the second largest building in the complex. It can house up to 800 players due to some simple math, although there have still been reports that stay is way too expensive—even from 
Theo
 himself, who was in major debt to the hotel, having to give up his elytra, which he later received back after "paying back" his debts. 

Post Office/Mail Building

 
 
 
 
 
Picture of the Mailing Complex
 
 

The Mail Building/Post Office is home to the city's mailing industry, located near Merchant Street. Here is where you pick up and drop off mail, and from there, the workers will take care of the rest, carefully sorting out and placing your deliveries where they are supposed to be. The line is long, just like the Train Station, but you can still have a faster waiting experience if you just look around for a shorter line. 

The Lionels

 
 
 
 
 
Picture of the Left Lionel
 
 

The Lionels are some of the most exotic attractions in the entire city. They resemble two huge lions, each on a short pillar. They have a powerful sense in them that suggests to the city's strong power.

The Far Lands City

Far Lands

Structure

To thrive, the Farlands was divided into 3 different districts; the 
Builders
, the 
Farmers
, and the 
Warriors
. Each district's objective was to fulfill their role to benefit everyone in their Civilization.

Builders

Builders were tasked to build for the Civilization.

Farmers

Farmers were tasked to provide resources for the Civilization.

Warriors

Warriors were tasked to defend the Civilization and were the main and biggest faction.

Sorting

A player was put into a test to decide which district they belong in. They were required to kill a wither with 5 items of their choice. The player would then be sorted into their faction depending on what approach they take.

If the player decided to fight the wither head on, they were most likely to be sorted into the Warriors.

If the player decided to hide from the wither until it's gone, they were most likely to be sorted into the Builders.

If the player decided to devise a way to trap the wither, they were most likely to be sorted into the Farmers.

The Wonders
 were five builds used to store glitched items and features. The builds were originally created by the Master Builders, and used by 
SpokeIsHere
 and 
JamatoP
 to store glitched features. Each wonder has a guard, who is not allowed to check inside of any wonders. 

List of The Wonders

The Gardens of Babylon

This massive temple is most likely based off of the Hanging Gardens of Babylon, an ancient structure destroyed by earthquake. This Wonder, placed in a savanna biome, with its gardens full of lush acacia trees and yellow flowers, was the first to be blown up by an orbital cannon. 

The Frosted Palace

The Frosted Palace is a huge ice castle built on a snowy hill surrounded by ice. This gargantuan build is filled with extraordinary details, scattered with large stone rocks, giant trees, and ice spikes. This wonder was the last to be destroyed, attempted to be saved by 
ParrotX2
, but was blown up nonetheless.

The Crone

The Crone is a massive stone statue standing on a plain completely surrounded by river. The cloaked woman holds a staff in her right hand and an unknown object in her left. This Wonder was the second to be blown up.

The Marshland Mansion

The Mansion is located in a swamp biome. This large overgrown manor, filled with trees, uses froglights to light up its mud and copper walls. The Marshland Mansion was the fourth Wonder to be destroyed.

The Pyramid of Anubis

The Pyramid of Anubis is an ancient Egyptian-styled pyramid dotted with hieroglyphics and contains a gold cap. Each side of the pyramid holds two large  statues of Anubis, the Egyptian god of funerary and care of the dead. This Wonder was the third one to be destroyed.
