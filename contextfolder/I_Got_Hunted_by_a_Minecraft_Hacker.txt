We were exploring an abandoned town when we were attacked by a Minecraft Hacker: a player who is able to fly, teleport, and do all sorts of other crazy unfair things. This is How I Got Hunted by a Minecraft Hacker.
— 
ParrotX2

I Got Hunted by a Minecraft Hacker
 is the 40th episode featured in 
Unstable Universe
.   

Plot

Parrot
, 
Nufuli
, and 
Leow0ok
 stumble upon an abandoned city that seems deserted by its residents. A player named 
TruOriginal
 appears. The three ask him what happened here, and he replies that he massacred the population because he was bored, and now he will kill them too. Leow0ok goes up to him for the first strike, but TruOriginal moves away quite quickly. His movements are not normal for a regular Minecraft character. TruOriginal is using hacks. To defeat him, they will need a cunning strategy. The leader of BAT pulls a fishing rod, which activates an orbital strike weapon. For a moment, the entire city is blown up by TNT, while the three stand in the completely untouched center. However, the opponent turns out to be above them, with flying hacks enabled. The battle moves into the water. The three swim, exchanging a helmet to breathe, while the hacker walks on water as if it were a solid block. Parrot spots an ocean monument. This is their ticket out of there. They go inside, blocking off the entrance and corridors, relying on the mining fatigue to slow down TruOriginal. They build a Nether portal and escape there with elytras.

Leowo0k, Nufuli, and Parrot find the massive BAT base in ruins. Derapchu and a player named 
Ciguato
 appear. They explain that some hacker came, killed everyone, and destroyed the base. Before the conversation starts, Ciguato shoots two creepers with a regular bow. But there's no way a regular bow can kill a creeper in one hit. When Parrot shares his thoughts, Ciguato's appearance changes to TruOriginal's, and his name does too. A fight begins, in which the hacker seems to retreat, and the three barricade themselves in the center of the grass field located in the middle of the base. But then TruOriginal digs up the blocks beneath them with one click – he uses cheats for breaking several blocks at once. The team ends up in a system of tunnels designed as a trap. However much they try, there is no way to escape – they can't get out via a tunnel or a pearl. The only way out seems to be a sacrifice – Leo is ready for this. Nufuli decides to join him, but the other two convince him to escape with them. Leow0ok's death message does not appear. Parrot, Nufuli, and Derapchu climb to the surface and establish themselves at a location pre-set as an emergency location. There they decide not to report the hacker to the server administrators because they suspect he has a connection to 
The Director
, and this way they can get more information about him.

Miraculously, Leow0ok arrives at the location. His armor is broken. The four build an underground base and cover it with lava so it won't be detected by x-ray. The next goal is to go to the trading 
Redstone Town
, to buy the materials needed to build a "polygon mirror receiver". 
This is an innovation in Minecraft redstone that can perform any function, provided it is activated - this happens only if the one who built it (and consequently threw a pearl inside) looks towards a specific angle set by them.

The shop owner 4CVIT asks for payment for the materials, but Parrot and Nufuli don't have diamonds. When Leo enters the shop, the owner doesn't hesitate to give him the components for the machine because the two are old friends who have built traps together in the past. On the way back to the base, they notice the hacker flying in circles above their perimeter. There is also a huge farm there. The four decide to hide there to blend in with the plants and thus confuse the opponent. On the walls, there are signs with the text "Do Not Enter". Despite this, the players enter, cover themselves among the lavenders there, but the property owners - two unarmed farmers - reveal their position by talking to them. Then TruOriginal lands and a battle begins.

Nufuli suggests trapping the opponent with cobwebs, but Leow0ok is skeptical. The battle doesn't go well, especially when TruOriginal uses cheats to clone himself. The confusion is absolute. The farmers' shouts accompany the action in annoying manner. Then the cobweb strategy turns out to be successful - the hacker's true figure is revealed. The team also uses TNT minecarts to break TruOriginal's first totem. However, the resources of the four also start to run out. TruOriginal declares he's leaving, simply flying off towards the horizon.

The team is too tired. The time is 2 AM. We learn that no one leaves the server because it wouldn't be possible to join again due to the huge number of players. At 5 AM, Parrot notices Nufuli talking in a monologue on the surface while guarding the base. The two pay attention to the only pair of twin stars in the sky.

In the morning, they go to the location where the orbital strike weapon is built. They build a polygon mirror receiver, through which it can be activated unnoticed. This task is entrusted to Nufuli. He sets the viewing angle to where the two twin stars will appear on the horizon. He must activate the system only when Leow0ok orders him to. The team goes to a location where TruOriginal will certainly notice them.

After a long wait, he appears. In the battle, the four manage to play it well, as if pretending to lose. "I am God, I am invincible" - TruOriginal behaves arrogantly. Parrot lures the hacker to the coordinates where the strike will pierce everything down to bedrock. Nufuli activates the weapon. Just before the explosion, however, the most impossible thing happens - TruOriginal swaps his position with Parrot's. Now the hero has 5 seconds to avoid certain death. Using two chorus fruit, he manages to escape. Now, however, they are unarmed against a hacker who can even slow down time. They use a trick to defeat him - they bluff that they have a second charged strike ready. TruOriginal retreats.

During the night, Parrot and Nufuli are on shift to guard the shelter. Because of Derapchu's loud snoring, they decide to guard on the surface. Without great surprise, TruOriginal finds them. The battle is relatively light. "Come, hit me, I turned off my kill aura" - the hacker tempts Parrot. The hit turns out to be a fatal mistake - Parrot's entire inventory is cleared, but for some unknown reason, TruOriginal simply flies thousands of blocks up. Then he swaps his position with Parrot. Everything seems lost. The hero is approaching the ground. In fractions of a second, a blurry figure of a flying horse appears in the air. Parrot manages to get onto the horse, which is ridden by Nufuli - he saved his friend's life. The two can't believe it. During the flight, they are not pursued. Nufuli says he wants to be like Leowo0k. Parrot gives the following lesson: 
"He became Leo because he acted like himself and made his own decisions. You spend so much time trying to be him that you forget who 
you
 are."

Parrot and Nufuli return to the base only to discover that the others are missing and there is a sign that reads "Meet me at BAT". They assume their friends are trapped. They gather TNT, make TNT minecarts, and place them under the entire base. Surprisingly, they meet Leow0ok. He doesn't answer most of their questions, which raises the suspicion that this is TruOriginal. Their suspicions are correct. The fight reaches the field at the BAT base. Parrot and Nufuli climb up with blocks and build an obsidian platform. The hacker pushes them, and they activate the trap. His arrogance knows no bounds: 
"The Director told me to keep you alive, but it has become boring now. I will kill you, I am above the Director! Anything you want to say?"
.

Nufuli immediately understands what Parrot means. He looks at the stars, more precisely at the only twin pair. The BAT base begins to disappear layer by layer. This is a strike from the orbital strike weapon. The falling TNT kills TruOriginal. The battle is won.

The two find their friends in the base's prison. They go to the front, where Nufuli declares that he wants to leave the team. 
"I cannot be my own version of myself. Don't get me wrong. We are friends, but sometimes I feel like I don't even know who I am."
 Leowo0k, tearfully, shows understanding. Nufuli says goodbye to the team and leaves.

At that same moment, however, a row of arrows pierce him fatally. This is a railgun. Behind them appears an invisible figure in netherite armor, colored in white trims - The Director. Leow0ok is angry: "You killed my best friend!". Parrot's fate had now befallen him.

"Here we meet again. I killed Nufuli because he stopped serving his purpose. Soon you and Derapchu will not be needed either," the Director states firmly.

"What do you want from us?" - the question is rhetorical.

"You know what I want from you, Parrot. You've known it from the start, but you are simply terrified of the truth. Now the story is coming to a close."

"Who are you?" - asks Leowo0k.

"You will find out who I am very, very soon. And when you do, all this will end!" - these are the Director's last words for the video, and they start to go after The Director to try and kill him.

Analysis

The moral of the story conflicts with the Season 2's motive - teamwork. Another philosophic problem regarding the series.

It is not clear whether The Director has intentions of killing Parrot - he says "this will end", which could refer just to the "game of cat and mouse". We learn from TruOriginal's words that The Director has told him to keep Parrot alive - another support for the upper statement.

Near the end of this video, TruOriginal states that he didn’t care that The Director said not to kill Parrot, and says that he will. Nufuli activates the orbital strike cannon, killing Tru before he got the chance to, essentially saving Parrot.
