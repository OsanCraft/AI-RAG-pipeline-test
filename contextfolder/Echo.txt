Echoes
 or 
Memories
 are strange, looping phenomenons that are found beyond the 
Far Lands
 and are prevalent in 
The Underworld
's fields. Echoes are physical replays of moments in the 
Unstable Universe
 seemingly stuck in a loop infinitely, consisting of both detailed structures and simulated player entities from the past.
