 was a minor antagonist of 
FlameFrags
's 
Season 3
 videos featured in the 
Unstable Universe
. He was the nominal leader of 
Mist Civilization
 and prevented every player from leaving 
the Mist
.

History

When Mistrul first made his appearance, he was shown inside his base, hidden in a cave deep within the Mist Civilization. He was reading a book until 
FlameFrags
 and 
Lomedy
 found him. Flame challenged Mistrul to a duel. Mistrul initially feigned retreat before ambushing FlameFrags, immediately demonstrating his ruthless and deceptive fighting style. showing that he was a ruthless fighter. This initiated a duel between Mistrul and FlameFrags. Mistrul was shown to be evenly matched against FlameFrags in this encounter. Mistrul eventually overpowered FlameFrags thanks to his superior resources and mastery of the mace, forcing him and Lomedy to fly away on a Happy Ghast. Before FlameFrags fled, Mistrul noted that FlameFrags had lost four of his totems, while Mistrul had only lost two. FlameFrags responded by claiming that maces were an unskillful combat method.

Not long after, Mistrul shot Lomedy, Flame's partner, off the edge of the Happy Ghast while they were hundreds of blocks in the air. He then used his mace to destroy the Ghast completely, causing FlameFrags to fall to the ground. After Mistrul found FlameFrags, he challenged him to another duel and refused to let FlameFrags restock the gear that had been drained during the previous fight. Despite his already overwhelming advantage, he also had an army of around 100 players to help him defeat FlameFrags. This ended in another victory for Mistrul, but FlameFrags was assisted by 
NotSkrilley
, who gave him a Potion of Invisibility, allowing him to flee. However, he was separated from Lomedy. NotSkrilley led FlameFrags to a small village of Mist Civilization explorers. The players made a deal with FlameFrags: if he helped them successfully escape the Mist, they would help him find his friend, Lomedy. This was also when FlameFrags met Mistrul's secret ally and trainer, 
Flowtives
.

Mistrul deliberately orchestrated their next encounter through Flowtives, who betrayed FlameFrags by luring him into an ambush under the pretense of searching for Lomedy. As the trio explored the cave, they heard the horn of Mistrul's army, meaning they had been spotted. Flow recommended that they blend in by using the same gear as the soldiers and Potions of Invisibility to hide their identities. This was a tactical ploy to downgrade Flame's armor, as it was enchanted with Curse of Binding. However, Mistrul and his army spotted them immediately because they were the only invisible players. FlameFrags then made an intelligent maneuver: he dug into a one-by-one hole and lured a few members of the Mist army inside. While in the hole, he splashed his second Potion of Invisibility, creating multiple invisible players. Mistrul was unable to tell them apart and decided to kill every invisible player, allowing the trio to escape. When they made it back, Flowtives decided to search for Lomedy at an escape route.

Mistrul's third encounter with FlameFrags happened shortly after the previous one. Back at the village, FlameFrags taught the explorers combat to help them defend themselves against Mistrul. However, the training session was interrupted by Mistrul and his army. They burned the village, killed all of the explorers, and forced NotSkrilley and FlameFrags to flee as the only survivors of the massacre. FlameFrags and NotSkrilley eventually found Flowtives and Mistrul in a tunnel, standing beside what appeared to be a burial site. Mistrul revealed that Flow had killed Lomedy, and Flowtives also revealed that he was the one who had trained Mistrul to become as strong as he was. This sent FlameFrags into a blind rage, but before he could attack, Mistrul detonated a TNT trap and destroyed the floor beneath them, sending both of them deep underground. Flame promised Skrilley that he would keep his promise to help him escape the Mist and told him to run as he was attacked by both Mistrul and Flow at the same time. However, Mistrul decided to chase after Skrilley, leaving Flowtives to deal with FlameFrags alone. FlameFrags struggled to defeat Flow despite wearing Netherite armor compared to Flow's Diamond armor. This could be attributed to his mental state. Meanwhile, Skrilley somehow managed to survive against Mistrul but chose not to run away, instead returning to help Flame fight the two of them. Mistrul and Flowtives reunited and fought FlameFrags, leading to the popular moment where FlameFrags dropped his sword mid-battle. Skrilley and Flame fled as Mistrul chased them. When they finally escaped, Skrilley fled the Mist, while FlameFrags decided to stay behind, giving himself time to recollect his thoughts.

Mistrul's fourth and final encounter, as well as his final on-screen appearance, happened after FlameFrags took a brief pause to recollect himself. Continuing his search for Mistrul, he eventually found him. Mistrul was so confident in his victory that he told his soldiers to wait outside. However, Flame was unusually silent because he had achieved the Zone, which allowed him to defeat Mistrul without any difficulty.

After seeing Mistrul's death message in chat, his soldiers rushed in to fight FlameFrags, but they were ultimately killed.

Upon returning, Flowtives was furious to see that FlameFrags had managed to kill Mistrul along with his army. He attempted to fight FlameFrags but was ultimately overpowered. FlameFrags threw Mistrul's armor at Flowtives. He told Flow that he could have the Elytra and mace as well, but burned them as soon as Flow came close. This left Mistrul dead, the civilization in ruins, and Flowtives defeated.
