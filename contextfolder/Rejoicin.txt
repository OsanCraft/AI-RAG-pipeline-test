Personality

Rejoicin was a kind and peaceful player with a slight clueless streak. He craved friendship after his long period of isolation, and didn't want to see anyone die, friend or enemy. he is also seen to be fond of sunflowers, and has a chest stacked with these flowers. He was one of the few players Wemmbu actually got along with quite well. 

History

Rejoicin is seen as a guard at 
Proton
.

Rejoicin joins a civilization who is afraid of the 
Mafia
 and is given the job of guarding a spire. 

He is later found by 
Wemmbu
 and 
Eggchan
 after the pair were trapped down there by and along with 
Jaden_MAN
. Wemmbu informs him that the Mafia has been disbanded before inquiring if he's seen Jaden. Rejoice points them toward the armory and shows the pair the ice boat highways. 

Rejoice tries to break up Wemmbu and Jaden's fighting, but Jaden proceeds to attack him. Rejoice helps Wemmbu by giving him potions. Jaden escapes and Rejoice follows Wemmbu back to 
Loppezz
 and Egg who had stayed behind in the forest. Rejoice and Wemmbu plan on how to kill Jaden and he officially joins Wemmbu's team. The four players go out to hunt for Jaden and discover him in a stone cube full of wardens. They all manage to enter the cube and attack Jaden. 

Rejoice gets cornered by several wardens and, despite Wemmbu's effort to save him, dies. Wemmbu proceeds to fight Jaden in silent mourning of his friend's death.
