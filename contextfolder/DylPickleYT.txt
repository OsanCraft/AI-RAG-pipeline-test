DylPickleYT is a character in the 
Unstable Universe
. He is the leader of three separate Pickle Army and presumably the founder of Pickletopia; a town in the glitched Farlands. He is first seen attempting to get trained by 
ItzRealMe
, but does not succeed in this endeavor.

Spoke

Mapicc

Jumper

NULL solders

He achieved his first kill in 
1000 Players Simulate HUNGER GAMES in Hardcore Minecraft

After this, he is given a new Pickle Army on the terms that he must kill Spoke or they will kill him. He obliges and attempts to kill Spoke but fails.
