It's Impossible to Catch this Player
 is the 6th episode featured in 
Unstable Universe
. 

Plot

The episode starts off after 
Wemmbu's
 fight with 
SpokeIsHere
, in which Spoke escaped using an ender pearl stasis chamber. Wemmbu, of course, makes it his goal to kill Spoke. Spoke’s base was, of course, empty, so he heads towards 
ParrotX2's
 base to ask him if he knows Spoke’s whereabouts, but Parrot doesn’t tell him anything. The only other player on the server that could know where Spoke was was 
JamatoP
, but after interviewing him at his base, Wemmbu finds out that he doesn’t know anything. He is about to leave when he spots other players in the base and quickly hides. The other players turn out to be 
ManePear
, 
Sorhin
, and 
FlameFrags
, three of 
PrinceZam's
 guards who are also looking for Spoke. 

Wemmbu decides to follow the guards back to the 
Zam Empire
 to see if PrinceZam wanted to form an alliance with him in their search for Spoke. However, in the middle of their conversation, 
Mapicc
 jumps them and they see a plane launching TNT onto the Zam Empire in the distance. PrinceZam’s castle is blown up and a fight ensues between Wemmbu, PrinceZam, and his guards, ManePear, FlameFrags, Sorhin, 
LuigiToan
, 
ECorridor
, 
zyye
, 
loppezz
, 
yungwillx
, 
Peentar
, 
SykerMC
, 
Chiffoi
, 
What_Max
, 
Elemental_Man
, and 
ItsRyanStuff
, and Spoke’s cronies, Mapicc, 
JumperWho
, 
TeamKalal
, 
RealReeon
, 
Only_A_Squid
, 
Derapchu
, 
Arcn
, and 
Sharooh
. They manage to blow up TeamKalal before Spoke’s goons stasis away.

Wemmbu and PrinceZam check with 
TheRealSquiddo
 first for the whereabouts of Spoke due to her previous affiliations with him. Squiddo agrees to DM them the coordinates of where Spoke last was. They find contraband items, stacked totems, and a spawn egg in her chests, so they 
chunk ban
 her.  

Wemmbu and PrinceZam spot Parrot and 
Wifies
 at the lighthouse on the outskirts of the Zam Empire. After a bit of prodding, Parrot reveals that Spoke sent them on a scavenger hunt for a secret that could change the server. Wemmbu demands the disk (when played with a texture pack reveals the next clue) they were talking about, but they refuse. Wemmbu and PrinceZam catch them breaking into the castle and chase them around the empire grounds, eventually losing them. 

They start heading to Squiddo’s coordinates, which are in the end. At the stronghold, they are attacked by Mapicc, 
Sharkilz_
, and RealReeon, three of Spoke’s henchmen who are guarding the portal. Mapicc and Sharkilz_ are able to escape while they manage to chunk ban RealReeon. 

Wemmbu and PrinceZam find a massive end base at the coordinates and sneak up on Spoke, JumperWho, and Derapchu. They chunk ban Derapchu and JumperWho, but Spoke escapes into the void. 

Seeing that chunk banning Spoke wasn’t as easy as they thought it would be, Wemmbu and PrinceZam come up with a plan to get him without a totem, then one-shot him with a 
mace
. They trade for a trial chamber map and mess around for a while before finding the chambers, where they get the ingredients for the mace. However, Arcn, Only_A_Squid, 
Fantst
, Sharooh, and Mapicc jump them in the trial chambers, so Wemmbu heads to the surface to get wood craft the mace while PrinceZam leads them to him. They then engage into a big fight and Arcn is chunk banned. 

Wemmbu and PrinceZam change plans and decide to go after Spoke’s henchmen, assuming that they will lead them to Spoke himself. They use an arrow tracker to track down Only_A_Squid and chunk ban him. They leave the underground base and after a bit of flying, spot a nether portal. They land to check it out and are attacked by Fantst and Sharooh, chunk banning Sharooh. 

The next location is a giant obsidian box. They break inside and see Mapicc, engaging in a fight. They are about to drop him when a group of invisible players that are later revealed to be Spoke, Sharkilz_, and Fantst  jump them. They take PrinceZam hostage and use him as a bargaining chip for the mace. Wemmbu burns the mace, so they start attacking again. Wemmbu and PrinceZam are about to die when they suddenly stasis away, leaving them broke. 

Once Wemmbu and PrinceZam regear themselves, they head to another obsidian box which, upon entering, isn’t infested with Spoke or his minions. Inside the obsidian box is a maze, and after wandering around, they find themselves back at the entrance. They assume that this is a trap set up by Mapicc until they hear Parrot and Wifies’s voice somewhere else in the maze. Wemmbu tells PrinceZam to barricade and guard the entrance while he starts searching for Parrot and Wifies. He is about to give up when he sees a name tag and mines in through the roof with a bucket of milk to counter the mining fatigue. He finds Parrot and Wifies without armour and makes a deal with them: if they show him where the disk is, then he’ll let them go alive. Wemmbu obtains the disk and turtle egg that was inside the chest at the center, but realizes that they need the texture pack Spoke sent Parrot to listen to the disk after Parrot and Wifies already left. 

Regardless, Wemmbu and PrinceZam head back to the Zam Empire to set a trap for Parrot and Wifies, knowing that they would likely try to steal the disk. Since the arrow tracker pointed them to Parrot and Wifies, they must be related to Spoke in some way. They put the disk in PrinceZam’s castle’s vault and set their ender pearl stasis chambers in a pile of gold and emerald blocks. 

Wemmbu and PrinceZam arrive at a manmade hole dug into the floor of an ocean decorated with custom coral, ruins, and many elder guardians. They come across a giant underwater castle and enter it, finding Sharkilz_ and chunk banning him after a short fight. However, not ten seconds after they ban him, the entire castle blows up, including the chunk ban, allowing Sharkilz_ to log back on and start attacking, as well as Fantst, who was hiding in the castle to jump them. In the end, they manage to chunk ban both Sharkilz_ and Fantst. 

Seeing that they only had one more location to go to, PrinceZam and Wemmbu split up so PrinceZam could go back to his empire to fetch more people and chunk bans and Wemmbu could stall Spoke. 

Wemmbu flies away until he eventually finds two twin towers guarding a blown up entrance into a mountain. He goes in and spots an invisible player, assuming that it was Mapicc. He communicates using signs and tells Wemmbu that he doesn’t care if he bans him off and that he would take Wemmbu to Spoke under a few conditions. 

The invisible player leads Wemmbu to a giant ice mountain that was hollowed out to create a parkour course inside. The player tells Wemmbu that he needs to complete the course without cheating and with no armour. After a few close calls with death, he completes the parkour, where he learns that this is part of the scavenger hunt Parrot and Wifies were doing. 

The player then leads Wemmbu to the next location, the same obsidian box he and PrinceZam previously found Parrot and Wifies in. The player tells Wemmbu that Spoke told him that he didn’t properly complete the maze. He forces Wemmbu to put all of his stuff in a chest and gives him two minutes to solve the maze. Wemmbu easily finds his way to the center and the player tells him that phase two is complete. He takes back all of his gear and the player leads him to the third location. 

The invisible player puts down a sign that says “Squiddo’s castle.” Wemmbu, realizing that this wasn’t Mapicc, but instead, Spoke, starts critting him out, but he pulls his ender pearl stasis and disappears after a quick fight. 

Wemmbu elytras to Squiddo’s castle, assuming that Spoke is there. The castle is almost completely burned down with fire spreading everywhere. He quickly finds Spoke and lands in front of him. They quickly engage in battle while Spoke tries to overpower Wemmbu’s moral with his taunts. Spoke tells him that at the end of the day, no matter how many people Wemmbu chunk bans, he will still be on the losing side of this war. Wemmbu says that PrinceZam is bringing people right now to help him, but Spoke retorts that no matter how many materials or manpower he brings, the amount of gear Spoke possesses is unimaginable. After a long, long, fight, Wemmbu manages to steal Spoke’s shulker full of all his materials and finally drain him of his resources, forcing him to yet again stasis away. However, before he left, Spoke shot a few arrows that Wemmbu could use to track his location, which he must’ve intended for him to find. 

Before Wemmbu could process any of the information, he was teleported to PrinceZam’s vault by the ender pearl stasis chamber they had set earlier. He sees PrinceZam, ManePear, and Peentar battling two invisible players that had stolen the disk and turtle egg and realizes that Parrot and Wifies had come to rob them. They chase them all the way to their underground nether portal and head inside, where they quickly find Parrot and Wifies behind a cluster of magma blocks. Wemmbu and PrinceZam strike a deal with them that they would hand Parrot and Wifies the disk to decode and then they would work together to find Spoke. Once Wemmbu and PrinceZam return to the overworld, though, Parrot and Wifies break the portal and escape. Wemmbu and PrinceZam return to the nether to search for them, but it was already too late. 

They gather 
Merffinn
 and LuigiToan, two of PrinceZam’s guards and head in the direction Spoke’s arrows pointed towards. However, when they reach the location, they see something interesting, finding Parrot chasing Spoke around in the sky. They follow parrot and start attacking Spoke. He leads them into two obsidian surrounded staircases and into what must’ve been the final challenge in Parrot and Wifies’s scavenger hunt, which included a puzzle, a redstone parkour course, a maze, a potion obstacle course, another parkour, a mini escape room, and a life-sized chess set. 

A fight ensues in the last room with the chessboard, which Merffinn discovers contains an active chunk ban. Spoke, on the other hand, says a few last words to Parrot and steps into the chunk ban. Everyone is shocked and confused, but Wemmbu realizes that he would rather be chunk banned than die at the hands of the Zam Empire, which meant that prisons would have to be constructed to keep the chunk bans from being broken. Now, all that was left to do was rebuild the Zam Empire.

Errors

In various sections of the video, the health bar switches from hardcore hearts to regular hearts.

Trivia

Spoke was finally caught in 
Finding 1000 Players Who Went Missing
, 124 episodes and a little under 2 years later.
