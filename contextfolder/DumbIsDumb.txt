DumbIsDumb
, also known as 
Dumb
, is a supporting character featured in 
Unstable Universe
. He was a member of the 
Warriors Council
 in 
Far Lands
.

History

DumbIsDumb is seen as a member of the 
Skyblock Civilization
.

Parrot
 agrees to fight DumbIsDumb to totem pop to become a council member. 
Parrot
 wins the fight by flooding the arena with water and using tridents. DumbIsDumb is banished from the 
Warriors Council
 for losing to 
Parrot
.

Trivia

He makes scripted videos on another server, Abyss SMP, as a protagonist along with Iamstormz and a third unknown protagonist who was later revealed to be Gopg, a former deuteragonist of the series.
