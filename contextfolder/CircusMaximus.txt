History

After LettuceK's fall he takes over LAW and reforms it into New LAW. His reforms make LAW into a good organization again dedicated into helping players. 

Despite his best efforts howerver, SargeLAW and his rouge members of Old LAW take over LAW again turning it bad yet again. It is unknown how they did this or if SargeLAW was always in LAW but it is clear that LAW's attitudes changed drastically under his leadership. 

It is unknown what happened to CircusMaximus but he could have been killed by SargeLAW.
