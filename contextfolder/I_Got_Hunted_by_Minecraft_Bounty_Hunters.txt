 are some of the most Elite Minecraft Bounty Hunters on the entire server. They're experts at strategy, combat and player tracking. Yet for some reason, they now want me dead. This is How I Got Hunted by Minecraft Bounty Hunters.
— 
ParrotX2

I Got Hunted by Minecraft Bounty Hunters
 is the 34th episode featured in 
Unstable Universe
. 

Plot

Parrot enters his room to find a chest containing two stacks of pearls, an invisibility potion, and a sign reading "Run." Suddenly, he receives mining fatigue effect. The entire BAT team storms in, blasting through all the walls in search of Parrot. However, he has concealed himself on top a massive circular lamp on the ceiling. Failing to locate him, they depart, allowing Parrot to relocate to a house 10,000 blocks away from the base. Yet, within minutes, he is discovered.

It turns out that Parrot was framed as being The Director.

"You have 60 seconds to come out!" The team hesitates to enter, suspecting a trap. During this time, Parrot constructs a Nether portal and escapes to the Nether roof, with the team hot on his heels. He runs out of rockets, but instead uses wind charges to propel himself forward with his elytra, successfully evading capture.

After hours of wandering, Parrot discovers an abandoned city. There, he encounters an invisible player—JumperWho, leader of BAT's spy team. She explains that she had saved Parrot through the chest. When he expresses doubt, she confirms the chest's contents: two stacks of pearls and an invisibility potion.

"Parrot, I care only about the truth. I know you've been framed," she asserts. "I'll take you to the base to show you why they believe you're The Director."

The plan is to introduce Parrot as a new spy with the codename "Alkaline." They proceed to the spy hall but are intercepted by Leowo0k and Derapchu. Jumper explains that she's showing the base to a new spy. In the library, they find a book stating that Any spy who hears the emergency signal—a horn with the "Call" sound —must report to the person who issued the signal. After moving on, she leads him to a secret underground room, turns the lights on, and outlines three reasons for the suspicion:

A letter from The Director to the team that matches Parrot's writing style.

Witnesses, including ItzRealMe, ashwagg, and others, claim he is The Director.

Most compellingly, the book MrCube delivered in prison on The Director's behalf mentioned the dandelion just placed in the cell. This implies that either The Director had magical knowledge of events inside, or Parrot is The Director.

In the corridor, Leowo0k confronts JumperWho and the invisible player again.

"We're conducting a spy check," announces the BAT leader.

Parrot is taken to a room where he and several other invisible players line up.

"When I give the signal, you must take up a green shield. If you don't have one, you're a mole."

Parrot lacks such a shield.

"Why didn't JumperWho tell me about this?" he wonders.

Fortunately, JumperWho distracts Leowo0k. Everyone exits the room. Parrot notices several green banners placed on the wall and crafts a green shield from one. Upon their return, the leader gives the command. However, only Parrot's shield is green.

"Parrot!" exclaims Leowo0k, realizing that only an outsider would deliberately use the banners from the wall. In the ensuing chase, Parrot escapes using the launch chambers.

Back in the abandoned city, Parrot finds a book left by The Director, stating they will meet at this location in 24 hours. He shows the book to JumperWho. The plan is to bring BAT to observe the meeting, proving Parrot isn't The Director. However, Jumper mentions an important team meeting about the current issue that Parrot must attend. He asks why she can't just tell him what happened, but she provides no clear answer.

"Do you trust me?" she asks.

Parrot hesitates but affirms. JumperWho then gives him materials to spawn multiple withers.

"She's gone too far to prove my innocence," he thinks.

They head to the meeting hall, which Parrot himself built to guard against MrCube. It features 5-block-thick obsidian walls, a 52-block cubic structure, and floor lighting activated by stepping on it. In the center are several blocks and chairs for participants. JumperWho instructs her friend to eavesdrop from a glass pane near the ceiling. All BAT team leaders enter and ascend the podium. Their speeches lack significance, raising Parrot's suspicion of a trap. Indeed, Jumper looks up along with everyone else.

"Jumper, you betrayed me!" Parrot exclaims in disbelief.

A battle ensues. Parrot must escape the massive cube with its 5-block-thick obsidian walls. He recalls the wither materials JumperWho provided earlier.

"You're incredibly stupid!" Parrot declares.

He spawns as many withers as possible to break the obsidian, but to no avail. He then approaches the entrance, likely blocked by Leowo0k with only a single layer of obsidian. This escape attempt also fails. Amid the chaos, Parrot manages to hide in the water-filled space beneath the room.

Back in the abandoned city, Jumper approaches Parrot, who refuses to speak with her. After she explains the situation, they restore their trust. She had to prove her loyalty to the team after bringing Parrot into the base. To do so, she had to set a trap for him but intentionally gave him the wither materials to facilitate his escape.

"Ah, now I understand why you're the leader of the spy team. You're a double double agent!" says Parrot.

Suddenly, an explosion is heard—someone is nearby. It's undoubtedly a spy. All seems lost; there's no way to stop him from reporting to Leowo0k. Parrot remembers the book from the BAT library, stating that every spy must respond immediately to a horn signal. The strategy works—a visible player appears right in front of the massive central structure. Parrot immediately attacks. They trap him in a small box to prevent escape. JumperWho faces a dilemma: kill the spy or let him go, risking the operation.

"We have to kill him!" Parrot insists.

JumperWho resists. "What's the alternative?" he asks rhetorically.

Parrot watches as Jumper is forced to kill one of her own. On the grave, she places a sign reading, "In truth, there's always something that keeps it from being perfect. Jumper."

She sets off to gather the team, telling Parrot, "Whether you've manipulated us or not, I care about the truth and nothing else. Whether you're The Director or not!"

Leowo0k, Derapchu, Nufuli, JumperWho, and several invisible players arrive at the location, just outside the entrance of the ruined structure. Parrot stands in the center.

Suddenly, an invisible figure appears—wearing full netherite armor with white trims.

It's The Director. His voice is masked by a voice changer.

The conversation between them is brief, but revealing:

Parrot:
 "Can you tell me why you wrote that book and why you showed up? After everything you've done? Why now?"

The Director:
 "Right now, you need me more than I need you. This is the only way to prove to your team that you're not The Director. Otherwise, I wouldn't have shown up, would I?"

Parrot:
 "Why did you kill Wifies?"

The Director:
 "Because I had to."

Parrot:
 "He was my friend!"

The Director:
 "That's why I had to kill him."

Parrot:
 "Take off the voice changer. Look at yourself, bro! You killed Wifies and then sent everyone after me! How did you know about the dandelion?!"

The Director:
 "You won’t understand, just like you won’t understand many other things, Parrot. And that’s how it should be."

Parrot:
 "Take off the voice changer."

The Director:
 "The voice changer isn't for me. It's for you. If you knew who I was, you wouldn't be angry. You’d be terrified. Maybe one day, but not today."

Parrot:
 "Who are you, man?"

The Director:
 "Who do you think I am, Parrot?"

Before Parrot can respond, the entire BAT team jumps into action, attacking the invisible figure. The Director vanishes.

As they search the area, they come across the grave of the spy JumperWho had to eliminate.

"It was him or us!" Parrot declares firmly.

"You killed one of your own?!" Leowo0k exclaims, unable to believe it. "That's unacceptable! What even happened here?!"

JumperWho explains everything.

"This is NOT how we operate! The question isn’t whether you wanted to do it—it’s whether you DID!" Leowo0k shouts in frustration. Then he asks, "Why are we working against each other?"

Parrot snaps. "Leo! Don't get me even started! Look at yourself, bro!"

"You guys were the ones who first decided that Parrot was The Director!" JumperWho states bluntly.

"Jumper, you might be right, but we can no longer trust you. You can no longer be part of our team," Leowo0k declares. Then he asks, "Jumper, do you regret what you did?"

"No. Because it was the right thing to do" JumperWho replies.

Without another word, she takes off—flying over the ocean.
