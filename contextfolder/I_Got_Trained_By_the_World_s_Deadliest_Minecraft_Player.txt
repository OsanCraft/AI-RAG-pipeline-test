I Got Trained by the God of War
— 
FlameFrags
I Got Trained By the World's Deadliest Minecraft Player
 is the 144th episode featured in 
Unstable Universe
.

Plot

Spoiler Alert

The episode begins with 
FlameFrags
 and 
TheG0DofWar
 coming to an agreement where TheG0DofWar would train FlameFrags as long as he helps him reclaim his old base. In the civilization, TheG0DofWar explains that ever since The Law was defeated, the base became a civilization but he eventually got overthrown and exiled. He tells Flame to help him take it back, but he wants to evaluate Flame's sword skill before that. After Flame passes the evaluation by comboing him 5 times, TheG0DofWar warns Flame that the members of the civilization don't think the same way they do because they would do anything to win.

As they traverse through the civilization, TheG0DofWar and Flame fight the champions of the civilization and they kill both of them. After the fight, the members of the civilization put Flame and TheG0DofWar down. TheG0DofWar also teaches Flame how to spear-swap. While learning how to spear-swap Flame asks TheG0DofWar why they were fighting champions instead of regular civilization members and why they just watch instead of helping the champions. He explains that the civilization was made up of Law Elites and were more loyal to TheG0DofWar than The Law. Since they were the best they believed in total strength and since TheG0DofWar showed weakness, they lost their faith in him and it got worse after he suffered many defeats afterwards. One day, 
Antaggg
 and his group of warriors defeated TheG0DofWar once again and exiled him and they also believed that true strength comes from victories and the power that their weapons have. As Flame continues practicing the spear-swapping technique, TheG0DofWar tells him that swiftness potions will help him before they head to the next arena.

As they reach the next arena, they fight another group of champions. This time, it's harder than the last fight. One of the champions has turtle master potions while the other has an elytra and a spear. Eventually, one of the fighters' armour breaks. Flame finds a way to counter the elytra-spear user by placing blocks while they are flying towards him. As they elytra-spear user attempts to spear Flame, he holds out his spear and attacks him while he is flying. TheG0DofWar finishes both of them off. FlameFrags burns the elytra but quickly regrets it as he realizes that he could just sell it instead. Flame and TheG0DofWar both get confronted by two members of the civilization and they tell them that they're getting weaker and they don't understand what a fighter is truly about. They tell the members of the civilization that true fighters are those who don't use shortcuts and rely on their skill. They tell Flame and TheG0DofWar that the whole point of fighting is to win. TheG0DofWar explains that the only reason break their standards is if it is agreed upon by both sides to do so.  

TheG0DofWar asks Flame if he can use a crossbow + TNT minecarts because it'll be useful against the next champions. Flame thanks TheG0DofWar for actually understanding the path to the Combat Grandmaster rank like he does because everyone who has trained him in the past never understood it. TheG0DofWar tells Flame that he feels the same way. As they reach the next arena, they noticed that there were three champions instead of two. One of the champions have a mace. Flame manages to beat him despite using a mace while TheG0DofWar fights the other two champions. Since TheG0DofWar is drained in resources, Flame takes one of the guys he is fighting and he swiftly kills him. While they were fighting the last guy, he calls in his henchmen to fight Flame and TheG0DofWar with him. Flame runs out of minecarts and TheG0DofWar's helmet breaks. Flame also runs out of potions. TheG0DofWar begins beating up the champion and the champion kills him using an arrow cannon. Since Flame would have no chances of winning the fight, he uses the mace he got from killing the first champion and kills them all. After the fight, the audience begin making fun of Flame and made fun of TheG0DofWar's death which leads to Flame crashing out. As they continue taunting him, he burns the mace. Flame vows to take the civilization back for TheG0DofWar.   

As Flame heads to the last arena, he meets 
Antaggg
. Flame and Antaggg finally have their match and during their fight, he calls his henchmen to make a full beacon of Jump Boost. After Flame pops his first totem, Antaggg taunts him and begins doubting his combat master title. Afterwards, he calls out his henchmen to give Flame Mining Fatigue while he has Haste and he also gives Flame Weakness. Flame also begins using carts and he begins winning the fight. Antaggg runs away and claims that he won the fight. The audience continues clowning on Flame and Antaggg uses this to boast himself as the combat master. Flame vows to get a rematch and win for TheG0DofWar.
