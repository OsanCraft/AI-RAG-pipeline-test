Lomedy's Farm
 was a major location in the 
Unstable Universe
, especially in 
FlameFrags
's videos. It was owned and run by Lomedy before it was overtaken and seized by the 
LAW
.

History

FlameFrags took refuge here when he was getting hunted by LAW. When 
Deputy Ace
 and his team arrive for Flame, they seize the farm and arrest Lomedy for conspiring with and aiding a criminal. It was likely abandoned after Lomedy's arrest.
