Kingdoms

Willsion and Spongs follow Flame and Lomedy back to their new base. Willsion splashes an invisibility pot and Spongs thought he got splashed as well, so Flame easily spots Spongs following them and confronts them. Flame hits Willsion with a sword and he cries about it. Flames give them bother warnings that neither heed, as they water bucket into the middle of their base while him and Lomedy are talking about securing the base. Willsion is intrigued by the topic of traps and wants to "help" them build some. He stays behind with Lomedy while Flame and Spongs head to Redstone City.
