InfamousJJ, often called JJ, is a character in 
Unstable Universe
.

History

In 
1000 Players Simulate THE PURGE in Minecraft
, he serves as a antagonist against Wemmbu and his team. He is shown to have partial netherite armor and totem(s) of undying. It is unknown how he got those items, but it is likely he got it from a smuggled ender chest. He guards the exit of THE PURGE to prevent Wemmbu's team from escaping. At the end of the episode, Wemmbu leads JJ on a chase into booby-trapped woods, ultimately killing him as a booby-trapped tree blows up right next to him.
