Personality

At first, Wifies had a quiet and calming demeanor, was collected and well-spoken, and tended to sacrifice himself for the needs of other people. At least, this was how he appeared on the surface. Underneath his friendly and calm exterior, he was also very intelligent, perspicacious, and manipulative, often outsmarting Parrot in the process. He was always more than a step ahead of others and feigned this normal persona, but he also was someone who was driven only by wanting to protect his best friend, saying that he would have no purpose nor reason to be on the server if he wasn't able to be with Parrot. He has also shown a lot of devotion and loyalty towards Parrot, and this allegiance is unequivocal throughout Season 1. 

Wifies used unethical methods to hunt down his best friend Parrot and predict whatever he will do in any stress-inducing situation, and dismissed how his friend felt about him, as long as he could be preserved. He is portrayed to be extremely overprotective over Parrot, not willing to let him fight in a world of peril (shown when he was apprehensive about Parrot trying to get to the 
Far Lands
) and even trapping Parrot to keep him safe, being vigilant enough to remove all malicious entities in order to make sure that he would be unscathed within the prison. While his ideals were genuine, his obsession and several malicious acts done to Parrot led him to unintended consequences and eventually his death.
