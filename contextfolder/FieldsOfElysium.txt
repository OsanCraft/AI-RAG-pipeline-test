FieldsOfElysium
 is a supporting character in 
Unstable Universe
. He is the current ruler of 
The Wheat Kingdom
 and wields a mace.

History

FieldsOfElysium is seen in the beginning telling a hostile group to leave. When the group tries to resort to violence, 
Wemmbu
 steps in and threatens them with his mace. 
Yungyx
, the leader, flees. FieldsOfElysium thanks Wemmbu for the help and offers him bread which Wemmbu vehemently declines yet is given anyway.

Wemmbu is concerned for the kingdom's safety, which prompts FieldsOfElysium to tell him the story of an old protector they had that has since left the server, and owned a mace. He tells Wemmbu to look for 
LordSirWessley
, as he is the only person the hero told about the whereabouts of the mace. Wemmbu leaves hastily after FieldsOfElysium tells him the festival Wessley would be at would be ending soon. 

Wemmbu returns with the mace and gives it to FieldsOfElysium. The monarch thanks him profusely and offers him anything the kingdom has in return, including two horses, one for Wemmbu and the other for his best friend, 
Eggchan
.
