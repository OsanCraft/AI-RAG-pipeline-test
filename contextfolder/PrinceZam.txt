PrinceZam
, also known simply as 
Zam
, is a supporting character in 
Unstable Universe
. She was one of the two secondary antagonists (alongside 
FlameFrags
) in Season 1. She serves as the tritagonist of Spoke's 
BAT Arc
.   

He was the former ruler of the 
Zam Empire
, a faction that once thrived on influence and control. Early in the server, Prince Zam and 
Wemmbu
 had a very strong alliance, however Zam's paranoia and need for control ultimately led to her betraying Wemmbu. The Wemmbu Arc spanned several episodes, with Zam constantly obsessing over chasing Wemmbu down, then letting him escape and forcing Wemmbu to live in fear. This conflict comes to a head after Wemmbu detonates an Orbital Strike Cannon against Zam, when ClownPierce imprisons them together for entertainment. Zam and Wemmbu are forced to work together, and they become close again, although they are never truly allies again.  

Despite its earlier prominence, the Zam Empire ultimately fell, leading to its dissolution following internal and external conflicts, including opposition from notable figures like 
Wemmbu
, 
ClownPierce
 and 
Reddoons
. This collapse marked a significant turning point in Zam's arc within the server's story.

Personality

PrinceZam is known for her ambitious and tenacious nature, aiming to consolidate power and secure dominance through strategic planning and fortifications, such as the infamous wall that led to discord among other factions and various prisons where he chunkbanned many of his enemies. Zam's leadership showcased both determination and an ability to command respect, even amidst controversy. Zam is also characterized by an intense need for control of both her citizens and the full server, as well as extreme paranoia and concern over people who potentially pose a threat to the Empire.
