The 
Kingdom of the Caves
 is an underground civilization ruled and founded by the self-proclaimed king 
arachn1d_

king
 of the server. They are created after the 
Kings Arc
, being initially irrelevant; though they pose a great threat as the story goes by. They serve as the primary antagonist faction in 
The Kingdoms Arc
 of 
Wemmbu
's POV, subjugating him through taking 
Eggchan
 imprisoned.

In 
Wemmbu
's video, 
I Joined a 100,000 Player Minecraft Server
, several members of the Spider Web Faction are seen to demand Wemmbu join them in the caves, to which Wemmbu responds by using a 
stab shot
, killing them. It is important to note they may have intimidated many others using this method and forced them into the kingdom.

They traded their entire armory with the invisible blue trimmed player to capture 
Eggchan
 and had him throw 16 different ender pearls to ensure he wouldn't escape. Wemmbu is unwillingly working with them because he wants to free his best friend. 
Jaden_MAN
 joined the civilization with Wemmbu to help him.

Structure

Leadership

Arachnid is the King of his kingdom, alongside the second-in-command 
Wemmbu
, and the commander 
yungyx
. 

Hierarchy

Their civilization has a ranking system similar to 
the Invisible Mafia
. It has about 50 or so royals and the majority of its players are citizens, while some unlucky ones are Bottom Feeders.   

Bottom Feeders

Bottom Feeders have to work in the mines to help the kingdom get resources. The mines are dangerous as they are almost directly near a sculk biome, risking a warden as shown how Wemmbu accidentally spawned them. They also get do not have access to any special resources and have to do missions in order to be promoted. They are put into prison for not doing work, even having to fight to death in the Arena to be send back.

They hold enchanted black and white pattern shields. 

Citizens/Spiders

The citizens get access to a lot of perks, including owning property and getting access to The Night Vision Stands, Market, Storage Room, Enchanter, Weaves' Drop and more.

They are expected to get geared and go on missions on Arachnid's orders. They hold enchanted black and white pattern shields. 

Royalty

The royals are the highest level of membership a player can get in the Kingdom. They live in separate mansions on top of the webs above everyone else. Due to the low amount of people, the Webs are almost always empty.

They are also permitted to enter Arachnid's Castle, and the meeting room without any items. They carry out elite missions for Arachnid, like fighting in The Civil War. They hold the purple horizontal beam across the shield.

Location

The kingdom is located inside a snowy mountain. Its main base is completely dark and citizens get night vision potions for free. It is built around a mineshaft to get access to spider spawners. It is also near a sculk biome which can prove deadly sometimes as wardens can spawn.
