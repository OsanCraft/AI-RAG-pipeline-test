Personality

Flame is a highly skilled and combat-focused player whose personality has largely centered around his pride in his fighting skills and fortitude. He has demonstrated capabilities to endure long fights and shows resilience and resourcefulness even in the face of impossible odds.  Thus, he's earned the title of "Immortal Demon". He eschews maces because he believes they are a dishonorable weapon and it would reflect badly on his skill if he were to be seen using them. 

He can be condescending towards players of inferior skill, reflecting a confident attitude towards his PvP strength and a healthy ego. His self-worth is deeply intertwined with his title, causing him to stubbornly defend his reputation and taking on difficult challenges and fighting strong opponents in a constant battle to prove himself. On multiple occasions he has demonstrated the opinion that violence is usually the answer, and he is rather quick to pick fights.  

He has shown great care for his friend, 
Lomedy
, going great lengths to save him and their relationship. Flame struggles to show his emotions outside of battle and will sometimes prioritize a fight over personal relationships. He seems to be afraid of letting others get too close and then losing them. He doesn't care very much about morals but tries to refrain from taking fights that don't benefit him or killing needlessly for Lomedy's sake and to prevent another 
LAW
 situation. He tends to opt for being alone most of the time and believes teammates slow him down, relating to 
ShoeBilly
 in this way, but in recent episodes he's shown to have a slight change of heart and will often hang out with his friends; accepting their help with most things (though when fighting he generally dislikes help or teammates).    

Flame loves dogs and builds 
machines
 to unleash their combat power. His favourite words are bro and like.
