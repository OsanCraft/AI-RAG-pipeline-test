 under

History

He was a commander of a small squad attempting to capture Parrot and Wemmbu. He was slowed down by the pig he was riding when the pair decided to cross a river. His squad then lost Parrot and Wemmbu in a cave. He later found them again, along with a large amount of other LAW members, but failed to capture Parrot and Wemmbu.  

He likely left LAW after Lettuce was defeated.
