Trident
 
 

The trident is one of the rarest and most effective weapons on the 
Unstable SMP
.  They are unique in the fact that tridents can be used as both melee and ranged weapons. They are usually enchanted with Riptide, allowing the user to dart around in water and even fly when it's raining. They are incredibly scarce because the server's mob cap causes Drowned, which are the main sources of tridents, to almost never spawn. On top of that, only 6.25% (or 1 in 16) drowned spawn wielding a trident. Even if the drowned wields a trident, the trident only has a 8.5% chance to drop their trident when killed by a player. The chance is increased by 1% for each level of Looting up to a maximum of 11.5% with Looting III.  Riptide tridents can be used to boost ender pearls, and tridents are the only weapon that cannot be crafted.
