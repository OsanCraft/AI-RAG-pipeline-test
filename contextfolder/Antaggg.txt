Personality

antaggg is shown as a smart, opportunistic, and notably a self-centered character; being the leader of 
TheG0DofWar
's civilization whom he indoctrinated with his beliefs. He believes that victory comes from power instead of skill; winning through the usage of unconventional tactics in combat to gain advantage against opponents which Flame claims to be unfair. Despite this, he seems to be rather cocky, claiming 
Flame
 will never be able to beat him; and cowardly, fleeing as soon as Flame popped his totem. 

History

Antaggg defeats 
TheG0DofWar
 using unfair advantages and exiles him from his civilization. He moves in with his own followers.

After 
TheG0DofWar
 is killed by an arrow cannon, 
FlameFrags
 challenges him to a fight. Antaggg uses his rigged arena to get the upper hand but flees when 
Flame
 adapts and begins to win.

Antaggg hosts a PVP tournament where 16 fighters battle until one player is standing. The last player would have to fight him after.

Flame
 eventually wins the PVP Tournament but loses to Antaggg because he cheated by switching players mid-fight(revealed by Falconu).
