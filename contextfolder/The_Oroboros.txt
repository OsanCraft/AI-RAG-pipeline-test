The_Oroboros
 was a high ranking member of 
the Law
, being one of its commanders.

History

He was 
LettuceK
’s third or fourth in command in 
The Law
 and a fearsome crossbowman, often using tipped arrows to fight players, much to the chagrin of 
Wemmbu
, whose Achilles heel in combat is arrow effects, particularly slow falling. He has also used weakness arrows as well, particularly to counter 
FlameFrags
's melee combat skills.

He was first seen fighting Wemmbu and The Pirates in 
I Explored the Minecraft Great Sea
.

He was seen guarding the orbital strike cannon in 
Unstable SMP: The Battle for The Great Sea
. He briefly fought Wemmbu, and fled from combat using an elytra.

He was killed by  
TheobaldTheBird
 In 
2 Minecraft Pros VS 1000 Hunters
 after a lengthy, destructive TNT minecart battle.

A grave was made for him with a sign quoting ¨loser¨ on it.
