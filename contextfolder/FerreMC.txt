FerreMC
, also known as 
Ferre
, is a character in 
Unstable Universe
. He is the teammate and righthand man of 
ClownPierce
, as well as the co-leader of 
The Invisible Mafia.

History

Season 1

Leow0ok
 agrees with 
ParrotX2
 and 
Wifies
 to put Parrot in an obsidian vault as bait for ClownPierce. Wifies decides to go with him. ClownPierce, FerreMC, and 
Mugm_
 come over and try to break in and kill Parrot. A fight between people against and with ClownPierce ensues.

Parrot convinces Leow0ok’s team to help him kidnap 
BranzyCraft
, as he’s the only person ClownPierce cares about. They go to negotiate with ClownPierce now that they have Branzy. FerreMC comes back with Wifies as a hostage. Parrot agrees to let Branzy go, and ClownPierce lets Wifies go. ClownPierce and FerreMC find Parrot's jungle base and destroy it.

Wemmbu
 and 
Eggchan
 set up a job where people can hire them to blow up things. 
Woogiex
 hires them to blow up Ferre’s base. They find that Ferre has a secret tunnel system in his tower, so they go to investigate. Ferre catches him and Wemmbu tries to escape. He goes underground, and eventually Ferre leaves. Wemmbu and eggchan come back to destroy the base. They send three TNT missiles to destroy the base. They are able to destroy the base using TNT, and are able to escape by going underground.

ClownPierce puts 
PrinceZam
 and Wemmbu in a prison. FerreMC is seen as a guard at this prison.

FerreMC joins the Invisible Mafia and becomes the leader of the diamond players.

ClownPierce and FerreMC destroy 
ManePear's Treehouse
. Wemmbu and eggchan escape by going into the nether.

Wemmbu tries to kill ClownPierce and FerreMC but fails. FerreMC threatens to kill eggchan if Wemmbu doesn’t let ClownPierce kill him. Ferre fails to kill eggchan and they escape. They almost kill Ferre by breaking all of his armor. Ferre gives them a mace in exchange for his life to be spared. (Not without Wemmbu attempting to kill him until Eggchan convinces Wemmbu not to)

Wemmbu, 
Wato1876
 and eggchan distract ClownPierce and FerreMC by fighting them while 
SpokeIsHere
 destroys 
The Invisible Mafia
.

Season 2

ParrotX2, FlameFrags, and TheobaldTheBird take FerreMC hostage in the Nether. He is eventually able to escape.

Trivia

FerreMC is confirmed to be part of a Nether Civilization consisting of ClownPierce, Mugm, and other powerful players who aim to monopolize resources only obtainable in the nether.

FerreMC is ClownPierce's righthand man.
