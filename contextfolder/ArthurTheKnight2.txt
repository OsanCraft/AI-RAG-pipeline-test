Merchant City Vanguard
 under 
DanchoGold
. He was killed by 
ShoeBilly_
. He wore full netherite armor with full redstone wayfinder armor trims. 

History

Arthur became the leader of the Merchant City Vanguard. He was instructed by Dancho to keep 
Parrot
 and 
Theo
 safe from the Capitol City Police so the pair could pay their 100000-diamond debt back. Arthur was one of the Vanguards who entered Capitol City with the hidden Parrot. He bribed the guards at the entrance to let them into the city as the guards were aware they were trying to sneak someone in. After entering the city, he goes with Theo to keep look out and inquires if Theo was one of the people who fought in the battle against LAW. He asks Theo to train him. Theo inquires if he knows of a player named Billy. Arthur says yes. Theo reveals to him that he used to train with Billy and he has a suspicion that he's somehow involved in this. ShoeBilly maces Arthur from behind, surprising Theo.
