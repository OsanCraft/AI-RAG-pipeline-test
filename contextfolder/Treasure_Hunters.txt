The 
Treasure Hunters
 were a team composed of 
Boosfer
, 
Kier
, and 
Dev
. They grew extremely rich from stealing items and auctioning them off. Boosfer hired 
Wemmbu
 to blow up a biome that was a part of Parrot's quests in 
It's Impossible to Find This Player
. Wemmbu is stopped by 
Parrot
, who convinces Wemmbu to join him and 
Wifies
 in raiding the Treasure Hunters' base. The team effectively broke apart after Wemmbu, Parrot, and Wifies attacked their base and destroyed it with TNT and withers. Wemmbu obtained many shulkers full of diamond and netherite blocks from this raid.
