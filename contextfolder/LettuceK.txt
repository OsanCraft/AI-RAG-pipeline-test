LettuceK
, known as Lettuce or LettyK, was a supporting character featured in 
Unstable Universe
, and is one of the main antagonists in Unstable Universe Season 2. He is a minor antagonist in 
The Pirate Arc
, the secondary antagonist in the 
Betrayal Arc
, a major antagonist in both the 
Fake Identity Arc
 and the 
Skill vs Power Arc
, and the main antagonist of 
The Kings Arc
. 

Personality

Throughout Season 1 and early Season 2, LettuceK is unserious, goofy and easily intimidated by more powerful players such as Wemmbu into complying with their wishes in the Pirate Arc into giving up his items inexchange for his life. 

LettuceK portrays an intelligent personality throughout Season 2. He is willing to use violence and deception to achieve his goals and he calculates his moves carefully. He often uses other's fears as leverage, especially when he sees a threat.  He uses every strategy, gimmick or army that he has to fight. He rarely questions his own decisions from a moral standpoint, focusing instead on what he believes is the greater good. However, although he believes himself to be a righteous arbiter, his actions become progressively more morally dubious as time goes on, leading to the betrayal from his right-hand man 
Deputy_Ace
. 

Unlike 
ParrotX2
, LettuceK plans out his actions and prepares for what is to come. He is a master strategist always looking at the future and planning ahead. He can see things that others can't and can predict unrest in the server.
