If you are reading this, I am either captured or dead.

A few months ago, I exiled myself permanently

Stop the exploits

Protest the server

Protest my friend

However, it's seemed as if the server has only gotten worse

Over the past few weeks, I have been hunted relentlessly by the Mafia

A player by the name of Ashswag demanded I tell him how to perform the exploits, but every single time

I refused

He then sent hundreds of invisible players after me, but eventually the threats got worse

He threatened to kill Spoke

I knew giving the exploits up would destroy the world

But I couldn't let my friend die

I traveled to spawn and stood in my home until Ash arrived with the diamond players

I gave myself up, and they took me to the Outpost

There, I proceeded to perform every exploit he demanded

However we discovered they were patched

I still don't know why or how this happened

But it didn't stop Ash from trying to kill me

However, he didn't realize what I had just done

But everyone that plays on it

I have exploited, betrayed friends, and have given Ash a reason to destroy everything I love

But hopefully this item will give us all freedom

Best of luck traveler
