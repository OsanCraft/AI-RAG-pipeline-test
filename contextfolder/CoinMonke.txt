CoinMonke
, also known as 
Coin
, is a supporting character in 
Unstable Universe
. He formerly worked as a guard in 
Capital City
. He betrayed Reddoons after the death of LuigiToan and escaped to the Far Lands. LuigiToans journal reveals that he and CoinMonke were friends before the events of 
Mining City
, and it is implied that Luigi told Coin about the Far Lands and they planned to escape together before Coin became a warden at Capital City. 

Personality

CoinMonke first appears as a rude and nagging individual, being in charge of collecting the diamond quotas in Capital City. He gives numerous amounts of threats to the workers, and is quick to burst at them when offended. He is strict and often belittles the workers if they are not producing enough diamonds.

It is later revealed that he heavily dislikes the thought of killing, unable to kill 
ParrotX2
 or 
LuigiToan
 during the roulette events of 
I Survived Minecraft's Most Corrupt City
. His pacifism lead to him being fired by 
Reddoons
, however CoinMonke returns to Mining City for LuigiToans funeral, showing how sensitive he can be. He also offers to assist in the uprising against Capital City, however, Parrot and Wifies insist on Coin going home because he had everything to lose. Despite this Coin stays behind to protect his friends, and while 
Dean
, Wifies, and Parrot were being threatened by 
Ashwagg
 and the 
Mafia
, CoinMonke pulls their stasis and helps them escape, showing how loyal and determined he is to stand for what is right.    

CoinMonke, although he was a minor character, had great character development, and was loyal to his friends after he realized how corrupt 
Reddoons
 was.    

History

LuigiToan and CoinMonke are revealed to be formerly friends in a book.

Parrot, 
Wifies
, and 
deanthebean9
 are forced to mine for diamonds for Reddoons in capital city and have to reach a quota of 10,000 diamond blocks. They find a group of miners with CoinMonke as the warden.

Parrot and his friends steal from Capital city vaults. Parrot gives 
PikaTM
 more than 3,000 diamond blocks so CoinMonke lets him free. Parrot gives more people diamonds he stole from Capital city for them to be set free.

Reddoons finds out about the people getting set free and sees that his diamonds have been stolen.

Reddoons makes it so whoever has the lowest amount of diamonds plays a game of roulette to decide if they live or not. Deanthebean9 and Parrot are tied for lowest. Parrot loses, but CoinMonke says he can’t kill him, as he doesn’t want to kill him. Parrot threatens to tell Reddoons that he didn’t do his job unless he helps them steal from Reddoons. 

CoinMonke tells them Reddoons plans to lock down all of his vaults.

Parrot overhears Reddoons talking to CoinMonke. Reddoons mentions that ashswagg suggested they kill people. Reddoons forces people to play a game which decides if they live or do not live. Reddoons rigs it so LuigiToan dies. CoinMonke refuses to kill him. Reddoons kills LuigiToan himself.

CoinMonke agrees to give the miners gear to steal Reddoons diamonds for leverage. The team gets caught. Reddoons gambles if they can leave or not. Reddoons lets everyone free except for Parrot, deanthebean9, and Wifies, even though he won his bet. Reddoons brings them to ashswagg and the Mafia. Ashswagg suggests they join the Mafia, but they say no. They try to force them to join, but CoinMonke activates their stasis chamber and they escape.

CoinMonke escapes to 
Far Lands
.

CoinMonke is part of a team that tries to break into the Tomb of the Mafia for the gear and is the only member who survives. He joins Parrot and 
TheobaldTheBird
 when they journey back to the closer end islands.
