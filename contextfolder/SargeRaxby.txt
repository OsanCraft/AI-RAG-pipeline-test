History

SargeRaxby is seen as the prison warden and PvP trainer. 
Wemmbu

Loppezz

He believes the prisoners will eventually reform work for The Law, and as such trains them in PvP. 

As Wemmbu, 
PrinceZam
, 
Fantst
, and 
Baablu
 are escaping, SargeRaxby pleads to them and laments that he thought they were better, before being slammed and killed by Wemmbu.

PrinceZam would take and use SargeRaxby's Black Law Shield as it was the only shield he had, which was later noticed by LettuceK and Wemmbu, the latter of which laughingly remarking that Zam was evil for it.

Following his death, his brother SargeLAW suceeded him as Law Commander and swore to avenge his death, relentlessly hunting Wemmbu down.
