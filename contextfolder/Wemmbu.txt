Personality

Wemmbu is loud, sarcastic, competitive, and emotionally explosive. His behavior frequently oscillates between friendly banter and destructive hostility, depending on how threatened he may feel in the moment. He has a tendency to act within his own self-interest, sometimes refusing to help others unless there is something for him to gain. 

He is quick to resort to violence and has a desperate need to be "the strongest." This could suggest that he has a deep inferiority complex, and his bellicosity and confidence may be hiding this.  He believes in doing whatever it takes to achieve victory and that fighting with honor is an unnecessary limitation (a belief he learned from 
ManePear
). Although he can be cunning, his impulsivity often leads to a lack of strategic thought behind most of his decisions. He is an extremely skilled and capable fighter who holds great respect for his weapons, particularly his signature maces, 
Gambit
 and 
Crucible
. Wemmbu is also known for his use of 
Orbital Strike Cannons
 that he uses whenever he feels outnumbered or outskilled (for example, when Wemmbu fought 
FlameFrags
 and opted to blow him up with his Orbital Strike Cannons rather than fight fairly). On multiple occasions he has shown a degree of disregard for life on the server, but he deeply cares about his friends and will go to great lengths to safeguard them. 

Wemmbu is also heavily protective of his best friend 
Eggchan
, despite the latter's weakness and inexperience with the game. He will put Eggchan before his own life and has fought under the rule of others for the sole purpose of keeping Egg safe. He has also grown to learn to forgive his enemies rather than kill them (
PrinceZam
, 
Jaden_Man
, and 
LettuceK
) and that violence isn't the answer to every problem he faces, such as when he had a chance to kill two players to steal their Riptide tridents and instead chose to help them in exchange for a trident.

Trivia

He is surprisingly good at 1.8 PvP, his first channel being assorted Bedwars clips.

He often overestimates his 1.9 skill level, as when he tried to claim to 
ManePear
 on two separate occasions that he was high tier and the same level as 
Mane
, only to be badly beaten by 
Mane
.

After being told to "lock in" by 
ManePear
, Wemmbu actually did. Not only killing three gold tier players and bringing back their chestplates to 
Mane
, but even holding his own against 
FlameFrags
 on the occasion when 
Flame
 was attacking Snow Civilization.

When he went invisible after faking his death, he chose netherite as his trim material because of 
MinuteTech's
 own trims. He decided not to wear netherite armor to not bring attention to himself but having netherite trims made the effort useless, as it stood out.

He hates when other people have maces and will try to kill them.

He is told by 
MinuteTech
 that his rivalry with 
FlameFrags
 is going to kill them both one day.

His Density-enchanted mace is named Gambit, while his Breach-enchanted mace is named Crucible. Only Gambit has the Wind Burst enchantment on it.

He had a netherite sword named Nightrider.

He holds the record for the longest video in the series, being "
I Mastered Minecraft Combat
" which is 3:59:59 long.

Ironically, he also holds the record for the shortest video in the series, being "
How I SURVIVED the Unstable SMP
" which is 24:21 long.

In April 2026 he is the 5th most subbed member after 
Cash,
 
AJTHEBOLD
, 
NotNico
, and 
Wifies
. He has the most subs for a protagonist followed by 
Parrot
.

Before starting Unstable, Wemmbu was known for using hacks and exploits, similar to Spoke. However, unlike Spoke, we don't see him using hacks or exploits in Unstable.

The crown on his head is actually not a crown but it is a tiara as in one of his old videos he says that he thinks crowns are overused.

Wemmbu's IGN was originally lowercase "wemmbu" when the series first started, but it was later changed to 'Wemmbu'.

Wemmbu got his name by playing a game called Bit Heroes and saw the character named 'Wemmbo'. He replaced the 'o' with a 'u'
