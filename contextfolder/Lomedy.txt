Personality

Lomedy is a supportive, attentive, encouraging, and caring person. He has a passion for farming and dedicates most of his time to maintaining and expanding his farm which he cares greatly for. He has an open-mind and understands why people fight even though he hates it. He motivates others to keep going, and does his best to maintain peace.

He cares greatly for his best friend, 
FlameFrags
, going out of his way every time Flame needed him, providing assistance and shelter whenever Flame needed it. He also has a strict moral compass, as shown when he made the difficult choice to end their friendship after hearing Flame had killed a thousand lawmen and seeing Flame's stubborn refusal to change.

Season 2

During Season 2, Lomedy was at his base when Flame suddenly arrived, threatened with death, Lomedy allowed Flame to stay, but faced many invasion and attack of the Toxic Civilization lead by VENOM. Even becoming a fighter, but not a very good one. 

Flame asked Lomedy to rebuild his base, when Lomedy arrived and showing double mace on both hand, they started to rebuild the wall and the house, but Jaden came back and stole Lomedy's mace using decorated pots and Lomedy hide. Lomedy wanted Flame to get it back in which Flame pursue Jaden for it.

Season 3

He helps Flame build a new base.

He is forced to serve as a part of the reformed Law with Flame.

Whilst FlameFrags was infiltrating The Mist, he had to choose a trusted teammate from outside the mist to be his partner in The Mist Civilization. FlameFrags chose Lomedy. While he initially wasn't in favor of being dragged into FlameFrags' mission of killing Mistrul, he ended up complying. After finishing their assignment in a trial chamber, they sneak out with Invisibility potions to go and look for Mistrul's base.

Whilst looking for the base, Flame and Lomedy ended up finding a wall of cobblestone, only to get jumped by hunters. They eventually run off to a cave, where the hunters retreated, fearing the person who lives there. Lomedy insists on leaving, however FlameFrags, saying that this was their only lead to Mistrul's base, ended up going in and exploring with Lomedy.

The two of them find a house, where they find Mistrul AFK. Flame and Mistrul begin fighting with nearly equal skill, while Lomedy stays in the sidelines uninvolved. They end the fight off, and Lomedy leaves the cave with FlameFrags with a happy ghast.

While flying back to The Mist civilization, Lomedy disrupts their transportation, telling Flame "You really don't think that one of these flights is eventually gonna --" however, before he could finish his sentence, an arrow came from the right and shot him off the ghast. 

Later, NotSkriley roamed around the Ice Spikes in the mist, And found Lomedy Alive. They went back to Flame's base.

Lomedy finds a team of old players called 
Cindercrest

Lomedy fights in the battle of merchant city and is one of the only
