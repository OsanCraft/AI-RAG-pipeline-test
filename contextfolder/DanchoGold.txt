History

He ordered the rescue of 
ParrotX2

Merchant city Vanguard

Parrot then turns to him and Merchant cities vanguard to join The Royal Army. Dancho refuses and attempts to kidnap him and Theo to force them to pay the diamonds they owed. It is unlikely what happened to him after Parrot took over Merchant City but he is probably imprisoned or running free and planning some sort of revenge for Parrot and Theo.
