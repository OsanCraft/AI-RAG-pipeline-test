The 
Mist Civilization
 is a Civilization located in 
The Mist
 and the members prevent anyone from leaving. They are the main antagonists of the video:"
I Became a Minecraft Assassin
". The goal of Mist Civilization is to prevent anyone from leaving The Mist.

History

FlameFrags goes to the civilization to assassinate 
Mistrul
, a "combat master" that has been eliminating a lot of its' members. FlameFrags completes wind charge trials there but gets ratted out by 
FluffalsSupreme
 and pops his totem. The civilization is very gloomy, which suggests its' gray and foggy look. Flame and Lomedy sneak out with some difficulty to get a fight out of Mistrul, which is ironic because the civilization is supposed to prevent people from leaving. The guards don't spot them on the way out.
