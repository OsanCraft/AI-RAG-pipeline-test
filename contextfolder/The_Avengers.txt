The Avengers
 was a group of players consisting of 
Wemmbu
's old enemies, who attempted to kill Wemmbu.

History

During a battle in the Spikelands within the 
Farther Lands
, saw The Avengers fought against both 
Wemmbu
 and 
Eggchan
. The battle almost ended with a victory for the Avengers but it took an unprecedented turn, as 
FlameFrags
 decided to betray The Avengers and fight alongside Wemmbu and Eggchan. The battle ended in a defeat for The Avengers, and Wemmbu's mace was subsequently returned to him by Flame, who claimed that he didnt need it.

The Avengers was a ragtag group of players assembled  by Jaden_Man. Frequent member conflict led to its complete collapse. In the final battle FlameFrags betrayed the alliance and fought the Avengers.  This led to the betrayal arc in which Jaden and FlameFrags fought.

The effects of the battle can still be seen especially from Jaden & Flame's rivalry which started in this battle. The rivalry still continues even though neither one of them regards Wemmbu as an enemy and Jaden being one of Wemmbu's closest allies.
