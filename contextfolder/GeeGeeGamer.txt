GeeGeeGamer
 is a supporting character in 
Unstable Universe
 Season Two.

Personality

He loves building. He has no mic and instead communicates with signs, with most of what he writes being about how much he loves to build. He is  described as an og player by Eggchan, due to his use of the programmer art texture pack.

History

GeeGeeGamer shows 
Wemmbu
 and 
Eggchan
 his place. The textures get replaced by classic Minecraft textures to show the feel of old Minecraft while GeeGeeGamer tours them. Wemmbu tells GeeGeeGamer to tell him where The Forge is or else he will die because he knows that they are just trying to distract them. GeeGeeGamer responds, telling him that only the fighters know where The Forge is and that he only builds.

Wemmbu and Eggchan follow them and they make it to 
Lakerek's
 room in the city. They make it to the top of the staircase and find an end portal. 
Serpentbound333
 tells Wemmbu that The Forge is on the other side of the portal and GeeGeeGamer aims a punch bow at Wemmbu during their conversation.

Eggchan notices GeeGeeGamer pointing the bow and as he shoots, Eggchan takes the arrow and gets sent into the end portal. 

Wemmbu realizes that the entire thing was a trap as 
Nitenly
 attempts to hit Wemmbu in and they have a fight. During their fight, Wemmbu gets hit with slow falling and he also doesn't have strength giving him a disadvantage. They eventually run out of slow falling making the fight easier for Wemmbu.

Wemmbu kills GeeGeeGamer.
