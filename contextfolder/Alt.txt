Origin

Alt is first seen when Spoke is shown JamatoP's base which came from an armour set with "Ctrl" (helmet), "Delete" (leggings), and "Escape" (boots). This armour set has all types of incompatible protection enchantments, which is normally impossible. Wemmbu destroyed the rest of the set, but Spoke kept Alt for months. 

Alt has pronounced symbolic significance: It can represent Spoke's pride, Spoke's facade, Spoke's personal heroism, Spoke's false friendship with 
JamatoP
, Spoke's manipulation, Spoke's crime, or Spoke's inner goal to use exploits to take over the server. 

Ironically, Alt was broken by Spoke's former best friend, 
Mapicc

Finding 1000 Players Who Went Missing
