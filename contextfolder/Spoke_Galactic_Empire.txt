 The 
Spoke Galactic Empire
 was created by 
Spoke
 for fun and a revenge spree. Even though he was seen to be absolutely terrible at being a leader, he still managed to amass a massive crowd (1017 players). This was done by getting a lot of fans to join via the Unstable Events Discord.

Eventually somethings happened between 
Spoke
 and the rest of the army to make all the members want to kill 
Spoke
 and 
Mapicc
. 
The Executive
 took power of the army before it collapsed and was destroyed by Spoke, Mapicc, and 
Leo
. It is theorised that Spoke's neglectfulness and general indifference to the state of his citizens led to a rebellion, as the civilisation was basically anarchy to begin with.

Some members were seen with armor trims, like a full Iron armor with gold trims (Raiser, Silence, Coast and Ward), a full diamond armor with gold silence trims on helmet, chestplate and leggings, and diamond snout trims on boots, a full diamond armor with redstone rib trims on chestplate, many full diamond armor with lapis snout trims on helmet, chestplate and boots, lapis vex trims on leggings and lapis rib trims on helmet. Some of these armors were stolen from other enpire members due to the continuous killing between each other.
