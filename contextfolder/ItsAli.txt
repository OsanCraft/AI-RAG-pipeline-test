ItsAli_
, or simply 
Ali
 is a supporting character featured in 
Unstable Universe
. He is an ally of 
SpokeIsHere
 in 
the NULL Arc
, and a major antagonist in the 
Purgatory Arc
.

Personality

Ali's most significant contribution to the Unstable SMP lore is founding a rebell group against the 
Null Hunters
. After the mysterious Group NULL infiltrates the surface, spreads chaos, and causes hundreds of players to vanish, Ali reacts strategically. He guides the remaining players deep into the subterranean world. There, he establishes a self-sustaining society that must survive completely cut off from surface resources and sunlight. In this role, he acts as a protective leader striving to save the community from total erasure. But he has a dark side visible when 
DevMCChamp
 dies in Purgatory. He relentlessly hunts down Spoke, wanting to end his suffering by ending the one he believes is responsible for it. He even sides with NULL in an attempt to get Spoke killed.

History

As SpokeIsHere, JumperWho, and Mapicc traveled through the red custom biome to discover why over a thousand players had vanished, they eventually arrived at an abandoned survivor camp. Nearby, they encountered ItsAli_ and his people. However, instead of receiving a friendly welcome, they were immediately overpowered and detained. Mapicc, in particular, aroused Ali's suspicion, as he had previously been captured by NULL. To Ali, it appeared that the three of them might potentially be working for NULL.

Consequently, Ali interrogated them first, seeking to uncover their true identities. During this process, it became clear that he had long survived in opposition to NULL and knew exactly how dangerous the organization was. He appeared cautious, tense, and like someone who had seen it all.

After some time, Spoke, Jumper, and Mapicc explained their situation. They revealed that they, too, were fighting against NULL and were attempting to rescue the missing players. Mapicc's story, above all, finally convinced Ali, as he himself had once been a prisoner of NULL and had only narrowly managed to escape.

For the first time, Ali placed his trust in them. In doing so, he effectively became an ally of the group. However, shortly thereafter, the situation escalated. Actual NULL members launched an attack on the camp. Chaos erupted, and the group was forced to decide whether to stand and fight or to flee. Ali quickly realized that Spoke and the others likely represented the only chance to uncover the truth about NULL and perhaps to save other players.

He therefore made a decision while Spoke, Jumper, and Mapicc were to make their escape, Ali and his group would stay behind to hold off NULL and buy them time. In doing so, he sacrificed his own freedom so that the others could get away.

After the protagonists had fled, ItsAli_ and his people were overpowered and captured by NULL. They subsequently vanished.

NULL brought him and his people to their prison 
Purgatory
.

Some time in Purgatory he hears of Dev's execution. He then snaps and decides to channel his anger towards who he believes is as responsible as NULL for killing his friend: Spoke. He finds Spoke whilst he is fighting Mapicc and decides to kill him. Mapicc helps Spoke, and together they overpower him.

He later returns with three more inmates. He traps Spoke, who even after trying to talk some sense into him is unable to change the situation. Spoke uses his horn to call the guards. Ali runs.

Ali then sells out the black market in hopes of NULL killing Spoke. He works with his enemies to achieve that.
