History

Roshambogames is hired to be a guard of one of 
The Wonders
.

Roshambogames tells 
ParrotX2
 that the wonder he was guarding got destroyed. He’s also seen talking to 
Wemmbu
 about how he has to be more subtle when distracting Parrot. He also talks about "illegal items", likely referencing the glitched items from 
How to Destroy the Universe
.

Roshambo is convinced by Wemmbu to not let Parrot use his orbital cannon. Roshambo later agrees to team with Parrot to build a new orbital cannon to stop Spoke’s orbital cannon from destroying the Frosted Palace Wonder.

Roshambo is seen guarding a vault. He tries to stop 
SpokeIsHere
 from escaping the vault, but fails.

Roshambo traps Spoke in the end, but Spoke manages to get away by flying to the outer end islands. Spoke follows Roshambo to his end base.

Wemmbu and 
eggchan
 set up a job where people can hire them to blow up things. Roshambo hires Wemmbu and eggchan to blow up Spoke’s end base. They are successful in doing so.

Wemmbu sees that 
FlameFrags
 and 
Merffinn
 are trying to invade 
Roshambogames Civilization
 (unnamed in video). FlameFrags almost kills Roshambo, but Wemmbu stops him. Wemmbu challenges FlameFrags to a 1v1. He manages to get everyone in the Roshambo empire to evacuate, and he escapes using a stasis chamber. He hasn't appeared since.
