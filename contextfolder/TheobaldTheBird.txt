Personality

Theo is a highly skilled and charismatic player, being a PVP minecart focused fighter. He is Parrot’s best friend and loyal bodyguard, with Theo being highly protective of the other and often putting his friend before himself, even in life-or-death scenarios. Theo is often the victim of friendly robbery and teases from allies which he always indignant of. He is shown to be highly airheaded outside of battles, with him having issues with writing, reading and numbers. This bird-brained personality leads act as a comic relief and he tends to lean into this trait of his to lighten dark situations. However, he is prone to aggressive outbursts and has an incredibly short temper. Additionally, he regularly uses intimidation and violence against others to get what he wants.

Theo shares Parrot's vision of wanting the server to be a safe environment for newcomers of the server without the threats of being banned or facing corruption. Theo has his ideals about helping the server, he is also sensible and focuses on saving the live of the many rather than the lives of the few. He is an adamant individual, sticking to his guns despite external influences and people of his past, such as 
ShoeBilly_
. Rather than dwelling on his past, he focuses entirely on the future of the Unstable server.

Season 3

The Kingdoms Arc

Theobald serves as the bodyguard of 
The King
 of the Unstable SMP, often threatening to cart people who attack the King. 

Theo goes on missions with Parrot, and on one of them, they 
exposed
 a corrupt police force led by Bossstream. Theo threatens to blow all of them up, again, if they attacked Parrot. 

Theo and Parrot founded the civilization of Skymore to spread out the population density on the server since Parrot opened up spawn to "new players" on the server. Unfortunately, when they came back to check on their progress, all of the civilians were nowhere to be found. Once they found out, the one who murdered the one thousand player civilization was Theo's 
trainer
, ShoeBilly. Theo is insanely angry at why his former trainer decided to murder them. His trainer said that he was hired by a faction called Cindercrest to wipe out all the new players on the server, including Parrot.

Theo and Parrot decide to combat Cindercrest's forces all in netherite with highly skilled players including FlameFrags, they would have to create an army with tens of thousands of players. Theo scouts and sees FlameFrags and ShoeBilly on their 
blimp
.

Once the civil war starts between Cindercrest and Parrot's Kingdom, Theo wipes out many Cindercrest players with his stereotypical carting style. After the Civil War, Theo is banished by the new king of the server, Saparata.
