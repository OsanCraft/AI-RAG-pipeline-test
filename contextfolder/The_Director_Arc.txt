Story

ItzRealMe
 attacks 
ParrotX2
, but the 
Bounty Assassin Team
 saves him. They plan to trap and kill him, but Itz reveals he has a mace and uses it to kill half the attacking players. Parrot and 
Leow0ok
, head of BAT, go to spy on Itz, but get caught and nearly killed. Itz lures the rest of BAT away from their HQ and nearly kills Parrot, but Leo saves him. Itz visits Parrot and reveals the Director sent him, but that he’s done now.

MrCube6
 attempts to kill Parrot, presumably by the Director's orders. They play mind games, each trying to outsmart the other. 
Derapchu
 continually causes problems, culminating in all of BAT's wealth being stolen. Parrot and Derapchu go out to recover it, and do so successfully, fighting off bandits along the way. Cube and Parrot meet for one final contest, and after Parrot outsmarts Cube, Derapchu arrives and takes him into custody. He gives Parrot a message from the Director, greeting Parrot.

BAT attempts to kill Parrot, but Jumper helps him escape, and later explains that BAT thinks he’s the Director. She sneaks him into BAT, but he gets caught and barely escapes. Later, she betrays him to BAT, but items she gave him helps him escape, and she later explains she had to regain BAT’s trust. One of her spies overhears them, forcing her to kill him. The Director arranges for a meeting with Parrot, which Jumper secretly brings BAT too. After the Director leaves, BAT finds the spy’s grave, and Jumper is kicked off the team.

BAT get attacked by 
TruOriginal
, a hacker. They escape, but he destroys BAT HQ. They flee and set up an underground base, then get materials to build a mirror receiver, planning to link it to their orbital strike cannon to kill Tru. That fails, however, and Parrot is nearly killed, but Nufuli helps him escape. Leo and Derapchu are captured, and Tru tells them to come to BAT HQ. They do so, and this time manage to successfully kill Tru with the cannon, and save Leo and Derapchu. Nufuli decides to leave the team, but gets killed by the Director.

BAT follow the Director and get trapped with netherite zombies, but kill them and claim the netherite. They bribe a shop owner for info, then follow who they believe to be the Director, but it's actually MrCube. The Director gives Parrot two weeks to find his identity, then tries to kill him, but he is saved by Jumper. Jumper brings them to her base, where they stay for a while, and after Parrot believes one of BAT betrayed, Leo tries to kill him, blaming him for all the bad things that have happened. He escapes and runs, but Jumper and Derapchu follow him, then get trapped by the Director, who will kill them if Parrot doesn't guess who he is. Parrot gets Leo to save them while he distracts the Director, only for the Director to reveal himself as 
Wifies
, having faked his death and done all this to test how Parrot reacts to things, in order to keep him safe forever. Parrot follows the Director.

Wifies brings Parrot to 
Paragon
, an inescapable prison for them to build a new life. Parrot tries to kill Wifies, then tries to escape, but a stasis chamber and invisible guards foil his respective attempts. He plays nice, then tries to kill Wifies when he's supposedly AFK, but Wifies planned that. They go back and forth for a bit, Parrot continually failing to outsmart Wifies, before he finally tries one too many times. Wifies makes him drop all his items and brings him to a room with two pressure plates, where if either of them step off, they die. Parrot reveals he snuck his spyglass in, and drops it, then leaves. Wifies steps off his pressure plate and grabs the spyglass, dying for real this time. Parrot escapes Paragon.
