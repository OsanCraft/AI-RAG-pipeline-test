History

This faction consisted of an army of players who lived underground in a deep dark biome. After allying with Arachn1d, they take 
Eggchan
 hostage, possibly on Arachn1d's orders. They are in the midst of interrogating him, including stealing his notes, when 
Wemmbu
 attempts to release him. In the massive battle that follows, 
Yungyx
 leads around a thousand Skulk Kingdom players to their doom against Wemmbu.  After Wemmbu departs, the players who were grilling Eggchan to get Information about 
The Abandoned Civilization
 are killed by the 
invisible player
 and Eggchan's notes stolen by the player.
