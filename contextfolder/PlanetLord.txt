History

Season 1

PlanetLord is guarding a skybase. Spoke comes to the skybase and destroys the duped items using an orbital cannon, despite PlanetLord trying to stop him.

PlanetLord is seen at an end base.

Spoke and Mapicc find a stash of what he thinks are duped items. He finds the chests are empty. A player named PlanetLord comes in and they try to kill him as he places shulker boxes which appear to be duped because they have identical items in all of them. PlanetLord is able to escape.

Spoke and Mapicc see 
Roshambogames
 and PlanetLord at a flower farm. 
The Mafia
 destroys the entire farm with TNT. 

Spoke and Mapicc find PlanetLord. The Mafia comes over and tries to kill them. They trap the Mafia players using a sand trap, though none of them die. They are able to escape.

Mapicc is captured by the Mafia. PlanetLord introduces 
Baconnwaffles0
 and 
TheRealSquiddo
 to 
MinuteTech
, a friend of Spoke. A Mafia member named 
BobDaGreenFrog
 tries to kill them, and baconnwaffles0 runs away. MinuteTech kills the Mafia member. The Mafia starts trying to kill them again. They mysteriously give MinuteTech a book, to which MinuteTech replies "no". The Mafia continues attacking them. Spoke is able to escape, though he almost dies.

TheRealSquiddo shows the team some coordinates that she was given that could be the coordinates to a Mafia outpost. She also states that everybody in the master builders group has disappeared. She says she was commissioned to do a build by an anonymous player.

The team goes over to an outpost and find a large amount of invisible players. MinuteTech messages the team to run away after being handed a book.

PlanetLord and Spoke discover that 
JamatoP
’s base is destroyed. Spoke and PlanetLord try to dupe items in the base, but realize that it doesn’t work anymore. 
Ashswagg
, the leader of the Mafia, comes in and reveals that all the glitches and exploits were patched, and that Jamato told him so. Ashswagg reveals that MinuteTech was working for him, and orders him to kill Spoke and PlanetLord. Spoke and PlanetLord escape.

Spoke and PlanetLord go under one of the wonders where they put weaponless mobs. They find that the mobs are named coordinates. They find a replica of Jamato’s base made out of wood, and all of the glitched features have been removed. He finds a book stating that if he is reading the book, he is captured or dead. It states he has exiled himself, that he has been hunted by the Mafia, that he was captured in an outpost, and that there is an item that can fix everything. They find a compass that is named “The Mafia”.

Following the compass, they find a Mafia outpost. They see Mafia members in a room. They go above the players and drop dripstone on them, killing one of the players and using the totem of undying of another player named 
Leow0ok
. Leow0ok agrees to team with them, and reveals he is only in the Mafia hire teammates to help him kill 
ClownPierce
. The outpost starts exploding. They follow a member of the Mafia to 
Mafia City
, where there are different tiers of Mafia members. They find Mapicc in a prison and break him out.

Spoke and the team are tasked to kill 
ParrotX2
 but fails. Ashswagg kills one of the members who was helping them. They run away. They are then tasked with guarding a vault. 
ManePear
 and 
Wemmbu
 start trying to break into the vault.

The team is now tasked to get diamonds. Ashswagg promotes them to gold tier.

The team is tasked with killing PlanetLord, but they do not kill him. Ashswagg talks to them. He orders ClownPierce to kill the Mafia members until whoever let PlanetLord go steps up. Spoke steps up and says he did it. They are dismissed.

Ashswagg tells Spoke that to become a diamond player, he will have to fight a diamond player that ashswagg assigns to him.

Spoke and the team come up with a plan to kill ashswagg. He starts fighting a diamond player to become diamond. Spoke’s totem of undying is used during the fight, revealing to ashswagg that he is Spoke. They try to kill ashswagg but he escapes by using a stasis chamber. The diamond player reveals he’s MinuteTech.
