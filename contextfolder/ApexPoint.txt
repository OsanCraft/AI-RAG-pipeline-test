Personality

ApexPoint is loud, obnoxious and very full of himself, often proclaiming his name to players (like Spoke) several times and asking if they know who he is. His overconfidence would ultimately be his downfall as he would meet his end at the hands of one of Spoke's traps. ApexPoint fashioned himself as "The Ruler of the Amplified" and believed that everything about him was amplified.

History

After applying for BAT's membership, 
Spoke
 was tasked to hunt down a griefer known as ApexPoint. He eventually discovers ApexPoint being AFK in his obsidian base. ApexPoint is ambushed and loses his Netherite Sword, retreating and losing Spoke in the process. He destroyed a base belonged to a player named TREELEAF2021, and fights Spoke again as a revenge for losing his sword. ApexPoint is continously drained and vanishes from Spoke's sight, somehow convincing 
Leow0ok
 that he was dead.

ApexPoint returns with his new teammate named ZeroCycleDemon3D. Him and ZeroCycleDemon3D attempted to rob JackLive_XD, but later invites him to his own team. He brings the new player into his base and showed him a storage room. Leo appears in front of his base pretending to be a salesman, but him and ZeroCycleDemon3D asks Leo to leave repeatedly. Mapicc accidentally goes uninvisible and Apex alongside his teammates hunts him down, but later discovers Spoke and attacks him aswell. He loses Spoke and Mapicc.

Him and his team goes to 
TheRealSquiddo
's base to ask the whereabouts of Spoke and Mapicc. He is constantly annoyed by the invisible duo and Squiddo's base is burned in the process. They leave to raid 
Jepexx
's base, which they later discover. The invisible Spoke and Mapicc helped Jepexx escape by constantly disrupting ApexPoint's team, which was a success. ApexPoint and his team hunt down the duo relentlessly, while also destroying their secret base, and later discovers and abuses the same glitch Spoke and Mapicc used to become invisible.

ApexPoint and his team fails to abuse the glitch as they take forms of damage. They eventually negotiates for a truce, but Spoke gives ApexPoint an item while breaking the cobblestone block that leads to a dripstone trap, killing ApexPoint.
