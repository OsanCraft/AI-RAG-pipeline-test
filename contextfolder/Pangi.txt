Pangi

Unstable Universe
. Pangi was a member of 
Wemmbu's Civilization
, a member of 
Skyblock Civilization
, and a member of the 
Zam Empire
. 

Personality

Pangi is a generally positive, excitable, and energetic player who is very loyal to his friends and allies.  

Although he has a small role in the overall story, Pangi is a loyal ally and tries his best to stay out of conflict and be helpful.
