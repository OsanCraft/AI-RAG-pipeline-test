Personality

He is seen to be gullible and stubborn; falling for Flame's lie about duplicating the mace and refusing to give it back to Wemmbu respectively. 

History

Conexion was a competitor in a PVP tournament.

Conexion steals one of 
Wemmbu's
 maces called "Crucible" using a pot. Wemmbu chases him into the Farlands to get it back, where Conexion almost falls into the void. Wemmbu ends up saving him and asks for the mace back, but Sharpness secretly hides it in his ender chest instead.

Later, Sharpness and 
The Avengers
 hunt Wemmbu down. Sharpness gives Wemmbu's mace to 
FlameFrags
, thinking that he knew a duplication glitch. Flame eventually fools him before the fight with Wemmbu, The Avengers lost the battle after Flame betrayed them, and Conexion flees. After that Flame returns Wemmbu his mace back.
