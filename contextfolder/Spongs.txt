Personality

Spongs is chaotic, unserious, and frivolous. He tends to create problems and will often make questionable plans that don't achieve anything. He is quite rash and impulsive; throwing away items without any heed for their value. He has a level of obliviousness towards how others feel; not listening to Flame when he tells him he isn't helping. He does not seem to understand why others get mad with him. He can be somewhat cocky; overestimating his ability and understanding of game. He generally attempts to be helpful, but his lack of social awareness means he causes more problems than he ever solves. This same lack of social awareness makes him bubbly and friendly person.
