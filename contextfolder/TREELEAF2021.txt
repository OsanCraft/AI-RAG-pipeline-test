TREELEAF2021
 was a minor character in 
Unstable Universe
 
Season 2
 who lived in the amplified. He was generally perceived as annoying and childish by others but was willing to repay kindness with kindness; giving Spoke a trident and delivering the compass for him.  

History

His house was saved by 
Spoke
 from 
ApexPoint
 as a mission from 
Leow0ok
. He later managed to track down Spoke at the BAT headquarters and gave him a channeling trident named "Mjolnir" as a gift for saving his house. Spoke then gave him the mission of delivering a loadstone compass to 
Wemmbu
 (to get Wemmbu and 
Flamefrags
 in the same place for a fight) which resulted in TREELEAF's death as Wemmbu found him annoying.    

He wears golden armour, despite posessing a trident, which suggests he had the ability to acquire superior gear but instead chose to stick with what he had.
