The Invisible Mafia's Stasis Chambers
, built inside of a massive castle located inside an artificial mountain, were the locations where the pearls of all mafia members were stored. It was built by 
Quackenstein123
 and his daughter 
iMajesticRose
. 

History

Quackenstein gave 
SpokeIsHere
 and 
Mapicc
 a book with coordinates leading to a chest in the sky with directions through the underground tunnels where pearls were transported, then sent to the chambers with a pearl cannon. Mapicc modified the cannon to shoot them slightly shorter, allowing them to approach from afar. After scouting it out, Spoke and Mapicc returned to destroy the chambers by dropping the pearls into the void. Spoke initially went in without a shovel, forcing him to place a shulker of iron and craft one. After running out of fireworks, his stasis was pulled by Mapicc and Mapicc gave him an efficiency Diamond Shovel, allowing Spoke to break the chambers faster. 

While Spoke was still breaking chambers, two 
diamond players
 attacked, forcing him to use more fireworks to evade. The diamond players covered up all of the possible exits with obsidian, trapping the three below the void. However, Mapicc broke the diamond players' stasis mechanisms, preventing them from teleporting out. Spoke was able to use chorus fruit to teleport above the bedrock, while both diamond players eventually ran out of fireworks and died in the void.

After the stasis chambers were destroyed, the castle was abandoned, and hasn't been seen plotwise since. This castle is not to be confused with 
Mafia City.
