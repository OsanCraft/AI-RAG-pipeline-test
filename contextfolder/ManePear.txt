Personality

ManePear is portrayed as a cynical but highly skilled player. Mane prefers to play Minecraft for fun instead of sweat. He often encourages FlameFrags to stop caring about how others perceive him and to play for the "moola" (money) and the thrill of it. Despite his history of betraying people out of boredom, he maintains a brotherly bond with FlameFrags, stating, "Brothers are always a friend".

History

Season 1

Originally, he served as a high level guard for 
PrinceZam
 alongside 
Merfinn
, 
FlameFrags
, 
Pangi
 and a hand full of other good PvPers. However, after Zam betrayed 
Wemmbu
, ManePear ended up betraying Zam "For Fun" and helping Wemmbu kidnap 
Peentar
, 
Yungwillx
, and 
Sorhin
. ManePear helps in the destruction of the 
Zam Empire
. 

During the beginning of Mafia Arc. 
Spoke
 and 
Mapicc
 attempted to recruit Mane to their team to defeat 
The Invisible Mafia
. Mane, thinking they were trolling started killing them. Spoke and Mapicc ran. 

Later in Mafia Arc, ManePear helps train Wemmbu in mace PvP. He gave Wemmbu advice to name his mace. After some time, ManePear discovers that The Diamond Trim Mafia member that he fought in 
Ice Mountain Civilization
 was 
FlameFrags
, he comes to Wemmbu and asks for his mace "Gambit". Wemmbu refuses and flees. Mane eventually leaves, placing a sign saying "Lost Cause". 

Season 2

There was a rumours that ManePear was hiding in the deep, remote caves. During his exile, ManePear became the first player to fully master the Spear. He developed a combat style that prioritizes speed over defense. He famously does not use totems or shields, choosing instead to rely on "Movement Mastery" to avoid hits and deal massive burst damage.

FlameFrags tracked ManePear down to learn the Spear. ManePear agreed to train him only if FlameFrags helped him take down a group of "stacked" players who were preventing ManePear from "eating good" (looting).

Later on when FlameFrags was captured by the faction known as 
The Law
, ManePear abandoned his "lone wolf" persona to stage a rescue. He successfully broke FlameFrags out of a high-security cell by destroying his curse of binding armor, and looting LAW's vault as a payment for his mentoring to Flame.
