The 
Blimp
 (known as Airlander 10) is a redstone airship created and used by the 
Cindercrest
 army. This airship was designed into Minecraft by 
@krodofox
.

History

The first blimp made its first appearance in 
I Created the World's Largest Minecraft Army
. This blimp was used to do a aerial attack on 
Fort Feather
. Then Cindercrest trying to do the same at 
Merchant City
 but was stopped by 
ParrotX2
's arrow cannon. Its status is unknown.

5 airships were created and used by Cindercrest and the 
Kingdom Of The Caves
 to attack 
Steampunk City
. It transported all the armies and resources of the coalitions. Parrot raided those airships and destroyed them.
