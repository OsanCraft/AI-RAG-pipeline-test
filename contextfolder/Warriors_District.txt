The 
Warriors District
  is one of the three districts in the 
Far Lands
 civilization. Its purpose is to protect those who are part of the civilization.

History

The Warriors District fails to protect the Farmers District when they needed help, so they stop giving the Warriors resources.

One of the mission leader's dies to a diamond tier Mafia player.

Parrot and 
TheobaldTheBird
 have a competition for who becomes the next mission leader. TheobaldTheBird wins using TNT minecarts.

The warriors and the farmers play capture the flag. The farmers win the game. 

Parrot agrees to fight 
DumbIsDumb
 to become a council member. By flooding the arena with water and using tridents, Parrot is able to win.

The civilization discovers that there are 
Mafia
 players near the farlands. They find a gold tier player in the nether who is able to escape. They intercept the gold tier player when he is giving his message to 
ashswagg
. They pretend to be diamond tier players, and then kill him with a fall trap.

Purpled
 tries to have the council members kill Parrot, but he escapes. Parrot becomes the new leader because Purpled is outvoted by the council members. Parrot unites the warriors and the farmers, and tells everybody to throw their diamond trimmed armour into a cactus and replace it with normal netherite armour.
