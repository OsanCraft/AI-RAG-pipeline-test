History

Season 1

Spoke accidentally lost George after doing an MLG inside of the Pyramid of Anubis, and George noclipped into a wall and died. He ends up getting a new one.

Spoke holds George in his offhand in 
The Invisible Mafia
 to differentiate him from the other Mafia members. In the Mafia, he was code-named "Fish Bucket".

Season 2

Spoke accidentally killed George with a riptide trident. He later buried it and got a copy from his shulker box full of George Junior fish buckets.

Spoke gave a copy of George Junior to 
Leow0ok
 as collateral to get a full set of Netherite, and then got a copy of George Junior from his shulker box. The previous George Junior was returned to him later.

Season 3

In the episode "
Finding 1000 Players Who Went Missing
", 
JamatoP
 corners a helpless Spoke in the Farlands maze. JamatoP makes Spoke drop all the items in his inventory and Ender Chest. JamatoP then burns everything using his flint and steel. The shulker box of George Juniors from Spoke's Ender Chest was also presumably burnt, effectively destroying the identity of George Junior.
