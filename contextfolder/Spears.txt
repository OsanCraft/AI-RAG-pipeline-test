Netherite Spear
 
 

The 
spear
 is a weapon that deals more damage the faster you move relative to your enemies. When they were first introduced to the 
Unstable Universe
, they were extremely over-powered due to the fact that players were using modified spears that would deal significantly higher damage than the normal version. However, after the events of 
I Got Trained By a Minecraft Spear Master
, modified spears ceased to exist.

They are frequently used by players to travel and paired alongside the 
mace
 for spear-mace combat, a form of combat that utilizes the spear's 
Lunge
 enchantment to move across distances swiftly.
